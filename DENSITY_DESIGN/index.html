    Instruction-density design (cycle 204, v2 — adjudicated)¶

    Wave-9 rank 3. Question: when one prompt carries many simultaneous instructions, at which density does adherence start dropping, and how does satisfaction vary by serial position — measured per template family on a local checkpoint, with every constraint decidable from a digest commitment and every verdict re-derived by the diff validator. v1 was rejected by the cycle-204 adversarial round (two FATAL findings: no satisfiability guarantee; an uncalibrated falsification statistic); this v2 incorporates the adjudication in agents_out/cycle204/coordinator_adjudication.md.

    Utility¶

    A practitioner stacking simultaneous instructions into one prompt learns, per template family on their own checkpoint, how adherence falls as simultaneous-instruction density rises from solo to sixteen, and how satisfaction at full density varies across early, middle, and late serial positions — before shipping a template under which the later instructions silently stop being followed. Position claims are scoped to what the denominators support (see Readouts): per-position rates at full density carry n = 20 per cell, so the headline position readout is band-pooled.

    What this probe is not¶

    • Not IFScale (Jaroslawicz, Whiting, Shah & Maamari, arXiv:2507.11538 — verified against the abstract in this cycle): 500 keyword-inclusion instructions on a long business report, 20 frontier API models, best 68% at max density, bias towards earlier instructions. That is the phenomenon at API scale on long free-form output; it does not vary the chat template, does not run under exact-token discipline, and its output imports a verbosity confound this design removes with short answers. The uniqueness here is the combination: simultaneous-instruction density × serial position × template family, on a local checkpoint, with digest-decidable constraints and a recomputing diff validator. The claim is the combination, never first.
    • Not hierarchy (two conflicting privileged instructions), not distraction (irrelevant text carrying no demands), not multiturn (instructions spread over turns), not structured (one schema, adherence only). None varies the count of simultaneous compatible instructions as the independent variable.
    • Not a judge-based instrument: no LLM scores anything.

    The digest-decidability constraint (slate adjudication)¶

    The diff validator derives satisfaction from stored digest data alone — never from stored satisfaction booleans. The vocabulary is capped to what a word-level commitment can decide, and the cap is printed here: case, sentence, and paragraph structure constraints (all-lowercase, no-commas, N sentences, bullet form) are NOT decidable from the commitment and are excluded from v1 by name. One deliberate measurement choice, stated where a reader can see it: word identity is exact after normalization — include_word("seven") scores an answer of "7" as failure; there is no digit↔word mapping.

    Construction¶

    CASE_COUNT = 20 cases, generated from the run seed by a frozen generator (density.py); the diff validator carries an independent mirror of the generator, the rendering, and the checking rule.

    Each case has:

    • A base task from a frozen pool of short-answer task templates over synthetic content. The base instruction asks for a short answer and carries NO numeric length demand (length demands live only in word_count_at_most, where they are counted). Each case carries expected_word_digest: the SHA-256 of the task value's normalized word (multi-word values: the ordered hash list's digest), so task performance is measurable from the commitment (task-value presence) without a whole-answer digest — a compliant answer at high density necessarily differs from the canonical task answer as a string.
    • A frozen ordered list of 16 constraints, positions 1..16. The K-density prompt for K in {2, 4, 8, 16} is the base task plus the first K constraints; K = 1 is the solo arm of constraint 1 (see Arms). A constraint's serial position is identical at every density that includes it.

    Constraint vocabulary (frozen) and kind allocation¶

    Kinds (each a pure function of the word commitment):

    1. include_word(W) — the answer's word multiset contains W.
    2. start_word(W) — the answer's first normalized word is W.
    3. end_word(W) — the answer's last normalized word is W.
    4. word_count_at_most(N) — the answer's word count <= N.

    Kind allocation per case is FIXED: 4 include, 4 start, 4 end, 4 count, in a kind order that rotates across cases by case index (case c starts the repeating kind cycle at offset c mod 4), so each position carries each kind exactly 5 times across the 20 cases. Count parameters come from the frozen set {8, 12, 16, 24}.

    Satisfiability invariant (generator-enforced, mirrored)¶

    Per case: all start_word constraints share ONE target; all end_word constraints share ONE target; the distinct word targets across groups — 4 include targets, the start target, the end target, the expected task word — are pairwise distinct AFTER normalization (D <= 7 distinct words); and min(N over the case's count constraints) >= D, which the frozen N set guarantees (8 > 7). A satisfying answer always exists (start target, include targets, task word, end target in <= 7 words). The invariant is proven by test over the pools and seed sweeps, including a pinned perfect-model test: a constructed perfect follower scores discordance f = 0 and joint satisfaction 20/20. Stated limit: at k16 the start and end demands repeat (same target four times), so for positional kinds the density treatment is partly a repetition treatment; per-kind readouts keep this visible.

    Word commitment and normalization (frozen rule)¶

    For each generated answer the stage records ONE commitment object: word_hashes, the ordered list of SHA-256 hashes of the answer's normalized words (multiplicity preserved), and word_list_digest, SHA-256 of the list's canonical encoding (compact JSON, frozen in the RULE_TEXT). A word is a maximal run of alphanumeric characters, case-folded; every other character is a separator. Derived from the list, everywhere (stage aggregation and validator alike): the word multiset (count the list), word_count (the list's length), the first word (list head) and last word (list tail), or None when the list is empty. The first word is the first NORMALIZED word: leading tokens that normalize to nothing are skipped by construction.

    Satisfaction of every constraint is DERIVED from the commitment; no satisfaction boolean is stored (the rotation F4 pattern — the primitive determines the verdicts for all four kinds). Records also carry answer_digest (SHA-256 of the exact boundary-cut answer text, the standing pattern).

    Stated limits. (1) Fidelity residue: the commitment's internal consistency is validator-checked (digest over the list), but its fidelity to the generated answer is stage-attested — no digest of free text admits membership recompute against the text itself, and the verbatim-text alternative (the numform cycle-190 exception) was ruled out for this probe by the slate adjudication. (2) Exposure: the ordered hash list discloses the answer's word SEQUENCE to a dictionary pass over the (public) frozen pools — a strictly greater degree of disclosure than a whole-answer digest, of the same class; accepted because the determination property (F4) requires the ordered primitive, and the answers are short synthetic-task outputs.

    Rendering (frozen)¶

    Each kind's instruction template, the constraint-list joiner and numbering, the base task templates, and the neutral statements are frozen text in density.py, carried by digest in the protocol (the needle SIZING_RULE_TEXT pattern). The payload carries, per (case, arm), the user-turn content digest; the validator's mirror re-renders the content and requires digest equality — the treatment is derived, not attested. Records carry input_tokens under the rotation arm_tokens discipline.

    Draw rules (frozen; the mirror reproduces them exactly)¶

    Slot order per case: task template, task parameters, the sham arm's neutral statements, then per position 1..16 the constraint kind (fixed by the allocation rotation) and its parameter. Collision rule, with "occurs" defined as normalized-word equality: word targets must be distinct across groups (above) and must not occur in the case's base task text; constraint 1's target additionally must not occur in the case's neutral statements (the only constraint that shares an arm with them). Redraw scope: the offending parameter only, at its own slot counter; redrawn parameters are re-checked against the same frozen texts. Termination: pools are sized so the redraw always has a fresh candidate (pool sizes frozen in density.py and stated in the protocol; the generator test sweeps seeds to prove termination). Pool screen (pinned property, proven by test): all pool words are pairwise distinct after normalization, and no pool word equals a number word after normalization.

    Screening (rotation-shaped)¶

    Stage level: before generation, all frozen texts (task templates, kind renderings, neutral pool, word pools) are audited with audit_special_token_literals against every candidate, and boundary-text containment is checked; failure fails the stage closed with density_screen_failed. Per case: a case whose rendered texts contain a candidate's decoded boundary text is screened as a unit, with its own counter. Records carry a per-record detail in {"", arm_evaluation_failed, arm_context_exceeded}; records with a detail, and boundary-screened cases' records, are present in the record list (completeness below) with word_hashes = [] and word_count = 0.

    Live population: records with detail "" whose case is not boundary-screened for that candidate. There is NO word-cap screen: generation is bounded by the frozen max_new_tokens (64), so every live record's commitment is complete and every kind's verdict is decidable for it in both directions. Zero-word live records (empty answer: first emitted token is the boundary, or the answer normalizes to no words) carry their own counter and are excluded from word_count_at_most checked denominators (their count verdicts are vacuous); they still fail word-target kinds, decidably.

    Screen accounting per candidate, counters disjoint by the stated order (boundary screen first, then detail, then zero-word within live): n_generated, n_boundary_screened, n_detail_screened, n_zero_word, n_live — all printed.

    Arms¶

    Per case and candidate, 21 generations:

    • 16 solo arms solo_1..solo_16: base task + only constraint j — the per-constraint paired baselines. solo_1 IS the K=1 density arm (the v1 design generated it twice under two names; merged).
    • 4 density arms k2, k4, k8, k16: base task + the first K constraints.
    • 1 sham arm sham: base task + constraint 1 + neutral statements selected by the frozen rule below, separating "many instructions" from "much text".

    Sham construction: block length is the user-turn word count of the appended block. Neutrals are appended in frozen slot order, wrapping to the start of the drawn list, until the sham block's word count first reaches or exceeds the case's k16 block word count; both block lengths are recorded per record through the content commitment, and the selection is a deterministic function the mirror recomputes. (Build-time amendment, measured: without wrapping the sham stayed shorter than k16 in 3,992 of 4,000 generated cases — the length match the control exists for almost never held. Wrapping adds no pool words: the neutrals are pinned pool-free, so repetition cannot smuggle a target in.) The sham readout is computed over cases live in all three arms (solo_1, sham, k16), denominators printed.

    Records¶

    Exactly 21 × CASE_COUNT records per candidate, in canonical (case, arm) order; the validator rejects any other count, order, or arm set, and rejects duplicate candidate names (the run-level guard's clause, stated as rotation's validator states it). Each record carries: candidate, case index, arm id, detail, answer_digest, word_hashes, word_list_digest, word_count, input_tokens, the content digest of its arm, and the backend fingerprint via the shared _backend_fingerprint guard (cycle-202 F2). The protocol object carries: the frozen rule texts by digest (generation, rendering, normalization+checking), pool sizes, densities, CASE_COUNT, the paired floor and discordant-case floor, alpha, the word cap implied by max_new_tokens, and the record/payload caps. Caps: MAX_DENSITY_RECORDS = 10,080 (21 × 20 × 24); MAX_DENSITY_PAYLOAD_BYTES = 52 MiB (sizing: a record's ordered list is at most 64 entries × 68 B ≈ 4.4 KB plus ≈ 0.4 KB of fields ≈ 4.8 KB; 10,080 × 4.8 KB ≈ 48.4 MiB — the derivation, printed per the house pattern). Stage detail vocabulary: {cancelled, density_record_budget_exceeded, density_screen_failed}.

    Readouts¶

    Per candidate, over live records; every rate is None iff its denominator is 0; every readout prints its denominators.

    • Satisfaction by density: for each K in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16}, the pooled satisfaction over that arm's constraints (K = 1 reads solo_1), printed BESIDE the same constraints' pooled solo rate (the composition control). The raw curve is labelled not composition-controlled.
    • Task-value presence by arm (the F6 readout): share of live records whose commitment contains the expected task word, per arm, beside the constraint readouts.
    • Position at full density: per-position satisfaction within k16 (16 rates, n = 20 denominators, descriptive), and the band-pooled headline: positions 1–5, 6–11, 12–16 pooled rates with denominators.
    • Per-kind satisfaction at solo and at k16, with denominators (the repetition-limit readout for positional kinds).
    • Joint satisfaction at k16: share of live k16 records with all 16 constraints satisfied, with denominator (the IFScale headline analogue — meaningful only because the satisfiability invariant guarantees it is attainable).
    • Sham readout: position-1 satisfaction in solo_1, sham, and k16 over the intersected live population, with the two block lengths printed.
    • Discordance: per (case, position) paired solo vs k16 satisfaction over eligible pairs (both records live; for count-kind pairs, neither record zero-word): f = solo-pass/k16-fail count, p = solo-fail/k16-pass count, printed pooled and per position (the per-position table is a density-and-position mixture — solo_j sits at effective position 1 — and is labelled as such).

    Falsification (pre-registered, scoped)¶

    Claim, scoped to the contrast the test supports: for a candidate, per-constraint satisfaction at full density (k16) equals its solo satisfaction — independence of the endpoint density contrast; intermediate densities are descriptive (the satisfaction curve), and a non-monotone intermediate dip does not falsify and is not claimed to. Decision, a total function per candidate over the eligible pairs: aggregate discordance per case, d_c = f_c − p_c. If the candidate has no live records, its block is unavailable. If the number of cases with d_c != 0 is below 6, the verdict is insufficient — the boolean is withheld, never defaulted to acquittal, and the counts are printed. Otherwise: falsified iff Σf > Σp AND the exact two-sided sign test over the discordant cases gives p <= 0.05; the p-value, f, p, and the discordant-case count are printed beside the boolean. The level is per-candidate; across 24 candidates at alpha = 0.05, roughly one false falsification is expected by chance (1 − 0.95^24 ≈ 0.71 for at least one), and the section text says so.

    Diff validator¶

    diff_density in diffreports.py with a full independent mirror: regenerate the cases, re-render every arm's content and require the payload's content digests to match, verify each record's commitment (list digest), derive every satisfaction verdict, task-presence verdict, headline, and the falsification decision (including the sign test), and require equality with the payload. Availability and insufficient states are mirrored per candidate. The diff-section blast radius is the standing three-element one (test_cycle67 oracle + flip_identical anchor + _identical_expected; test_cycle27 oracle + stub), verified by executed grep at build time.

    Budget¶

    Generations: 21 arms × 20 cases = 420 per candidate; 10,080 at the 24-candidate legal max. Solo arms are 16 of 21 arms; they earn their cost because the decision (fixed) and the composition control both consume them — the cycle-204 F17 sequencing condition. Construction is one render per arm. Records and payload caps as above.

    Open points — dispositions¶

    1. Solo-arm cost: PAID (16 solos kept; k1 merged into solo_1). The entry-density alternative covers positions 1–8 only and changes the estimand per position; rejected with the reason recorded.
    2. Sham position confound: position 1 is matched across solo_1/sham/k16 (held by both red teams); the length and population confounds are fixed by the frozen selection rule and the intersected population.
    3. Fidelity residue: stands as a stated limit (above); the commitment's internal structure is now fully determining (F5 fix), so the residue covers origin only.
    4. Vacuous count satisfaction: zero-word records are counted and excluded from count-kind denominators (F13 fix); the residue is printed, not hidden.
    5. Pooled-only boolean: replaced by the case-aggregated sign decision with an insufficient state (F2/F10 fixes); the claim is scoped to the endpoint contrast (B-F5).
    6. Replication control (B's alternative for the duplicated arm): considered and deferred — the case-level decision does not consume a noise-floor readout; if a future cycle needs one, the duplicate arm is the place to add it, with this paragraph as the pointer.

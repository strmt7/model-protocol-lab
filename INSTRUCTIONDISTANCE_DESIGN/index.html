    Status¶

    • Part II is the round-1 adjudication (coordinator #43, NO-BUILD), reproduced from the surviving working-tree copy.     Part I — The instrument as built (candidate text as amended)¶

    1. What exactly varies — the distance definition¶

    1.1 A dose is a verified rendered-token delta, per candidate¶

    Transplant of needle's dose settlement, in substance (model_protocol_lab/needle.py docstring and SIZING_RULE_TEXT):

    • For one case and one candidate, let adj_ids be the rendered token ids of the adjacent arm (§3) under that candidate's own template/backend. A separated or displaced arm at dose d is a construction whose rendered ids satisfy len(ids) − len(adj_ids) == d exactly, verified on the accepted construction's final ids before generation.
    • The filler span is built by the sizing discipline of §1.4 (whole sentences from the cycled neutral corpus stream plus one truncated suffix, probed in closeness order, encode budget 512 per cell) until the delta is exact. If no span in budget hits the delta exactly, the cell is arm_unmatchable — never approximated, never silently sized in characters.
    • Therefore "distance" is not a property of the filler text, not characters, and not a cross-candidate constant: the same filler string tokenizes differently per candidate, so each candidate gets a different filler text sized to the same verified delta. What is frozen across candidates is the dose (the delta), the corpus, the stream order, and the sizing rule (bound by digest in the protocol object, needle pattern).

    1.2 The measured separation, stated honestly¶

    The dose is the number of added tokens: in the separated arms the added tokens sit between the directive and the block; in the displaced arms the same number of added tokens sits after the block (the displaced arm's between-distance is the adjacent baseline; its dose is length). The adjacent render itself has a small nonzero separation (the fact sentence sits between directive and question); that baseline is a per-case, per-candidate quantity, reported per candidate as the per-arm rendered-token counts in the records, never as a single printed token figure in prose.

    1.3 Doses¶

    DOSES = (256, 1024) rendered tokens.

    • Derivation, not borrowing: 256 and 1024 are the two largest neutral levels of the distraction factorial (distraction.py NEUTRAL_LEVELS = (64, 256, 1024)), and 1024 is needle's smaller dose. The reasons they are right here are local: (i) the largest dose must fit the default context cap with the whole prompt — --max-context default is 2048 (cli.py) and under it a 1024-token filler plus the adjacent prompt plus template head plus generation budget is the largest power-of-four dose that is a real measurement rather than a guaranteed arm_context_exceeded column (executed in the fresh review: worst 1024-arm total 1,395 tokens against the 2,048 cap across every catalog family); (ii) two doses give three same-turn layouts at two doses (adjacent, 256, 1024) — three points, printed with denominators, from which no shape is claimed (§2.2).
    • The sketch's 128/512 pair is rejected: 128 is below every shipped neutral dose except 64 and maximises the short-dose null risk the sketch itself names; nothing in the tree supports it.
    • Cells that exceed the run's --max-context are arm_context_exceeded, counted per arm, never truncated into existence.

    1.4 The sizing rule (frozen text; amendment J)¶

    The protocol carries the SHA-256 of the sizing rule text (instructiondistance.SIZING_RULE_TEXT, "instructiondistance-single-phase-exact-v1"): spans are whole sentences plus one truncated suffix (span_text) over the cycled neutral corpus stream, every sized cell searching from the stream head; candidates are probed in span_candidates closeness order against the dose, each probe rendering the full arm content under the candidate's own template, accepting the first whose rendered token count minus the case's adjacent-arm rendered token count equals the dose exactly, verified on the accepted construction's final ids (the probe render is the arm render, so acceptance and verification are one encode). One encode per probe; the per-cell encode budget is 512.

    This is needle's sizing discipline with one named omission (amendment J / A-K5c): needle's rule carries an in-span offset-verification phase (needle.py's SIZING_RULE_TEXT), because needle measures where inside the filler a fact sits. This instrument has no offset variable — the filler sits wholly between the directive and the block, or wholly after it — and the "\n" joiner insulates the span from the block it precedes or follows, so delta-exactness on the final render is the only acceptance condition. The transplant is therefore not "verbatim in substance"; it is the needle discipline minus the phase that has no analogue here (executed in the fresh review: 12/12 cells exact under the single-phase rule).

    2. The claim and the ordering¶

    2.1 Claim (frozen, one-directional, per candidate, as¶

    re-scoped by amendment F)

    For one candidate, over the cases it completes in all three arms of a dose contrast (adjacent, separated, displaced) whose adjacent cell is compliant_correct (§5.2), let D be that population, S the cases in D whose separated cell is noncompliant, and T the cases in D whose dose-matched displaced cell is noncompliant. The label instruction_distance_drop_observed fires exactly under the rule in §6. No other sentence is a headline.

    The falsifiable statement behind it, with its conditioning and completion scope carried in the sentence (amendment F): per-candidate compliance with the frozen directive (§3.2), conditioned on the cases the candidate completes in the adjacent, separated, and displaced arms of the dose and answers compliant-correct when adjacent, is identical at added distance 0 and at added distance d. Proved wrong, per dose, only by the §6 pattern — discordant pairs (compliant adjacent, noncompliant separated) in excess of both the derived floor and the dose-matched displaced control. Reverse discordants (noncompliant adjacent, compliant separated) are counted and printed beside every decision (n_reverse), never pooled away.

    The label is an excess-of-between-losses label, never a distance effect simpliciter (amendment F, estimand restatements): a pure constraint-to-generation effect yields S = T → below_threshold even at a 24/24 → 0/24 collapse, and a genuine between-effect exactly masked by the control's own trailing-filler effect (S = 10, T = 10) also yields below_threshold. Both are the frozen estimand operating as defined, with S and T printed.

    2.2 What the claim is NOT — no ladder, no shape, no¶

    cross-turn rung

    • Not monotone decay, not a threshold. The readout prints the three same-turn compliance rates with denominators; per-dose labels can invert the raw dose order through control noise alone, so no sentence describes a curve, a threshold distance, or decay. The literature's "rapidly degrades" (LongIns, §10) is a claim about exam-task performance at 4K–16K tokens on API models; it is not a design input and no shape is pre-registered.
    • The cross-turn arm is excluded, by name. A scripted turn pair bundles turn boundary + assistant-turn presence + role alternation + token distance; it cannot sit on the token-dose dimension. Multiturn owns cross-turn delivery.
    • The adjacent-after arm is a control, not a rung (§4): distance 0, order reversed. It is reported descriptively and never enters a dose contrast.

    3. Population and frozen texts¶

    3.1 Generated cases, stage-local, never the Split¶

    Cases are generated from the run seed by a frozen SHA-256 derivation (needle pattern), live only in the stage, and never enter the user's search/held-out Split.

    Case shape: a fact block B that is byte-identical and internally adjacent in every arm —

    The {topic} number is {value}.
What is the {topic} number?
    • {value}: a 3-digit integer (100–999) from the seed derivation; pairwise distinct across cases (needle redraw discipline). 3 digits keeps the answer a single short digit string whose compliant form is unambiguous, and makes a closed-book guess a 1-in-900 event before any model prior.
    • {topic}: drawn from a frozen topic list of 24 single words (harbor, summit, quarry, tundra, granite, willow, copper, saddle, walnut, velvet, cobalt, marble, cinder, falcon, garnet, saffron, indigo, juniper, lagoon, magnet, nickel, onyx, pepper, quartz), verified absent from the neutral corpus as case-folded substrings and pinned by test. (The first candidate list had four collisions — meadow, orchard, timber, hazel occur in the corpus — and was corrected before freezing, needle's own freeze discipline.)
    • The fact is inside B, after the directive in every directive-first arm, so the fact↔question distance is constant across arms by construction. What moves is only directive↔B separation and, in the displaced control, B's distance to the generation point (§4).

    Case count: 24 (CASE_COUNT = 24, placement's count; amendment A — the candidate's §12 verdict sentence printed a stale case count, corrected in Part III). The derivation is the decision rule's, §6: the frozen rule at N=24 reaches power 0.9088 at a 50% conditional drop while at N=20 it reaches only 0.813, and N=16 (negation's count) cannot support the rule's D≥10 floor at realistic adjacent pass rates.

    Candidates: at most 24 (run_experiment's operative cap, experiments.py: "Supply between 1 and 24 candidates"; amendment A — the candidate cited an audit budget in determinism.py, which is not the operative cap).

    3.2 The frozen directive (single draw, scoped)¶

    Reply with only the number: no units, no sentence, nothing else.

    (64 chars — amendment A; the candidate printed a wrong character count, an error the round-1 verifier had already flagged.) Byte- identical in every arm that carries it; only its position and its distance to B vary. The claim is scoped to this string. A second phrasing is excluded from v1: two phrasings × the arm set doubles the panel and re-opens the single-rendering collinearity question for zero decision value — the instrument's subject is distance, and phrasing is held constant precisely so distance can vary alone.

    3.3 What the cases do NOT inherit¶

    The tree's shipped/generated populations already carry answer-only directives (the example fixtures, numform's templates, negation's contract). On user fixtures the frozen directive would duplicate or contradict an incumbent directive (hierarchy's construct), unscreened. On generated cases the frozen directive is the only directive in the prompt, by construction and by the §7 screen. That is why this instrument generates its own cases instead of treating user fixtures.

    4. Arms and the attribution contract¶

    Six arms per case. Layouts are within one user turn; joiner "\n" (distraction JOINER); B and the directive are byte-identical wherever they appear.

    Arm Layout Role
    adj C + B headline reference (added distance 0)
    sep_256 C + filler(256) + B headline dose 256
    sep_1024 C + filler(1024) + B headline dose 1024
    disp_256 C + B + filler(256) displaced control, dose-matched
    disp_1024 C + B + filler(1024) displaced control, dose-matched
    adj_after B + C order/recency control, descriptive

    4.1 Why the sketch's control set was insufficient¶

    The sketch claimed the cross-turn arm and adj_after "bound" the filler confound. They do not: neither contains filler, so in the sketch every separated cell differed from every control cell in two ways at once — filler presence (hence total prompt length) and between-ness. (Part II, F1, executes this; §4.2 is the cure.)

    4.2 The displaced control is the missing arm¶

    disp_d carries filler sized to the same verified delta d over the same case's adjacent render, in the same turn, but after B, so:

    • total rendered prompt length: equal to sep_d (both are adj + d tokens, verified per candidate — the sizing rule sizes each arm's span to its own exact delta, so seam tokenization differences are absorbed by the search);
    • filler presence/content class: same corpus, same dose. The two arms' spans are drawn from the same stream prefix before sizing; they are not the same span — per-arm sizing accepts different (whole, suffix) constructions (executed in the fresh review: one asymmetric seam merge gave a 284-char separated span vs a 285-char displaced span at dose 256, both delta-exact). A description of the two arms as sharing one span would be false; per-arm sizing is what makes the doses equal, not span identity.
    • directive↔B distance: the adjacent distance (the filler is not between them);
    • what does differ from adj: B's distance to the generation point grows by d, and total length grows by d.

    The headline contrast is therefore an excess of losses: on the headline population, S counts sep_d compliance losses and T counts disp_d compliance losses on the same cases, and the rule (§6) fires only when S exceeds T by a derived margin. A pure question-recency effect fires on disp_d, not sep_d, and works against the headline. A pure filler-presence/length effect fires on both — how completely the margin differences it out depends on how case-correlated the effect is, and §6 prints both bounds honestly (a perfectly case-correlated length effect gives S = T and can never fire; the independent-cells bound is printed as a number, 0.0788, with its premise, §6).

    Residual confounds, printed beside every decision, never argued away: (i) value-mediation — in sep_d the filler is also between the directive and the fact, but fact↔question adjacency is held (§3.1), and a compliance loss with the value kept (noncompliant_correct_value, §5.2) cannot be a fact-loss story; the S split by value-kept/value-lost is printed so a fact-mediated pattern is visible as such; (ii) filler-proximity (amendment G) — on the directive-first arms, filler immediately preceding B is coextensive with between-ness, so a mechanism that fails iff filler immediately precedes the governed block (the directive never consulted; executed in the fresh review as a deterministic mock firing S = 12, T = 0) fires this label, and the separating arm is not among the six; the value-kept/value-lost split mitigates but does not gate; (iii) filler content is one frozen corpus (§4.3) — the claim is scoped to neutral prose filler, and a topically-related or instruction-like filler is a different treatment, excluded.

    4.3 Filler¶

    distraction.NEUTRAL_CORPUS (71 sentences, digest NEUTRAL_CORPUS_SHA256), reused by needle. Executed hygiene: 71 sentences, no digit character of any kind — filler cannot contain the answer value, a competing number, or a digit that primes a numeric format. Spans are stream prefixes in stream order. Screening per §7.

    5. Scorer¶

    5.1 The span and the two verdicts¶

    The judged span is exactly core.evaluate's answer: the decoded pre-boundary span. Two verdicts are derived from the record per cell, never conflated:

    • Byte-exact verdict (descriptive, beside the headline): exact_match conjoined with boundary_found in the cell (sibling pattern). This is the lab's verdict; the probe does not redefine it.
    • Compliance (headline): a total function of the span under the frozen grammar §5.2, derived in the stage from the recorded span and recomputed by the diff validator from the carried primitives (§5.4).

    The reply contract is answer-only and byte-identical in every arm, so a fully compliant response is expected, byte for byte — there is no prose arm whose compliant output is a superstring of the expected answer; prose output is, under this instrument's frozen contract, noncompliance itself, in every arm equally (the negation settlement: byte-identical reply contract in every arm).

    Cap censoring: the default generation cap is 64 tokens. A compliant answer is ≤3 digits plus boundary — three orders under the cap — and no arm demands pre-answer prose, so the answer's position in the output does not move with the treatment. The remaining channel is input-side: longer arms invite arm_context_exceeded attrition (§7), printed beside every rate, and headline populations require completion in all three arms of the contrast (§5.3).

    5.2 Frozen compliance grammar and decomposition¶

    (amendments B1–B3, C)

    Grammar (total, no stripping, no casefolding, no parsing beyond the stated extraction): a completed cell is compliant iff its span matches \A[0-9]+\Z — fullmatch semantics (amendment B2: Python's $ anchor matches before a trailing newline, so re.match(r"^[0-9]+$", "417\n") is True while fullmatch is False; the frozen semantics is fullmatch). The digit alphabet is ASCII [0-9] everywhere (amendment B3): digit_count and first-run extraction use [0-9] only — str.isdigit() and \d disagree with each other on spans like "4²7", and Unicode digits (٤١٧, ４１７) are non-digits under this grammar.

    Decomposition of every completed cell into exactly one class (expected 417):

    Class Definition Example
    compliant_correct compliant ∧ span == expected "417"
    compliant_wrong compliant ∧ span != expected "418", "0417"
    noncompliant_correct_value ¬compliant ∧ first maximal [0-9]+ run == expected "The answer is 417.", "417 crates"
    noncompliant_wrong ¬compliant ∧ (no digit run ∨ first run != expected) "1,417" (first run 1), "I cannot find that number.", ""
    boundary_only ¬boundary_found ∧ the span is compliant-correct "417" with the boundary missed

    Frozen rulings: the value extraction is the first maximal ASCII digit run — "1,417" is noncompliant_wrong, not correct-value (grouping is a format violation and its first run is not the value); leading zeros are compliant but wrong ("0417"); a value embedded in a longer digit string is compliant-wrong ("14170"). Empty span is noncompliant_wrong.

    Boundary disposition (amendment B1, per negation §4.2 as shipped). Boundary-missing cells are grammar-classified over the whole-output span (the span core.evaluate defines when no boundary is found). A boundary-missing cell whose span is compliant-correct decomposes as boundary_only — boundary attrition only: it cannot count toward D (D requires the adjacent cell boundary-found, which compliant_correct already implies) and never toward S or T. Boundary-missing cells with any other span classify normally (a boundary-missing "418" is compliant_wrong; a boundary-missing "The answer is 417." is noncompliant_correct_value and does count as a loss in S/T). Completion = a span was produced. This disposition is exactly negation's shipped boundary_only rule; the candidate's blanket "boundary-missing cells are decomposed out first" was broader than the precedent it cited and is superseded (Part III, item 6, executes the three readings the blanket text admitted, yielding three different labels on identical generations).

    5.3 Conditioning¶

    D = cases whose adjacent cell is compliant_correct, inside the population of cases completed in the adjacent, separated, and displaced arms of the dose. Correctness alone is the wrong baseline property: a case answered correctly-but-noncompliantly when adjacent was never compliant, and its separated noncompliance is not a distance loss; a case compliant-but-wrong when adjacent can show a compliance loss but is not evidence the candidate both knew and formatted the answer at distance 0. The conditioning is on the reference arm only (exactly negation's D), so the treated arm's outcome never excludes a case from D; it only classifies it. The full per-arm 2×2 (compliant × correct) is printed for every arm (§8), so a correctness drop at distance is visible as its own readout — it is simply not the headline, because correctness at distance is needle/distraction's axis.

    5.4 Records: validator-recomputable primitives¶

    (amendment C)

    Per cell the record carries: verdict, boundary_found, boundary_text_match, input_tokens, answer_digest (SHA-256 of the span), first_digit_run_digest (SHA-256 of the first maximal ASCII digit run; "" when the span has no run — amendment B3), nondigit_projection_digest (SHA-256 of the span with every ASCII digit removed — the field amendment C adds), and the descriptive integers answer_len (characters) and digit_count (total ASCII digit characters).

    Every §5.2 class is a function of the digests plus the case's expected digest, and only of the digests: compliant ⇔ answer_digest != sha256("") ∧ nondigit_projection_digest == sha256(""); correct ⇔ answer digest equals expected digest; correct-value ⇔ first-run digest equals expected digest. answer_len / digit_count are descriptive only and never a verdict input: under the candidate's original bare-integer derivation (compliant ⇔ answer_len == digit_count), an executed forgery — identical digests, digit_count 3 vs 10 — flipped noncompliant_correct_value ↔ compliant_wrong undetectably (Part III, item 9). The diff validator recomputes the classes from the record alone; the stage classifies spans by digesting them into exactly these inputs, so stage and validator can never disagree (the cycle-190 digest-binding discipline; decidability executed on 15 spans by three parties, Part III). No raw answer text, no shape string, no prose is carried.

    Per record the stage also carries arm_tokens and id_digests per arm. An arm that was never constructed (the sized arms when unmatchable, or context-exceeded before sizing) carries neither — there is no render to measure — and every constructed sized arm's token count must sit exactly its dose above the adjacent arm's, which the validator checks on the stored counts.

    6. Decision rule — derived for this panel, executed¶

    Rule, per candidate, per dose d ∈ {256, 1024}, over cases completed in adj, sep_d, and disp_d:

    • D = # cases with adj cell compliant_correct.
    • S = # cases in D with sep_d noncompliant.
    • T = # cases in D with disp_d noncompliant.
    • n_reverse = # cases with adj noncompliant ∧ sep_d compliant — printed beside, never in the rule.
    • Fire instruction_distance_drop_observed iff D ≥ 10 ∧ S ≥ 6 ∧ S − T ≥ 5. Otherwise: D < 10 → insufficient; D ≥ 10 without the pattern → below_threshold (an observed null at this panel, weak evidence, never invariance).

    Operating characteristics (exact enumeration, four independent derivations agreeing; corrected per amendment E):

    Model Fire rate, frozen rule, N=24
    random labeller 0.0013
    no-effect competent 0.0058
    drop q = 0.25 0.2460
    drop q = 0.50 0.9088
    drop q = 0.75 0.9993
    pure length/presence (independent cells) 0.0788
    pure length/presence (fully case-correlated) 0.000 (by construction: S = T)

    The pure-length bound carries its premise (amendment E). The independent-cells figure is the worst case only under corr(sep, disp) ≥ 0. Under negatively paired nulls the bound is breached, executed by two independent methods that agree: antithetic Monte Carlo — 0.1085 at ρ = −0.3, 0.1333 at ρ = −0.6; Gaussian copula — 0.1102 at ρ = −0.5, 0.1327 at ρ = −0.8. The corrected statement: the pure-length rate is at most 0.133 at ρ = −0.8 in every executed model, while the no-effect rate stays ≤ 0.0077 in every paired model executed by any party (positive-ρ paired nulls only improve the rule: no-effect 0.0023 / 0.0004 / ~0 at ρ = +0.3 / +0.6 / +0.9, power 0.918–0.938). No single "the control rate is X" sentence may be printed.

    Family-wise (amendment E). 2 dose decisions × 24 candidates = 48 decisions per run. At the no-effect rate, the exact expectation is 0.28 false labels per panel with P(≥1) = 0.24. Under candidate-level frailty (Beta-distributed baseline rates, κ = 50/20/10) the no-effect expectation rises to E = 0.40–0.59, P(≥1) = 0.33–0.44. At the printed pure-length bound (0.0788 per decision): E ≈ 3.78 labels, P(≥1) ≈ 0.98. All three conversions print beside the verdicts (§8).

    Verdict availability (amendment E). The D ≥ 10 floor is reached with probability 0.511 at an adjacent compliant-correct rate of 0.40 and 0.153 at 0.30 (exact), so insufficient is the common verdict for weak candidates by construction; §8 states this in degree, not only in kind.

    Utility scope (amendment F). The panel is powered for large drops (≥0.5 conditional) and weak for a 0.25 drop (0.2460 exact; 0.78–0.93 power range at q = 0.50 across every executed perturbation — pairing, frailty conditioning, even N=20: 0.813). The instrument is a per-dose large-drop falsifier; the 0.25 row is labelled below resolution wherever the operating characteristics print.

    Withholding semantics (frozen): insufficient = the panel cannot speak (withheld, never acquittal); below_threshold = the pattern was looked for at this panel's stated power and not found (weak evidence).

    7. Fail-closed vocabulary (amendment D)¶

    No invented words beyond the section-label pattern:

    • Stage details: {cancelled, instruction_distance_record_budget_exceeded, instruction_distance_screen_failed}. …_screen_failed is bound to the stage-level screens: the special-token-literal audit over the frozen directive, fact/question templates, topics, and corpus against every candidate format, and the boundary-text containment check over the generated texts — either hit fails the whole stage closed before any generation.
    • Per-cell details: {"", arm_context_exceeded, arm_evaluation_failed, arm_unmatchable, arm_screened} — the needle cell set including arm_screened (amendment D: the candidate's §7 claimed "the needle cell set" while omitting it). Per-cell screening is the needle rule: a constructed filler span containing the case value (case-folded) or the candidate's decoded boundary text is screened — the cell is arm_screened, counted per arm in §8's attrition, and the stage continues. (Read literally with a stage-level binding, one screened span would have failed the whole stage; the shipped behaviour is needle's per-cell disposition, and the screening rule text in the protocol says so.)
    • Whole-stage failure (generator, renderer, serializer, non-UTF-8): {"available": False, "detail": "instruction_distance_unavailable"}.
    • Section labels only: instruction_distance_drop_observed / below_threshold / insufficient.
    • Model noncompliance is scored, never a detail. No arm_parse_failed, no per-arm "not applicable".
    • A construction that cannot be verified exact is never shipped approximate: sizing failure inside the budget is the per-cell arm_unmatchable (§1.1), which is the verification discipline operating, not an exception to it.

    8. Report shape¶

    Per candidate, per dose, in order: (1) per-arm 2×2 counts (compliant × correct) for all six arms with per-arm attrition (n_context_exceeded, n_evaluation_failed, n_unmatchable, n_screened — amendment D) beside each; (2) D, S, T, n_reverse, the decision, and S split by value-kept (noncompliant_correct_value) vs value-lost, with S − T beside it; (3) the adj_after compliance rate (order control) and the closed-book floor (§8.1); (4) per-case id_length_delta per arm against the adjacent render and max_abs_id_delta — token-ID divergence is reported, not gated (arms differ in text by construction; same-ids between adj and another arm is a rendering anomaly, and a same-ids compliance flip is a determinism finding); (5) the operating-characteristics paragraph (§6, in substance, at this panel's N=24 — printed, not footnoted), carrying amendment E's corrected conversions and amendment F's scope sentences.

    8.1 Closed-book floor (descriptive, not an arm)¶

    No extra generation: the value is a seed-derived 3-digit number stated only in the fact sentence, so a candidate that never saw the fact can be compliant-correct only by guessing the number. The section prints this derivation (900 equiprobable values under the generator; model priors over round numbers are a stated caveat, not a hidden one) instead of spending an arm on it.

    9. Overlap boundary — per shipped probe, with the¶

    duplication disclosure (amendment H)

    Probe Its owned axis The line
    needle (191) where an answer-bearing fact sits in fixed filler; recall vs depth needle moves the fact; here the fact↔question geometry never moves (§3.1); the directive moves relative to a fixed block, and the score is form compliance, not recall.
    placement (193) question vs payload order inside one turn placement moves the question around content; here question and content are one fixed block B and what moves is a directive around B, at verified token doses placement never uses.
    distraction (184) irrelevant filler presence/dose spliced into a fixture's own message, verdicts re-measured distraction's filler is the treatment and its readout is the fixture verdict. Here filler is the spacing medium, held present-and-equal in the displaced control so presence is differenced out; the readout is compliance with a directive the filler separates from its question.
    position which option position holds the correct MC answer no options, no permutation.
    multiturn drift (189) information sharded across turns all arms are single-turn; cross-turn delivery is excluded by name (§2.2).

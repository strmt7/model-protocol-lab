    Question bundling — design round (ADJUDICATED: NO-BUILD)¶

    Round summary (coordinator #46, design round only — nothing built)¶

    Wave-11 slate rank 5, the last wave-11 item: question bundling (tentatively run --bundle). The candidate sketch (~/workspace/agents_out/wave11/slate_candidates.md Candidate 5): generated cases of K=3 independent questions (fact recall + small arithmetic, disjoint answer spaces); a bundled arm (all K in one user turn, frozen convention "reply with one line per question, 1: <answer>", one generation, per-question verdicts parsed from the response) against a split arm (three scripted turn pairs with frozen assistant replies, the scored question rotated through all three positions); falsified by any (position, candidate) discordant pair; worst case 20 cases × (1 + 3) arms × 24 candidates = 1,920 generations (arithmetic verified correct by all lanes).

    • Tree at main a6705d1b9607 (cycle 213 closed green, 8,442 tests, precise coverage 95.3455%). This round was READ-ONLY except for this doc and the briefs; no suite was run, no push was made, no tag was cut or discussed.
    • Specialists (blind to each other): researcher (~/workspace/agents_out/bundle/research.md), red-team A (redteam_a.md), red-team B (redteam_b.md), verifier/claims auditor (verifier.md). All four briefs landed; the verifier resolved every externally checkable claim against primary sources, re-ran every surviving PoC, and recomputed all decision math with independent code; the coordinator re-executed the load-bearing claims itself (§4). Fabrication sweep: clean — a sixth consecutive round. No invented symbol, no fabricated citation, and no unreproduced load-bearing number in any brief; the failures found were two non-load-bearing line drifts in red-team B (≤ a few lines, substance intact), handled in §5.
    • Convergence: unanimous NO-BUILD — researcher, both blind red teams, and the verifier each reached it independently for the sketch as specified, fatal by fatal, by execution. The two red teams, working blind, converged on the same core defects (scorer inversion, unfrozen parser, cap censoring ordered by slot, the noise-firing falsifier) with independently executed numbers that agree. The researcher additionally specifies a complete redesigned instrument (run --multi-question, §3) and recommends it as a resubmission candidate; the verifier reproduced its decision math exactly and ruled it a resubmission, not an amendment, under this program's own test, and the researcher himself classifies it the same way. The coordinator concurs: the verdict is NO-BUILD — for the sketch, and as a build order for the redesigned instrument in this round. §3 records the resubmission specification in summary, including the seven points the fresh review must settle head-on.

    1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects of the candidate as specified¶

    Each defect below is individually blocking and was established by execution (attribution: R = researcher, A = red-team A, B = red-team B, V = verifier, C = coordinator).

    F1 — The scorer cannot emit the headline: one byte-exact span per fixture, and the arms' reply contracts are mutually unsatisfiable¶

    core.evaluate emits exactly one verdict per fixture: core.py:797 exact_match = status == "completed" and answer == case.expected, where answer is the whole decoded pre-boundary span (core.py:939 answer_ids = output_ids if cut is None else output_ids[:cut]; core.py:966 answer = decoded). There is no per-question verdict anywhere in the tree's scoring path. Executed through the real evaluate with stub backends (R poc1, A poc1, B poc1, V re-ran; C re-ran, §4): a perfectly correct, fully compliant bundled reply (1: Paris\n2: 42\n3: blue) scores False against every per-question expected string and against newline-joined bare answers; it scores True only against the whole bundled string as expected — which collapses the headline to one all-or-nothing verdict in which a 2-of-3-correct bundle and an all-wrong bundle are the same observation, an omission, an answer swap, and all-wrong are indistinguishable, and one trailing "." fails a fully correct bundle. Against the whole-string expected, 8 of 9 semantically identical formatting variants (no labels, 1. separators, a preamble, reordered labelled lines, a trailing period) fail — the verdict is formatting luck. And the inversion is verbatim DIRECTIVE F1: a disobedient model that outputs only the first answer (Paris) passes the Q1 fixture where the compliant bundled model fails. The split arm has the same trap in mirror image: under the shared convention its compliant reply 1: amber fails a bare-answer expected and passes a labelled one, while its bare reply does the reverse — whichever expected string is chosen, one of two compliant behaviours fails. The sketch never chooses, because it cannot: the two arms demand different reply contracts under one scorer.

    F2 — The line grammar is unfrozen, honest parsers disagree on the same responses, and parse failure has no honest home¶

    The sketch's per-question readout rests entirely on a "frozen line grammar" that is never frozen: it states no label syntax, no strip policy, no duplicate/missing-line rule, no rule for answers containing :. Executed (A poc2, B poc3, V re-ran): two honest parsers — positional vs by-label — return different verdict vectors on the same responses (reordered lines: [F,F,T] vs [T,T,T]; a preamble: [F,F,F] vs [T,T,T]). A charitable strict line parser passes only 2 of 10 semantically perfect bundled variants (prose preface or trailing prose, bold labels, blank lines, glued lines, unnumbered lines, 1) labels, 0-based labels all lose slots wholesale); one missing line loses every slot under positional parsing, and first-wins vs last-wins duplicate handling disagrees on self-correcting responses. The sketch specifies no parse-failure outcome code: folding parse failures into "wrong" makes the headline a formatting measure, and excluding them is treatment-selected attrition (the bundled arm produces the parse failures) — answer-syntax F2 through the parser door. The tree's precedent (structured's parse-vs-schema split, structured.py:68 OUTCOMES) exists precisely because this pooling is dishonest, and the sketch adopts none of it.

    F3 — Digest-only records cannot carry per-slot verdicts (instruction-distance F2 verbatim)¶

    Every sibling generated probe's records carry digests only (negation.py:57: "Records carry digests only"; the verdict identity is verdict == (answer_digest == sha256(expected)) and boundary_found, recomputed by the diff validator). Executed (A poc, B, C §4): ten distinct bundled texts — all-correct, Q1-wrong, Q2-wrong, a swap, an omission, a parse failure — collapse to the same record class under a whole-answer digest (the digest differs, but nothing in it says which slot failed, or whether anything parsed). Per-slot verdicts, swap flags, and parse decompositions are underivable from the records the lab keeps, and therefore unrecomputable by the diff validator. The sketch specifies none of the three tree escapes: a numform-style digest-bound answer text (the one sanctioned exception, cycle 190), density-style carried primitives (e.g. an ordered word-hash list), or stage-attested booleans (the forgery surface the digest discipline exists to remove). A probe whose headline cannot be recomputed from its own records is not buildable in this tree.

    F4 — There is no decision rule: the any-discordant-pair falsifier fires on noise, and the panel's cells are clustered¶

    The sketch's falsifier is "any (position, candidate) discordant pair" over 20 × 3 × 24 = 1,440 pairs, with no floor, no direction, no test. Executed (R, A, B, V; C independently, §4): P(any discordance in the panel) = 0.9439 at per-arm jitter ε = 0.001 and 1.0000 at ε ≥ 0.005 (analytic, Monte-Carlo confirmed by three lanes). The one apparent number disagreement of the round — the researcher's 0.763 at "ε = 0.001" — is a parametrisation difference, settled by the verifier and re-derived by the coordinator: 0.763 is P(any) when 0.001 is the per-pair discordance probability (1 − 0.999^1440 = 0.7632 exactly); 0.9439 is the same quantity when 0.001 is the per-arm flip probability. Both are correct under their stated nulls; the fatal is unaffected (the rule fires with probability 1.000 by ε = 0.005 under both). A random labeller reaches ≥6 discordant of 60 per candidate with probability 1.000000, so no borrowed floor rescues it either. Worse, the panel's bundled cells are clustered by construction: the three bundled slot verdicts share one generation, so one boundary miss, truncation, or parse event flips all three together (executed, A: a boundary miss fails all 3 bundled slots vs 1 split slot) — any pair-counting repair that treats the 1,440 pairs as independent is mis-calibrated on this instrument specifically.

    F5 — Cap censoring is ordered by slot, and the budget is asymmetric by construction¶

    The run's default cap is 64 new tokens (cli.py:198). The bundled answer is K lines; the split answer is one. Executed (A, B, R poc3; C §4): a perfect model whose bundled reply needs 70 tokens records completed, truncated mid-slot-3, boundary missed, retained as an ordinary failure — while all three split replies pass. Survival by slot at caps 50/64 is [T,T,F]: the cap alone manufactures the sketch's advertised headline, "position 3 degrades first", on a model with no bundling deficit at all. The asymmetry is constitutive, not a tuning detail: the split arm receives K × 64 tokens of budget across its generations against the bundled arm's 64. Raising the bundled cap breaks comparability with the split arm; keeping it censors the readout's own axis. The sketch has no liveness rule (a truncated cell removing its whole case, attrition printed) of the kind density's _live discipline provides.

    F6 — Neither arm is what the claim names: the contrast bundles at least six variables, and "position" is two different variables in the two arms¶

    Executed renders (A, B; chatml bundled 205 chars / 1 message vs split-Q3 354 chars / 5 messages): the split arm's unscored questions are never answered by the model at all — its history is frozen assistant text the sketch never specifies. Both readings of that text are already owned or confounded: answers as history = fewshot demonstrations; Noted. = multiturn's scripted arm verbatim (multiturn.py:85 SCRIPTED_REPLY). The production alternative the utility names — asking separately, i.e. independent single-turn calls — does not exist in the sketch. What the contrast actually varies, simultaneously: turn count, scripted history presence and content, convention load (one instruction governing K lines vs one line, repeated), prompt length (whose sign reverses by position: the bundled prompt is longer than split-Q1's and shorter than split-Q3's), generation-cap exposure (F5), scoring machinery (parser vs byte-exact, F1/F2), and autoregressive exposure to the model's own earlier answers (the contamination the sketch wants to measure is present only in one arm — it is part of the treatment bundle, not separable from it). No separating control can even be stated: a length-matched control would need a different filler dose per position, and length-padding is itself the shipped distraction treatment. A discordance from this contrast is attributable to "bundling" only as a named bundle of all of the above — and the sketch's claim is written as a single-cause packaging claim. Separately, "position" pools two variables: in the bundled arm a slot is a fixed question identity/type (no within-bundle permutation is specified, so slot ≡ item), while in the split arm position is history depth by renderer construction (scored positions 1/2/3 = 1/3/5 messages). A by-position readout compares list position against turn depth and calls both "position".

    F7 — The disjointness the swap readout needs fails by chance, and when it fails the failure is invisible¶

    The sketch's contamination mitigation is disjoint answer spaces, "planted so a swapped answer is detectable as a swap". Executed (B; C spot-checked the arithmetic, §4): two small arithmetic answers in one case coincide with probability ≈ 0.07, and at least one collision among three with probability ≈ 0.19–0.21 — under the natural operand distributions, coincidence is a routine event, not an edge case. When two expected answers are equal, a swap is byte-identical to a perfect response: contamination becomes invisible in exactly the cells where it occurred. The sketch specifies no generator screen (contrast multiturn's built-in redraw discipline), and adding one post hoc selects the population (cases whose answers happen to collide are systematically easier/harder — the screen is a population choice, not a hygiene step). Without enforced-by-construction distinctness, the swap decomposition the sketch promises cannot be delivered; with it, the population is no longer the sketch's.

    F8 — The selection headline is manufactured by conjunction, and forbidden on this population besides¶

    The utility promises whether "the template they selected survives bundling". Executed arithmetic (A, B; C re-derived, §4): if a case passes bundled only when all 3 slots pass, then at identical per-question accuracy p in both arms the case-level gap is p − p³ = 0.171 at p = 0.9, 0.288 at p = 0.8, 0.357 at p = 0.7 — a large "bundling effect" manufactured by the conjunction alone, with zero treatment effect. If instead no case-level aggregation is defined, there is no selection readout at all: the tree's selection rule consumes per-fixture verdicts, and a bundled fixture has three. And the deeper bar is already settled in-tree: the numform settlement forbids a winner/selection readout on a generated population (experiments.py:11049 pattern; negation v1 adopted the same bar) — the sketch's population is generated, so its headline readout is one the program has already ruled out for exactly this population type.

    F9 — The population cannot deliver the utility: one K, the wrong K, and "recall" that is not generatable¶

    The utility sentence asks for "the bundle size at which per-question verdicts start moving" for a front-end bundling five questions. The instrument has exactly one K, and it is 3. One point is not a size readout: no curve, no threshold, no "at which" (prune-sweep F5's permutation/shape point in miniature). The population is also undefined where it matters: fact-recall questions with known answers are not seed-generatable and validator-regenerable the way this tree's generated probes require — a fact the model is asked to recall must come from a frozen external set (a different instrument, with provenance obligations) or be planted in the prompt, in which case the task is extraction, not recall, and the claim must be re-scoped to say so. The sketch says neither. Finally, with no family × slot rotation, slot and question family are perfectly collinear (F6), so even the descriptive by-position readout the sketch falls back on pools item difficulty into "position".

    F10 — The name is taken: bundle is the sealed-bundle term of art (blocking rename, independently of F1–F9)¶

    Executed naming audit (R §5, A, B, V): pack.py:1 "Sealed result bundles (model-protocol-lab pack / verify --bundle)", format id mpl-bundle/1 (pack.py:49), a live verify --bundle <path> flag (verify.py:645-675, dispatch to pack.run_verify_bundle), and USAGE §13.1 (docs/USAGE.md:3320, "Sealing a result (pack, verify --bundle)"). "Bundle" in this tree already names one thing, in adjacent subcommands, with a file format attached. A run --bundle flag is free only as an argparse string; under the tree's USAGE naming discipline (one word, one meaning, the same terms in USAGE/FAQ/CLI help — repo AGENTS.md) it fails the audit outright. Any future instrument in this space ships under a different name (the resubmission proposes run --multi-question, §3).

    Non-fatal, adjudicated¶

    • Generation-count arithmetic: the sketch's 1,920 worst case is correct (all lanes).
    • Duplication, precisely: no arm is byte-identical to a shipped probe's arm (checked by render comparison, not asserted). The boundary is exact per neighbour — placement owns question serial position within one turn; multiturn owns the full-vs-sharded-vs-scripted turn-packaging contrast (the sketch's split arm is multiturn's scripted construction); density owns simultaneous-demand load and per-serial-position fall under a byte-exact single-task scorer; needle owns fact position in filler; structured owns multi-part response parsing on user fixtures. The only element no shipped probe owns — K per-question verdicts from one generation — is exactly the element F1–F3 show the tree cannot currently score or record. The candidate is not a duplicate; it is a request for the one readout the record discipline forbids as specified.
    • External citation: Cheng, Kasai & Yu, batch prompting, arXiv:2301.08721 — verified at the primary source by the verifier (the sketch's citation is accurate, and fenced: API models, plain/few-shot formats, per-sample accuracy, no template-family crossing). It supports the phenomenon's existence; it does not support this instrument.

    2. Amendment or resubmission¶

    Under the program's standing test (negation = amendment: same population, arms, scorer, and rule shape, all already frozen and executable; payload / answer-syntax / directive / instruction-language / instruction-distance / history-frame / prune-sweep = resubmission when those change), the researcher's redesign changes every load-bearing element: population, arms, scorer, decision rule, headline, and name (§3). The sketch was never executable as a measurement — its split arm has no specified replies (F6), its scorer cannot emit its readout (F1), its records cannot carry its readout (F3), and its falsifier is a noise printer (F4). This is the payload configuration, not the negation one. The redesigned instrument is recorded in §3 as a resubmission candidate. It is not a build order; no build cycle may act on it without a fresh blind design review.

    3. The resubmission candidate (summary; full specification in the researcher brief §3)¶

    run --multi-question (module multiquestion.py), specified in full in ~/workspace/agents_out/bundle/research.md §3:

    • Population: generated, stage-local panels at K=3 and K=5, 24 cases each; sub-questions are planted-fact extraction (placement/density construction, named extraction, never "recall") and small arithmetic, all with single-normalized-word expected answers; family × slot rotation; generator screens enforce pairwise-distinct expected answers within a case (redraw discipline), so swap detection is a construction property.
    • Arms: bundled (one turn, K numbered questions) vs split_j (one turn, only question j, renumbered to 1, no history anywhere) — the production "asking separately" the utility names. Length difference disclosed as constitutive of packaging, printed per case, never gated; the claim is scoped to the packaging bundle, not a single cause.
    • Scorer: one total, marker-aligned parser over density's carried ordered word-hash list, symmetric across arms; per-slot outcomes pass / wrong_value (with swap flag) / parse_failed (decomposed missing_marker / extra_words); no answer text carried — the digest-compatible design is density's, so the numform exception is not invoked. Composite byte-exact printed as sensitivity only. Liveness: a case enters the headline only if the bundled cell and all split cells completed with boundary found; attrition printed.
    • Decision rule (derived, never borrowed): per candidate per panel, case-aggregated discordance (slots within a case share one generation — clustering handled by construction); D ≥ 6 discordant cases, F > P, and exact sign test p ≤ 0.05 → multiquestion_drop_observed; D < 6 → insufficient; else below_threshold. Researcher's 20k-trial Monte Carlo (verifier reproduced the full table exactly): fire 0.017 random / 0.012 competent-null / ≤ 0.014 clustered at K=3; power 0.979 at a 25% drop (K=3), 0.999 (K=5). Family-wise expectation printed: 48 decisions, ≈ 0.81 false labels expected under the random null, P(≥1) ≈ 0.56.
    • Counts: 240 generations per candidate; legal maximum 5,760; 1,152 ground records; house 12 MiB cap.
    • Vocabulary: frozen house pattern only — decisions {insufficient, below_threshold, multiquestion_drop_observed}, whole-stage fail-closed multiquestion_unavailable; no invented verdict words (verifier checked the set against the negation/density pattern).
    • No selection readout (numform settlement honoured).

    The fresh review must settle head-on (silence = NO-BUILD): (a) split renumbering (to-1 vs keep-slot-number — different treatments); (b) the decision-rule operating characteristics re-run under a case-clustered drop model and at real completed-case counts after attrition; (c) parse-failure in the headline (counted as not-pass with decomposition printed, vs conditioning on parse-ok cells — the latter is outcome-dependent conditioning and needs its own rule); (d) K=5 cost (5,760 legal-maximum generations) vs a K=3-only probe with honestly narrowed utility; (e) word-rule leniency (case-folding, punctuation-insensitive) as the headline scorer, with byte-exact demoted to sensitivity — or the reverse, with the rule re-derived; (f) extraction-not-recall scoping (a genuine-recall population is a different instrument and may not be smuggled in as an arm); and (g) the verifier's addition: arithmetic sums landing in {1..5} are not screened from the parser's marker words in §3.1 as written and would break the §3.3 grammar — the screen set must be closed before any build.

    4. Coordinator re-execution (independent, this session)¶

    • Scorer (F1): stub backend through the real core.evaluate — a fully compliant bundled reply scores False against per-question expected amber and against 1: amber, True only against the whole three-line string; a split reply 1: amber scores False against expected amber and True against 1: amber, with the bare reply exactly reversed. Both arms cannot be satisfied under one expected-string choice.
    • Records (F3): whole-answer digests of all-correct / Q1-wrong / Q2-wrong bundles are three distinct hashes with no slot information recoverable from any of them.
    • Falsifier (F4): both parametrisations reproduced exactly — 1 − 0.999^1440 = 0.7632 (per-pair reading, researcher's number) and 1 − (1 − 2·0.001·0.999)^1440 = 0.9439 (per-arm reading, red teams' number); at ε = 0.005 per-arm, P(any) = 1.0000.
    • Conjunction (F8): p − p³ re-derived — 0.171 / 0.288 / 0.357 at p = 0.9 / 0.8 / 0.7, matching red-team B exactly.
    • Disjointness (F7): under a triangular two-operand sum distribution the coordinator obtains P(pair coincidence) ≈ 0.075 and P(any of three) ≈ 0.21 — same magnitude as red-team B's 0.068 / 0.191 under its operand range; the substance (coincidence is routine) is confirmed, the small difference is the operand-range choice, not an error.
    • Cap (F5): a three-line bundle of moderate answers exceeds the 64-token default in character proxy (71 chars for three 20-char answers plus labels) where each split line is 23 — the censoring gradient is on the readout's own axis, as the red teams' token-level executions show.

    5. Errors found in the briefs (none load-bearing)¶

    • Red-team B: two line drifts (structured.py OUTCOMES at :68, cited ~:56; the exact_match record site at core.py:797, cited ~:815). Substance verified at the correct lines by the verifier.
    • The researcher's F3 number (0.763) and the red teams' (0.944) initially read as a disagreement; both are correct under their stated null parametrisations (§1 F4). No brief is wrong; the design doc prints both with their nulls.
    • No other errors: the verifier's mechanical sweep resolved every remaining symbol, line, count, and external citation in all three briefs (catalog = 29 families executed; grep counts exact).

    6. Verdict¶

    NO-BUILD — unanimous (researcher, red-team A, red-team B, verifier; coordinator concurs on its own re-execution). The sketch's headline readout cannot be emitted by the tree's scorer (F1), cannot be parsed by the grammar it never froze (F2), cannot be carried by the records the lab keeps (F3), cannot be decided by the falsifier it specifies (F4), is manufactured by the generation cap on a perfect model (F5), compares two arms neither of which is the thing the claim names (F6), promises swap detection its population defeats by chance (F7), promises a selection readout that conjunction arithmetic fabricates and the numform settlement forbids (F8), at a bundle size its own utility does not ask about (F9), under a name the tree already owns (F10). The redesigned run --multi-question instrument in §3 is a resubmission candidate for a future fresh blind design review — not a build order, and not queued ahead of any existing item. With this round, the wave-11 slate is fully adjudicated: ranks 1–5 all NO-BUILD, each with a resubmission candidate recorded.

    Cross-format history probe — design round (ADJUDICATED: NO-BUILD)¶

    Round summary (coordinator #44, design round only — nothing built)¶

    Wave-11 slate rank 3: cross-format history (tentatively run --history-frame). The candidate sketch (~/workspace/agents_out/wave11/slate_candidates.md Candidate 3): hold every message's text byte-identical, render the history turns in a foreign chat-template family A while the final turn stays in the candidate's own family B, and test whether per-candidate verdicts and the selection are invariant to that reframing.

    The round ran the full blind team (briefs in ~/workspace/agents_out/historyframe/: research.md, redteam_a.md, redteam_b.md, verifier.md). The verdict is unanimous NO-BUILD for the sketch as specified — researcher, both blind red teams, and the verifier's blind interim audit each reached it independently, fatal by fatal, by execution. The fabrication sweep is clean — a fourth consecutive round: every cited tree symbol resolves, and both external arXiv identifiers resolve to the cited titles, authors, and substance. The errors found are counting/criterion slips, corrected in §5; none is load-bearing for the verdict.

    The researcher additionally specified a complete repaired instrument (research §6: bos-native field splice over synthhistory's frozen prepended history, carrier-Format evaluation, two frozen donor families, a derived McNemar-gated classification). The verifier upholds, and the coordinator concurs, that this instrument is a resubmission, not an amendment, under this program's standing test (§2): it changes the population mechanism, replaces the headline BOS semantics, changes the headline population, and adds the decision rule the sketch lacks. It is recorded in §3 as the resubmission candidate. No build cycle may act on it without a fresh blind design review.

    1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects of the candidate as specified¶

    F1 — The population is undefined: "history" has three incompatible readings, and the shipped fixtures are half-vacuous¶

    The sketch never says where the history turns come from, and the tree offers three mutually exclusive sources — the fixture's own messages, synthhistory's scripted turns, self-generated replies — each a different instrument with a different construct and budget (RT-A F1, RT-B F4, R §2 F1, V F-POPULATION). Executed on the shipped example set (examples/fixtures.json, verified by all four lanes): search = search-copy-blue (1 message, no history) and search-latest-green (3 messages); held-out likewise 1 of 2. For a single-turn fixture the foreign condition is byte-identical to control by construction — guaranteed-concordant filler in every denominator. The loader admits 1-message fixtures (datasets.py:53-72; MAX_MESSAGES = 21 at datasets.py:14), so no dataset guarantees the sketch's "100 fixtures" of history. Under the fixture-own reading, history length (dose) and content are per-fixture and uncontrolled — in the shipped set the history assistant turns are task answers ("RED", "NORTH") and the final turns refer to them, so discordance would pool framing with history-content leverage (RT-A F12, RT-B F4). Under the self-generated reading the budget is false: each foreign cell needs one generation per history exchange before the scored generation — up to 28,800 generations at history_6 depth against the sketch's 7,200 (RT-A F1, arithmetic verified).

    F2 — The hybrid renderer does not exist, its seam semantics are unspecified, and the sketch's seam paragraph is wrong in both particulars¶

    Format.render (core.py:76-115) renders a whole message tuple in one framing and requires a final user turn. Executed by all four lanes: render(history_alone) raises ValueError for 29/29 catalog families — a history prefix, which ends with an assistant turn, is not a renderable object. The sketch's literal recipe (render the segments, concatenate) fails its own pre-registered control check: concatenating a history render (which needs a dummy closing user turn to satisfy render at all) with a final-turn render duplicates BOS, strands family A's open suffix mid-prompt, and inserts a dummy turn (RT-A F2: 182 vs 132 chars chatml, 362 vs 246 llama3, control condition included). The sketch's seam note — A's render "may carry its own BOS and closing suffix mid-prompt", to be kept "verbatim (recommended)" — is executed-wrong twice (RT-B F1, verifier V-2.2): in a prefix-truncated A render there is no A closing suffix at the seam (the open suffix is appended only after the final turn), and A's BOS lands at position 0, replacing B's, not mid-prompt. The decisions that determine the prompt's bytes are all unmade in the sketch, and each is load-bearing:

    • BOS ownership. Coordinator execution: the donor-BOS and final-BOS splice readings produce different prompts on 658/812 ordered family pairs. Only the choice is frozen in §3's resubmission; the sketch freezes none.
    • repeat_bos cadence. No catalog base sets it, but shipped candidates do (llama3/toggle-bos via catalog.candidates(mutate=True)). A per-segment cadence restart produces 1 BOS where the control has 4 (RT-A F2).
    • None-vs-empty fallbacks. core.py:57-73 honours empty-string overrides as distinct from None; a hybrid written with the natural or fallback fails control identity for mistral_large alone (open_suffix "" vs assistant_prefix " "), so a casual control test on chatml passes while the renderer is wrong (RT-A F2).
    • Open suffix is not history framing. open_suffix != assistant_prefix for 15/29 families (verifier-confirmed list), 9 of them opening a think block; the suffix is the generation prompt for the next reply, and any recipe that keeps a history render's tail injects it, sometimes unclosed, at the seam.

    The control-identity check the sketch pre-registers is a test, not a specification: three independent executions this round (coordinator: 118 candidate Formats × 2 conversations; verifier; researcher: 944/944 renders) prove a faithful field-splice semantics can reproduce B.render(full) byte-identically when A == B — by a recipe the sketch does not contain.

    F3 — There is no decision rule: the any-difference falsifier fires on noise with probability ≈ 1, and the selection readout is a one-flip tripwire¶

    The sketch's falsifier is verbatim the one this program has killed twice (DIRECTIVE_DESIGN F2; INSTRUCTIONDISTANCE_DESIGN F3): "any fixture whose verdict differs … or a selection flip in any foreign-frame condition." No floor, no discordant-pair rule, no fail-closed vocabulary. Executed (RT-A F3, RT-B F3, V F-NOISE): at per-cell jitter ε = 0.001, P(any discordance in the panel) = 0.909–0.992; at ε ≥ 0.005, ≈ 1.000. batchinv.py exists precisely because identical token sequences need not reproduce across batch composition, so ε = 0 cannot be assumed. The selection half is worse: _select_winner (experiments.py:3847-3863) requires the winner to pass every search fixture, so one flipped cell manufactures a "selection flip"; conditioned on a native winner existing, P(it loses all-pass status in ≥ 1 of 2 foreign conditions) = 0.633 / 0.866 / 0.982 at ε = 0.005 / 0.01 / 0.02 (RT-B F3, verifier re-evaluated). Nor can a builder patch in a familiar floor: any (6,·)-shaped discordant floor fires ≈ 1.000 at this panel's N = 100, because the null discordance mean is 50 (random) or ≈ 18 (competent) — the sibling floors were derived for panels of 16 generated cases, and this panel's N is the user's fixture count (RT-B F3). As specified, the instrument can essentially only report "not invariant" — a verdict decided by the rule, not measured from the model.

    F4 — Attribution: the treatment is a bundle of at least five simultaneous changes, and the claim names one cause¶

    Control vs foreign differ at once in (RT-A F4, RT-B F2, R §2 F4):

    • Marker identity — the named treatment.
    • BOS identity/presence (F2): the prompt's first tokens change with A. The tree treats BOS as a verdict-moving axis in its own right (core.local_repairs, core.py:118-143, ships toggle-bos/no-bos candidates to measure it).
    • History token length. Same six synthetic turns framed: 341 chars (mistral) to 989 chars (kimi3) (verifier-confirmed); per-exchange prompt-length deltas are nonzero for 766/812 ordered pairs, range [−213, +213] chars under a bos-inclusive splice (segment-only splice: 770/812, ±216 — both executions correct under their splice definitions, and the divergence between two honest executions is itself F2's point). Length and final-turn position are shipped axes (needle, distraction, placement).
    • Think scaffolding. Nine families carry think material inside their assistant history prefix itself (glm45, glm53, laguna, ling, nanbeige42, kimi3, k2horizon, hy4, minimax3 — verifier-executed count; §5 corrects RT-A's five). Foreign history in these families injects a foreign reasoning-channel scaffold the control lacks — an output-priming component, not a delimiter relabelling.
    • Closure conventions. deepseek history has no user-turn terminator (user_turn_end=""); some families' turns run together. "Foreign framing" here includes a segmentation change.

    The sketch's claim sentence attributes any discordance to framing-as-such. What the contrast can honestly estimate is discordance between two named, frozen prompt strings per (candidate, fixture) — the payload settlement. The sketch's Monday-morning framing ("whether history must be re-rendered on migration or can be carried over as-is") additionally fails on the artifact: the hybrid (A's BOS at the head of a B session, B's final turn) is neither a carried-over transcript nor a re-render — no migration produces it (RT-B F2).

    F5 — The pair rule is broken: unnamed pairs, a disjointness criterion that excludes the realistic migrations, and a family/candidate unit mismatch¶

    The ordered pair space is 29 × 28 = 812; the sketch freezes 2 (0.25%), names neither, and gives no selection rule beyond "disjoint marker sets" (RT-B F5). Executed by all lanes:

    • Aliased pairs collapse to control. Under the history-segment criterion (bos excluded), 36 ordered pairs have a foreign history segment byte-identical to control: classes {chatml, qwen3, qwen38, kolibri, lfm25, nemotron35}, {gemma, gemma3}, {deepseek, deepseek41}, {muse_glimmer, muse_spark} — 21 distinct history segments across 29 families. Including BOS, 24 ordered pairs are byte-identical outright. Any such cell is a guaranteed null pooled as invariance — the canonical-form ids-identical trap.
    • Disjointness selects against the utility. The aliased ChatML-shaped families are precisely the plausible real migrations (8 families' user_prefix contains <|im_start|>; verifier-confirmed). A disjoint-marker rule excludes every one and selects maximally distant pairs, where F4's bundle is largest. Marker disjointness is in any case the wrong criterion: the operative check is rendered-segment equality (331–355 of 406 unordered pairs are textually disjoint under either lane's extraction, including pairs whose segments are identical).
    • Global vs per-candidate pairs dilemma. Fixed global pairs collapse for candidates B ∈ {A1, A2} (their "foreign" condition is the control); per-B pairs make the per-condition selection compare candidates who faced different treatments — the restricted-common-set defect. The sketch's selection headline is coherent under neither reading (RT-B F5).
    • Unit mismatch. Selection in this tree is over candidates, not families: a run's candidates are one family's base plus local-repair/thinking variants (catalog.candidates, catalog.py:914ff; 118 candidate Formats across the catalog, 89 beyond the 29 bases — §5 corrects RT-B's 107). "Final-turn family B" and "foreign family A" are undefined for variant candidates whose framing differs from base exactly in the fields the splice moves (RT-A F10, RT-B F5).

    F6 — The boundary mechanism: a correct answer terminating like its history fails as a wrong answer, and the sketch has no decomposition¶

    Stage verdicts conjoin exact_match and boundary_found with the boundary resolved per candidate — i.e. per final-turn family B (experiments.py _resolve_boundaries). The hybrid's history demonstrates A's terminator after every assistant turn. Executed through the real core.evaluate (RT-B F6; reproduced exactly by the verifier and by the coordinator with independent stubs): a stub answering GREEN and terminating with B's <|im_end|> → exact_match=True, boundary_found=True; the same stub answering GREEN and terminating with A's <|eot_id|> → exact_match=False, boundary_found=False, answer recorded as 'GREEN<|eot_id|>' — with no boundary cut, the terminator text joins the scored span, so the failure is double-counted as a wrong answer. The sketch never states which boundary governs a hybrid cell and provides no exact-only vs boundary-only decomposition (the payload/negation settlements made that decomposition mandatory). Under the any-difference falsifier, termination-style discordance is indistinguishable from answer discordance, and the migration advice differs completely between the two. Ordered attrition rides along: foreign framings run longer, so arm_context_exceeded censoring is ordered by arm (the DIRECTIVE F6(a) defect), with no per-condition attrition reporting specified.

    F7 — The token-encoding mitigation is incoherent: "token-real in both frames" selects the headline population by checkpoint, and its fallback contradicts the headline¶

    The sketch concedes the deliforge axis leaks in and proposes the headline population be "cells where the history's markers are token-real in both frames, or the confound is disclosed per cell, never pooled silently." Executed/adjudicated (RT-A F7, RT-B F7, R §2 F6, V F-TOKENREAL): token-reality is a property of (checkpoint tokenizer × literal), not of a frame — under HF's default split_special_tokens=False a literal becomes a real control token iff the checkpoint's tokenizer reserves it (control_tokens.py:1-14; backend encode is a plain tokenizer.encode(text, add_special_tokens=False, ...) call, backends.py:1192-1200, :1284). On a given checkpoint, typically only its native family's markers are reserved, so the headline population varies per checkpoint, is selected on exactly the axis being measured, and can be empty — with no eligibility pre-check and no fail-closed vocabulary for that outcome (contrast deliforge's find_encoding_pair, deliforge.py:238-281, which fails closed). "In both frames" is undefined for families whose markers are ordinary text. And the OR-fallback contradicts the pooled selection headline: a per-condition selection is a pooled object. Separately, the single-encode seam re-tokenizes the novel A-terminator/B-prefix adjacency; the tree's own precedent refuses exactly this where a seam matters (prefill joins at the token-id level, prefill.py:19-24), and the sketch records no seam-canonicality per cell.

    F8 — The build surface is misstated and the utility overclaims: "scoring untouched / size M" is false, and scripted history is not a migrated transcript¶

    core.evaluate renders through the candidate alone (prompt = candidate.render(case.messages), core.py:846), and no Format renders the hybrid (F2), so the probe needs a new evaluation surface — a plan-keyed backend wrapper (the DeliforgeBackend precedent, deliforge.py:492-547), an evaluate variant, or the researcher's carrier-Format route (§3) — plus a payload schema from which the diff validator can recompute each cell, plus configuration-digest binding of pair, recipe, and history source (the cycle-207 obligation, _require_native_match, experiments.py:3940; RT-B F9). None of this is in the sketch's size-M estimate (RT-A F5). On the claim side: whichever population reading is chosen, the sketch's utility sentence describes generated history — a live transcript written by a model under A — while the cheap readings measure frozen scripted strings. Multiturn already settled that scripted and self-generated history are different treatments and must not be conflated (multiturn.py:16-46, including its special-token screening discipline, which foreign-framed genuine history violates by design). Compounding it, for the think-scaffolding families the catalog's own comments record that real templates treat assistant history differently from the lab's flattened Format hypothesis (reasoning preserved, dropped, or required, by family and branch): a field splice measures the lab's Format hypotheses' strings, a scoping the sketch never makes (RT-B F8).

    Non-fatals, adjudicated (what passed)¶

    • Duplication is not a standalone fatal. The residue — genuine history turns, framed in A, preceding a final turn in B, benign content, verdict readout — is narrowly real (verifier §A7 map, upheld): synthhistory owns history presence over prepended turns rendered own-frame; multiturn owns sharded delivery and scripted-vs-self history, always own-frame; faketurn/deliforge own foreign delimiters inside content and the encoding axis; hierarchy owns single-turn privilege position; batch-invariance owns no part of this design but is the standing proof behind F3. The burden the sketch fails is not novelty — it is that the residue is unmeasurable as specified (F1–F8), and that a repaired version must justify itself as a new instrument rather than an arm on synthhistory_stage (RT-A F9).
    • Splice control identity is achievable (F2): three independent executions reproduce B.render(full) under a faithful field splice. Credited to the sketch's instinct, not its text.
    • The single-encode premise is tree-true: backends encode rendered text with add_special_tokens=False (backends.py:1200, :1284), so a concatenated string is a legal prompt input.
    • Budget arithmetic is consistent with the tree's caps (≤24 candidates, ≤100 search fixtures): 3 × 100 × 24 = 7,200 is an honest worst case for the sketch's three conditions; its defect is F1's, not arithmetic.
    • The name is free as a string: --history-frame / history_frame / historyframe return zero hits in the package, docs/USAGE.md, and README.md (all lanes). Whether it overclaims is a resubmission naming-audit question (F8), not a fatal.

    2. Why the repaired instrument is a resubmission, not an amendment¶

    The standing test (payload, answer-syntax, directive, instruction-language, instruction-distance rounds; contrast negation): BUILD-as-amended is available when the sketch's population, arms, and scorer were already frozen and executable before the round, and the corrections fix the decision rule and claim language of that same instrument. Here nothing load-bearing was frozen: the population source is unspecified (F1), the renderer and its seam semantics do not exist (F2), there is no decision rule (F3), the pairs are unnamed and the pair criterion is wrong (F5), the headline population is incoherent (F7), and the scorer cannot score the treatment (F8). The researcher's §6 repairs all of it — by choosing a population mechanism (synthhistory's prepended frozen history on the run's search fixtures), replacing the headline BOS semantics (bos-native headline plus a descriptive verbatim arm, against the sketch's recommended verbatim headline), changing the headline population (render/ids-divergent gated, against token-real gated), and adding a derived decision rule. That is the payload configuration: a new instrument, reviewable only as a resubmission through a fresh blind design review. Any deviation by a future build from this settlement voids it.

    3. Resubmission specification (NOT a build order)¶

    The resubmission candidate is the researcher's §6 (~/workspace/agents_out/historyframe/research.md), incorporated by reference. Its frozen items, in summary:

    1. Treatment (one dimension). The framing strings of the prepended history segment only — per-turn role prefixes, per-role terminators, the segment's internal repeat_bos behaviour — from a Format's fields. Message contents are byte-identical across arms by construction; the final segment, the prompt-initial bos, and the open suffix are always the candidate's own. Token length, seam shape, and marker encoding are consequences, reported per case; the claim subject is the named frozen framing pair.
    2. Population. The run's search fixtures only (≤100), post-hoc stage; held-out never treated; primary decision/winner/exit code never touched. Every search fixture is prepended with synthhistory.build_history_fixtures(cases, condition="history_6") (synthhistory.py:105-131): the frozen benign task-free SYNTHETIC_TURNS, reused, never re-authored. The fixture's own messages are the tail, always framed by the candidate's own family. The claim is scoped to this frozen transcript and says nothing about task-bearing or self-generated histories.
    3. Renderer (frozen formal semantics). A segment function over Format fields with is not None fallback semantics copied from core.py:76-115, global-index repeat_bos cadence following the segment's family, B's bos at the head, A's open suffix never appearing, no separator inserted at the seam. Under A == B it reproduces Format.render exactly (research PoC1: 944/944 renders; arithmetic verifier-confirmed: 118 candidate Formats × 4 message sets × 2 BOS readings).
    4. Evaluation path. One core.evaluate call per (candidate, arm, fixture) through a carrier Format(B.name, "", "", "", "") holding the hybrid text as a single user message (verified this round: the carrier renders its single message verbatim), with B's resolved boundary ids and the fixture's original id in every arm (request-seed identity, core.py:371), following the _canonical_form_cell pattern (experiments.py:9473). The own arm is byte- and verdict-identical to the synthhistory history_6 condition through the new path.
    5. Arms. own (headline baseline) / foreign_chatml / foreign_llama3 (headline contrasts; the pair is string-disjoint against each other and the obvious neighbours, executed) / verbatim_chatml (descriptive only: differs from its headline twin only in the leading bos string, so its discordance against foreign_chatml is the BOS-substitution share). Selection readout: per-arm conservative selection recomputed on the common completed set, withheld on mismatched or empty sets, winner headlines in canonical-form's vocabulary (winner_changed / winner_stable / winner_abstention_asymmetric / winner_undefined); flips against the primary run printed separately and labelled as pooling history presence with frame, never the headline.
    6. Eligibility and divergence accounting. Per (candidate, contrast): render-identical fixtures excluded and counted as n_render_identical (a contrast render-identical on every fixture is inapplicable for that candidate — no classification is emitted, and the inapplicable share is printed, never read as robustness); ids-identical cases likewise excluded and counted, a same-ids verdict flip recorded as a determinism finding; per-contrast id_length_delta list and max_abs_id_delta printed. The encoding stratum is a run-level label per foreign family (per-literal token_real iff backend.encode(literal) yields exactly one id; stratum all_single / all_fragmented / mixed, table printed when mixed), printed beside every classification and compared in diff-reports; it never gates the headline. No checkpoint fact is asserted: all token-real evidence this round is stub-level machinery definition.
    7. Decision rule (derived, with printed characteristics). Per candidate per headline contrast over paired completed render-divergent ids-divergent cases (n paired, a = own-only passes, b = foreign-only passes, d = a + b): n < 10 → insufficient; d = 0 → no_discordance_observed; d < 6 → below_threshold; a == b → discordant_balanced (descriptive only); else two-sided exact McNemar p < 0.05 (confidentiality.py:293 mcnemar_exact_p) → discordant_asymmetric, else below_threshold. The falsification boolean is discordant_asymmetric alone. The d ≥ 6 floor is forced by α = 0.05 (5–0 gives two-sided p = 0.0625; 6–0 gives 0.03125). Operating characteristics (research PoC5, verifier spot-reproduced within Monte Carlo error): asymmetric fire ≤ 3.6% on the random-labeller null, ≤ 3.1% on competent nulls, ≈ 0 under ε = 0.01 jitter, at every N tabulated (16–100); power at a 25-point drop is 0.99 at N = 100 but a coin flip (0.50–0.53) at N ≤ 30 — the exact random-labeller fire rate at the run's own n and the family-wise expectation (≈ 1.44 false labels per 48-decision panel under the random null) are printed beside the classifications, and below_threshold is labelled weak evidence in the section.
    8. Decomposition. Every discordant pair decomposed as boundary_only (answer digests equal), exact_only (digests differ, both boundaries found), or mixed; all three counts printed per contrast. Per-arm attrition (n_context_exceeded, n_evaluation_failed, n_render_identical, n_ids_identical) printed beside every paired rate.
    9. Vocabulary (frozen). Classifications exactly canonical-form's: insufficient, no_discordance_observed, discordant_balanced, discordant_asymmetric, below_threshold. Stage details exactly {cancelled, historyframe_record_budget_exceeded, historyframe_screen_failed} (the last bound to the pre-generation screens: S1 per-candidate control identity — any mismatch fails the whole stage before any generation; S2 frozen-text audit via audit_special_token_literals, fixtures.py:471; S3 budget caps). Per-cell details exactly {"", arm_context_exceeded, arm_evaluation_failed}. Whole-stage failure: {"available": False, "detail": "historyframe_unavailable"}; the stage never fails the run.
    10. Counts. 4 generating arms × ≤100 search fixtures × ≤24 candidates = 9,600 generations legal maximum (headline arms only: 7,200); 2,400 multi-cell ground records; 12 MiB records cap (house pattern). A run's actual candidate count is one family's variants (typically ≤ 8): the legal maximum is a cap, not an expectation.
    11. Records and validator. Digest-only records (hybrid-text digest and token count per arm, divergence flags, id_length_delta, canonical-form-shaped cells). The diff validator re-implements the segment renderer, hybrid, eligibility accounting, classification, and restricted-set selection — never imports them — and recomputes every aggregate. Report text passes through _escape_text (reporting.py:28) only.
    12. Claim (the only falsification-class sentence). "On this candidate, on this run's search fixtures preceded by the frozen synthetic history, rendering that history's turns in the frozen chatml [resp. llama3] framing instead of the candidate's own framing produced verdict discordance at or above this panel's derived floor, asymmetric by an exact McNemar test — a history-frame discordance for these named framings and this frozen history, on this checkpoint." No sentence in any report may generalise it to histories, migrations, or templates in general.
    13. Utility (resubmission, scoped). A team migrating a live product between chat-template families — or importing a transcript one client rendered into a session another serves — uses this, per candidate, to decide whether a carried-over history must be re-rendered into the new family's framing before the final turn is trusted, on their own search fixtures, before a silent history-frame flip changes the template winner they already picked. (The sketch's migration sentence is removed for any scripted-history instrument, §5 item 6; this scoped version is the fresh review's to accept or reject under item 14.)

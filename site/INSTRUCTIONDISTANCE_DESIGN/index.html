    1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects of the candidate as specified¶

    Each defect below is individually blocking and was established by execution (attribution: R = researcher, A = red-team A, B = red-team B, V = verifier, C = coordinator).

    F1 — Attribution: distance, filler presence, and total length are one number on every sketch arm, and the separating control does not exist¶

    By the sketch's own construction, on every same-turn arm distance_tokens == filler_tokens == added_total_length_tokens (adjacent 0/0/0; separated d/d/d — A poc1, V re-ran). Three causal worlds — compliance fails iff between-distance > 0, iff any neutral filler is present anywhere, iff total prompt length exceeds a threshold — predict identical observations [1,1,0,0,0] on all five sketch arms (A; B poc2 executes the length-only vs distance-only version: identical on every arm, diverging only on the missing control). The one arm that separates them — filler at the same dose placed not between constraint and question — does not exist in the sketch. Neither claimed control bounds it: adjacent-after carries no filler (it is an order/recency control only), and the cross-turn arm carries no filler either (it bundles turn boundary + scripted "Noted." history + prior-turn placement). The sketch's conceded mitigation (b) is therefore false by construction. The tree precedents the control twice — repetition.py:66-71 (the pad arm and the length_effect contrast) and density.py:121 (sham in ARM_KEYS) — and docs/DISTRACTION_DESIGN.md exists precisely because neutral filler at these doses is a verdict-moving treatment in this tree. Two further identities ride along (A, B): in the constraint-first layout, distance-to-question and distance-to-generation-point differ only by the constant question length, so "distance" and "recency of the constraint" are affine-identical on these arms; and the sketch never states which side the separated filler sits on (B: separated-before and separated-after have the same distance-between and opposite distance-to-generation — a build would have to invent the load-bearing choice). No discordance this instrument can produce is attributable to distance.

    F2 — The scorer relocates the byte-exact trap, and the promised verdict cannot be derived from the tree's records¶

    (a) The 2×2 is degenerate. The sketch promises a (compliant × correct) readout with correctness through the tree's only correctness axis, byte-exact (core.py:797: exact_match = status == "completed" and answer == case.expected, answer = the pre-boundary span, core.py:939/:966). Executed (B poc1, V re-ran): with a digit-string expected, exact ⇒ byte-equal ⇒ digits-only, so the (correct ∧ non-compliant) cell is structurally empty (0/12 rows), and prose-correct vs prose-wrong are indistinguishable on both axes — no value extractor is specified. DIRECTIVE F1 relocated, not escaped. (b) The grammar is unfrozen. "Digits-only match" states none of: strip policy, sign, leading zeros, decimal, grouping, ASCII vs Unicode digits — each executed to flip verdicts (A poc4, B poc1: byte-exact correct compliant answers "-42", "42.5", "1,234" score digits-only False — 3 of the 4 number shapes the tree itself admits). The tree's standard for a frozen digits grammar exists (confidence.py:91-96: ASCII only, no sign, no leading zeros, the int()/\d traps named) and the sketch meets none of it. (c) Digest-only records cannot carry the verdict. Every sibling generated probe's records carry digests only (negation.py:57: "Records carry digests only"; the needle/placement verdict identity is verdict == (answer_digest == sha256(expected)) and boundary_found). Executed (A poc4, B poc1 §E): compliant-wrong 999 and non-compliant 42 kg are the same record class (digest ≠ expected, boundary found). A digits verdict therefore needs answer text in the records (breaks the digest discipline), a stage-attested boolean (the forgery surface the discipline exists to remove), or a frozen carried primitive (density's word-hash pattern) — the sketch specifies none of the three. structured.classify_response (structured.py:568) does not transfer: it derives adherence from the full decoded text of user-fixture observations, a record shape the generated probes deliberately do not use. (d) The population is silently restricted to plain non-negative integers — perfect word, grouped, and negative answers all score compliance False (B poc1 §F) — a restriction the sketch never states and which makes its "fact question" population a numform-style lookup set, not the RAG population its utility paragraph promises.

    F3 — The borrowed floor, minus the test it was borrowed from, is a noise printer¶

    What density and field-order actually freeze is a conjunction: floor and directional majority and an exact sign test (density.py:1056-1066; fieldorder.py:708-720, floor constant fieldorder.py:99 / density.py:128). The sketch borrows the floor (6) and mentions neither direction, nor the sign test, nor α. Executed under both jitter models (the sketch never states whether discordance is counted per pair or per case-across-arms — the ambiguity is itself part of the defect): the floor-only any-arm rule fires 1.000 on a random labeller under both models (exact anchor: a single pair's P(≥6 discordant of 20) = 0.9793 — the floor sits below the null's expected discordance of 10); on a competent no-effect candidate it fires 0.8912 (A's per-case-across-arms model, exact; ≈21.4 of 24 candidates per panel) and 0.3532 (B's per-pair model, exact DP; ≈8.48 false candidates per panel) — V's independent enumeration confirms both models' numbers. The rule is also direction-blind (A, deterministic): a candidate whose compliance improves with distance "falsifies" identically. 96 unadjusted decisions per panel (24 candidates × 4 separated contrasts), no family-wise expectation anywhere. This is the negation round's borrowed-(6,2) failure at larger scale — that rule at least demanded a joint pattern.

    F4 — Conditioning on adjacent-correct deletes exactly the cases the treatment moves (answer-syntax F2, executed)¶

    Deterministic worlds (A poc7, B poc4 — agreeing): 20 cases, compliance 1.00 when adjacent; at distance the 10 adjacent-wrong-but-compliant cases lose the format too, so unconditional compliance falls 1.00 → 0.50 — a 50-point format-only effect that is the claim's subject — while the conditioned headline (pool = the 10 adjacent-correct cases) reports discordant 0, "identical at every distance". The mirror world hides gains symmetrically. And verdict availability is outcome-dependent (A world C, B §B): a candidate with ≤5 adjacent-correct cases can never reach a floor of 6 inside its conditioned pool, so the weakest candidates — the ones the utility is about — are unfalsifiable by arithmetic. The 2×2 printed beside the headline does not cure this: the falsification claim and its floor operate only on the conditioned slice, so the claim as written ("compliance is identical at every distance") is never tested.

    F5 — The falsifier cannot express "decay", the claim the slate title makes¶

    The rule is a function of pairwise inequality only — no direction, dose-order, or monotonicity term. Executed (A poc3, B poc3 §B; C independently, §4): monotone decay, recovery (compliance improving with distance), a threshold, a U, and a single-dose dip all return the identical verdict (('falsified', 8) under C's deterministic rendering). The tree's own position prior art is non-monotone (needle.py:25-34 credits Lost in the Middle's edge-minus-middle functional form); a design that presupposes monotone decay contradicts it, and this rule could not detect the contradiction if it occurred. Per-dose rates printed descriptively could show a shape; the sketch's headline — its only decision-bearing output — cannot. Blocking for any build carrying the word "decay" or a curve-based layout recommendation.

    F6 — The rungs are not one dimension, the doses are undefined, and the top dose is unmeasurable at defaults¶

    (a) Bundle (DIRECTIVE F3 verbatim). The cross-turn arm's rendered gap between constraint and question is a few dozen framing tokens — executed renders: 67 / 115 / 75 chars for chatml / llama3 / gemma (B poc2; A's independent renders agree) — below the smallest same-turn dose, while adding a turn boundary, an assistant turn, and per-family framing. Ordered by tokens it sits next to adjacent; ordered by turns it is last. The arms swap order by property, so "distance" names a bundle. (b) "F tokens" is undefined. A fixed filler text encodes to wildly different token counts under different tokenizers (A poc5: 200 word-tokens vs 1,140 char-tokens; B poc2: 192 / 959 / 479 under three toy encoders), and a merged token can span the filler/question seam (B, executed). The settlement that makes a dose meaningful exists — needle's: dose as a rendered-token delta over the case's own dose-0 render, per candidate, exact-hit or arm_unmatchable, frozen encode budget (needle.py docstring; distraction.py sizing discipline) — and the sketch adopts none of it: no baseline, no sizing rule, no budget, no seam policy. Here the omission bites harder than in needle: candidates are template families, so a text-level dose is a different dose for every row of the readout.

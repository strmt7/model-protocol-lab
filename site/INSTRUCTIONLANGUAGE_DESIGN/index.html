    Instruction-language probe — design round (ADJUDICATED: NO-BUILD)¶

    Round summary (coordinator #40, design round only — nothing built)¶

    • Tree at main f31e72e1ace4 (cycle 212 closed green, 8,439 tests, precise coverage 95.3455%). This round was READ-ONLY except for this doc and the briefs; no suite was run, no push was made, no tag was cut or discussed.
    • Specialists (blind to each other): researcher (~/workspace/agents_out/instructionlanguage/research.md), red-team A (redteam_a.md), red-team B (redteam_b.md), verifier/claims auditor (verifier.md). All four briefs landed. The verifier resolved every externally checkable claim against primary sources, re-ran every surviving PoC, and recomputed all decision math exactly; the coordinator re-executed the load-bearing claims itself (§4). Fabrication sweep: clean — a first for this program. Every symbol cited by any brief exists at or within a few lines of the cited location; the failures found were one Monte Carlo cell off by ~2.4σ, several unreproducible secondary figures, and sloppy line ranges, all handled in §5.
    • Convergence and divergence, in one paragraph: all three blind briefs return NO-BUILD for the candidate as specified in the circulated sketch (EN vs DE vs FR instructions over a numform fork, 9 frozen strings, answers numeric), and the verifier sustains that unanimously, fatal by fatal, by execution. The researcher additionally specifies a narrowed instrument (add as sole headline task, copy as competence gate, compare excluded, six frozen strings, a new locale-grammar decomposition, a panel-derived (8,5) rule) and recommends BUILD-as-amended; the verifier verifies every load-bearing number in it and rules it a resubmission, not an amendment, under the program's own test. The coordinator concurs: the verdict is NO-BUILD — for the sketch, and as a build order for the narrowed instrument in this round. §3 records the narrowed instrument as the resubmission candidate in full, with the questions a fresh review must settle head-on. The sketch is dead; what survives is a claim about six named strings, and whether that residue is worth the build surface is a judgment the fresh review must make with both red teams' recommendation against prioritising it in front of it.

    1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects of the candidate as specified¶

    Each defect below is individually blocking and was established by execution (attribution: A = red-team A, B = red-team B, V = verifier, C = coordinator).

    F1 — The translation confound: one rendering per language cannot identify a language effect¶

    The sketch freezes exactly one hand-authored string per language per task. Language and rendering are therefore perfectly collinear: a discordance between the EN and DE arms is, with equal support, an effect of the German language, of this translator's lexical choice, of register, of this sentence's length, or of an awkwardness in this sentence. No analysis can separate them, because the rendering never varies within a language. There is no equivalence theorem for translations comparable to canonical-form's UAX #15 basis: NFC and NFD are the same string under a standard that proves it; a translation is "the same instruction" only by stipulation. Negation escaped the analogous trap because its arm meaning was derived (the pair-closed complement rule, NEGATION_DESIGN §2.3); a translation's meaning is only ever stipulated — the payload round's F2 in linguistic dress. Executed (A; C independently, §4): equally valid renderings of the same compare instruction spread 7 chars within German and 7 within French on the coordinator's own strings (A: up to 12 within French), the same order as the EN→DE delta itself (C: +3 to +10). "The DE arm" is a sample of size 1 from a rendering distribution whose variance the design cannot estimate.

    F2 — The compare task has no coherent arm design (the sketch's premise is false for one of its three tasks)¶

    The sketch says "answers stay numeric". Compare's expected answer is the English word first/second (numform.py:597-603, expected_answer returns expected_label), not a number. Both horns of the label dilemma break (A, B, executed):

    • Labels stay English: the DE/FR instruction must demand an English answer word — a code-mixed hybrid no localiser ships; the arm measures instruction-switching, and a locally correct answer (erste) scores False.
    • Labels translate: the scorer's target moves per arm, the frozen gender/ordinal choice is arbitrary and load-bearing (DE erste vs erster; FR premier/deuxième/première — ≥10 mutually byte-distinct defensible DE forms, B), and the FR forms violate the canonical-form scorer pin: deuxième and première are non-ASCII and NFC/NFD-divergent (C, executed, §4). Negation's pin — reply contract byte-identical in every arm, so the answer vocabulary never varies — cannot be satisfied by a language probe at all on this task.

    F3 — The byte-exact scorer × locale number conventions manufacture failures and misattribute them¶

    Executed through the tree's real core.evaluate (core.py:797) with a semantically perfect localising backend (A poc5; C re-ran it, §4): the perfect localiser scores exact_match=False on every non-EN arm (erste, premier, 1.234.568). Worse, the shipped decomposition cannot even describe the failure: numform.parse_value("1.234.568") is None (C, executed), so a numerically correct German-grouped sum decomposes as value_fail — an arithmetic failure — not value_pass_form_fail. The forms axis and the language axis are not orthogonal: numform's comma form 1,234 reads as the decimal 1.234 under German convention (a factor of 1000, B's well-formed version of the point), and numform's space form is byte-identical to French U+202F grouping (render_form(1234567, "space") == "1\u202f234\u202f567", C, executed). Any design retaining comma/space forms across languages measures notation parsing; a plain-only design avoids the collision only by retreating to bare digits (see F6's utility residue).

    F4 — No decision rule exists in the sketch, and the borrowed rule is a noise amplifier¶

    The sketch names no decision rule at all — underspecification at the decision layer (the payload F1 pattern, one level up). The obvious borrowed rule (placement/canonical-form/numform (6,2)) is executed to death three ways: exact enumeration (B; V; C, §4) gives a directional "advantage" fire rate of 0.8598 on a random labeller at n=16 — an expectation of 61.9 fires across 72 decisions (3 pairs × 24 candidates) — and A's weakest-reading rule fires 0.8115 (exact; A's Monte Carlo printed 0.8196) on a competent no-effect candidate. A rule that fires 86% on noise and ~97% on a real 0.90-vs-0.60 gap discriminates almost nothing. Multiplicity is triple the sibling panels' before tasks are even separated, and numform forbids pooling rates across tasks.

    F5 — The arm-equivalence premise is unvalidatable in this tree, and the scope exemption fails¶

    The strings would be LLM-authored. A pinning test proves frozenness, not correctness, idiomaticity, or equivalence. Back-translation by the same model class is circular (A, B, V concur): it measures self-consistency, not fidelity; the tree contains no DE/FR reference artefact of any kind. The premise "these strings say the same thing" is the one premise no agent in the pipeline can check — a design that cannot state how its central premise could be falsified fails the repo's scientific standard. Scope: surface-noise excludes "semantic paraphrase of instructions" (surfacenoise.py:1251, :1403, not_measured lists, V-verified). Negation earned re-entry on derivable ground truth for the variation itself. Translation meets that test only via the payload-residue route — the claim's subject becomes the strings — not via a derivable arm equivalence (A11, sustained by V). A translation is a paraphrase in another language; it inherits the exclusion unless the claim is re-scoped.

    F6 — Bundling, fertility, and the utility residue¶

    Switching the instruction's language moves vocabulary, word order, length, character set, register (a forced Sie/du, vous/tu choice — the researcher's own draft strings drifted register mid-round and only deliberate checking caught it, V §5), typographic convention (FR space-before-colon), and tokenisation together (payload F3 at maximum strength: an entire linguistic system as the treatment). Canonical-form's ID-divergence gating is vacuous here — all pairs diverge by construction — so divergence can only be reported (negation's disposition, which all briefs adopt). Token fertility itself is UNEXECUTED in this round: the tree has no frozen local tokenizer (only backend.encode at run time), A's attempted real-BPE execution failed on a vocab-3 test fixture, B marked its counts UNEXECUTED, and no DE/FR fertility figure may be printed from any doc in this line (§5). What survives F1–F6 is a descriptive discordance between named frozen strings — and both red teams argue, on the Monday-morning test, that the residue does not clear the bar for the full sibling-probe build surface: an operator can already run the existing lab on self-authored German fixtures; the probe's marginal product is the frozen panel plus derived rule plus validator mirror (V downgrades A9 to a standing utility caveat on exactly that ground — every generated probe in this program offers the same marginal product). That judgment is recorded, not settled, here; §3 item 6(d) puts it to the fresh review.

    Non-fatals, adjudicated¶

    • Winner framing. The sketch's framing ("does the template verdict reached in English survive localization") presupposes per-language winner re-selection, which the numform settlement forbids over synthetic sets (experiments.py:11049-11051) and which has no three-arm total extension (A10, sustained). The narrowed instrument drops the winner readout entirely; the framing sentence is retired with the sketch (§5).
    • Naming. --instruction-language is free as a string (zero hits, all briefs' greps concur), but "language" already names language identification in the tree (the classify-language spec, fieldorder.py:258, structured.py:155, answer enum literally english/german/french), and the name asserts the treatment is language while the surviving claim's subject is strings. The mismatch is unresolved by any brief and is a §3 question, not a detail (A12, B §3, V §6).
    • Split contamination. REFUTED as a fatal, conditionally: stage-local generated cases never enter the user's Split (canonical-form/negation invariant). The narrowed instrument states the invariant as binding; a resubmission must too.
    • Role frame. The catalog's templates frame every arm in English role tokens, so even a perfect DE user turn is "German content inside an English protocol frame" (B §10). The claim's scope is user-turn instruction only, and must say so.
    • ID-divergence disposition — settled. Reported, not gated, not conditioned on (unanimous; §3). Same-ids cases are a rendering anomaly; same-ids flips are determinism findings, never language fragility.

    2. Why the narrowed instrument is a resubmission, not an amendment¶

    The negation round's BUILD verdict rested on a stated test: every defect was corrected by amendment to the same instrument — same population, same arms, same scorer, same joint-rule shape — whose population and arms were already frozen and executable. The payload and answer-syntax rounds rest on the complementary test: where amendments change the endpoint set, the population, or the headline scorer, the result is a resubmission and must be reviewed as a new design.

    Measured against that test, the researcher's narrowed instrument changes: the population (numform's three tasks → add as sole headline task, copy demoted to a competence gate/descriptive arm, compare excluded outright); the decision statistic (none in the sketch → an EN-conditioned joint rule with a copy gate, counting only a new parsed decomposition class); and it adds new scorer-side machinery (frozen locale grammars — DE dot-grouping, FR space-grouping — inside a six-class decomposition the sketch did not have), even though the per-cell verdict stays byte-exact and the arms (en/de/fr renderings) survive. The verifier's §6 ruling — resubmission in configuration — is adopted in full.

    The verdict is therefore NO-BUILD for this round. This is explicitly not a finding that the narrowed instrument is unsound: it arrives complete (frozen strings with a recomputed digest, a derived rule whose operating characteristics the verifier recomputed exactly, a frozen decomposition, counts, vocabulary, naming audit), and its numbers all check out (§4). What it has never faced is a blind red team of its own: both red teams attacked the sketch, not this instrument — the same gap that made the answer-syntax amended instrument a resubmission. And three conditions the red teams set for any resubmission are unmet (§3, item 6). The program does not build an instrument on verification alone, however complete the verification is.

No "first" is claimed anywhere; the contribution, if the resubmission is ever built, is the combination stated in research §10 (instruction sentence as sole varying component, language-neutral operands, byte-identical expected answer, chat-template crossing, panel-derived falsification rule), scoped to fetched sources.

    • Researcher poc2's 991-case rows as evidence, and the researcher's poc1 draft register-mixed strings (superseded by the poc5 freeze, digest in §4).
    • Any assertion that a DE/FR string in this round is "natural / idiomatic / register-appropriate" as an established fact — including the researcher's FR-space remark and B's ordinal grammar gloss.     • A winner/selection readout, a DE–FR primary contrast, pooled rates across tasks/languages/candidates, and any canonical-form ID-divergence gating for this probe.

    6. Sources the resubmission may safely cite¶

    All resolved against primary sources this round by the researcher and independently by the verifier (abstract level unless stated; fencing must travel with each):

    • Etxaniz et al., arXiv:2308.01223 — self-translate consistently outperforms direct inference over 5 tasks; models do not leverage their full multilingual potential when prompted in non-English languages. Fencing: treatment is translation of the entire input to English (external MT for translate-test, the model's own few-shot translation for self-translate); the instruction sentence is not isolated, no operand set is held fixed, no chat template is crossed.
    • Shi et al., arXiv:2210.03057 (MGSM) — 250 GSM8K problems manually translated into ten languages; chain-of-thought reasoning evaluation. Fencing: the translated unit is the whole word problem (numbers embedded in prose); manual translation is the fidelity gold standard this instrument is explicitly below.
    • Petrov et al., arXiv:2305.15425 — tokenization lengths for the same text can differ up to 15× across languages (extreme pair; 17 tokenizers). Warrant that tokenization disparity is real; NOT a DE/FR figure.
    • Ahia et al., arXiv:2305.13707 — abstract scope only: OpenAI API cost/utility disparity across 22 languages. No per-language multipliers.
    • Rust et al., arXiv:2012.15613 — a designated monolingual tokenizer plays an equally important role as pretraining data size in controlled comparisons; tokenizer adequacy is a real, separable factor (the warrant for printing fertility as a named rival).     • BIPM/CGPM Resolution 7 substance — in SI writing the comma or dot is only the decimal marker; digit groups are separated by spaces, never by dots or commas. The convention basis for numform's U+202F space form and for the locale-grammar discussion.

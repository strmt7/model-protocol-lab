    Verbalized-confidence × template (run --confidence) — design¶

    Cycle 199 (wave-8 rank #8, the last wave-8 item). This document is the shipped contract. It folds the wave-8 research brief (docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md, wave-8 round), the cycle-199 researcher pass (primary sources re-verified), red-team A's ten findings (F1–F10) and nine amendments (A1–A9), red-team B's disproofs of A's positive amendments (its own amendments B1–B7), and a verifier pass that re-executed both red teams' probes and confirmed every load-bearing claim (V1–V12). Where the brief and the shipped contract differ, this document wins, and the differences are the adjudicated amendments, recorded below.

    What this is, honestly framed¶

    --confidence appends one frozen suffix to each search fixture's last user message, asking the model to end its reply with a line of the form Confidence: <integer 0–100>, and re-evaluates every candidate template on the search split. The elicited text is parsed under a frozen grammar; the stated number is then set against the exact-match verdict the lab already trusts, per candidate: how often the model complies, what it states when it is right vs wrong, and what the suffix itself costs (verdict flips against the recorded native run). A generated pair runs alongside: planted-fact cases whose answer the prompt states (knowable) vs the same cases with the payload binding a different key (donor-key control), testing whether the stated number drops when the answer is not knowable from the prompt.

    Monday-morning utility: a practitioner serving a checkpoint behind a template learns whether the confidence number the model states under that template tracks whether it was right — and whether it drops when the answer is not in the prompt — before exposing that number to anyone downstream.

    A stated confidence is model output text, not a probability, and nothing in this probe turns it into one. The readouts describe agreement between the stated number and the measured verdict under each template. No readout is a calibration error; the words "calibrated", "overconfident", and "ECE" appear nowhere as findings (the verifier confirmed no shipped section uses them). What would change the picture: a stated number that separates correct from incorrect compliant answers and drops on the donor-key control is evidence the number carries answer-dependent signal under this instrument; a number that does neither is evidence it is theatre under this instrument. Both are claims about this checkpoint, this fixture set, this suffix, and this seed — nothing more is claimed.

    Prior art and the scoped uniqueness claim¶

    Verbalized confidence is a studied elicitation method; the lab's contribution is not the method but the combination: (1) per-template candidate crossing — prompt format as the independent variable, the stated number compared across the lab's candidate templates on identical fixtures; (2) the confidence line separated from the scored span under a frozen grammar, so elicited generations are scored by the lab's ordinary exact-match verdict on the span (published studies score generated text with a judge model, or score log-probabilities instead); (3) verdict flips against the recorded native run — the elicitation cost of asking, which no surveyed study reports; (4) a donor-key knowability control — a paired generated control in which nothing false is stated and the asked fact is simply absent; and (5) the result shipped as a fail-closed diff-validated section of the lab's deterministic report, recomputed from digest-bound ground records. No surveyed tool or study ships this combination (researcher pass, cycle 199: lm-evaluation-harness, HELM, Inspect AI, OpenAI Evals, promptfoo, DSPy — Inspect's primitives could assemble something similar, but primitives are not a shipped measurement).

    The neighbours, named so a reviewer does not have to discover them, with the researcher's corrections applied:

    • Kadavath et al., "Language Models (Mostly) Know What They Know" (arXiv:2207.05221): logit-based self-evaluation (P(True)/P(IK)), not verbalized confidence; cited only for the logit line of work, never as a verbalized precedent.
    • Lin et al., "Teaching Models to Express Their Uncertainty in Words" (arXiv:2205.14334): the origin of "verbalized probability", and a fine-tuning result (GPT-3 trained on CalibratedMath). Zero-shot untrained calibration is not shown there and is not claimed here.
    • Tian et al., "Just Ask for Calibration" (arXiv:2305.14975): the method ancestor (answer-then-confidence elicitation), with two differences that forbid numeric comparison: their scale is a float 0.0–1.0, and their correctness is GPT-judged, not exact-match. Their closing open question — how sensitive the method is to the prompt — is part of what a per-template crossing speaks to.
    • Xiong et al., "Can LLMs Express Their Uncertainty?" (arXiv:2306.13063): the lab's exact surface form (integer 0–100, "Answer and Confidence (0-100)") and the documented shape to expect: stated values cluster at multiples of 5 in the 80–100 range, and vanilla elicitation is heavily overconfident by their (judge-scored) measure. Top-bin-dominated, sparse low bins are the pre-registered expected shape here, not a surprise.
    • Seo et al., "ADVICE: Answer-Dependent Verbalized Confidence Estimation" (arXiv:2510.10913): a primary fine-tuning study (an earlier brief miscited it as a survey; the researcher corrected the genre and the verifier confirmed it against the paper). Its §7 answer-masking experiment — replace answer tokens with padding and watch stated confidence — is the nearest published neighbour of this probe's knowability control, and is cited by name for that reason: the donor-key control differs by manipulating the prompt's evidence instead of the model's own answer tokens, and by needing no fine-tuned model. ADVICE's five-format taxonomy includes ScorePercent; the lab's surface form is literature-standard, claimed as such, not as an invention.
    • Damani et al., "Beyond Binary Rewards" (arXiv:2507.16806): RLCR, the RL-with-calibration-rewards training method (verbalized-confidence ECE 0.37 → 0.03 on HotpotQA after training). An earlier brief attributed that number to a different paper (arXiv:2604.26152, a single-author observability survey); the researcher refuted the attribution and the verifier confirmed Damani et al. as the primary source against the full text. Training a model to calibrate is the opposite direction from measuring an untrained checkpoint, and no number from it is comparable to anything this probe prints.
    • Yang, Tsai & Yamada, "On Verbalized Confidence Scores for LLMs" (arXiv:2412.14737): the strongest published prompt-method-sensitivity result for verbalized confidence — reliability "heavily depends on how we ask for it", with method rankings inverting between small and large models. Named here because it is the published reason a per-template crossing is a measurement and not a formality.

    Cross-study numeric comparison to published calibration numbers is invalid and the docs must not invite it: the correctness ground truths differ (exact match here, judge models or human labels elsewhere).

    The instrument¶

    Suffix and surgery. The suffix is a frozen constant (confidence.SUFFIX): "Answer the question, then state your confidence that your answer is correct. Put the confidence on its own final line as a whole number from 0 to 100, in exactly this form:\nConfidence: ". It is appended to the last user message only, as content + "\n\n" + SUFFIX (dateline's joiner and surgery pattern; id, group, and expected are unchanged, so the per-fixture seed is the same as the native run's). The placeholder line in the suffix is deliberately not a grammar line: a model echoing the instruction verbatim is malformed_line, never compliant. Before any generation, the suffix is screened against every candidate's special-token inventory (fixtures.audit_special_token_literals, the repetition precedent): a collision fails the stage closed with confidence_screen_failed, because a framing that turns the suffix text into a special token would forge the elicitation.

    The grammar and the parser. A compliant final line matches ^Confidence: (0|[1-9][0-9]?|100)$ exactly — ASCII digits, no sign, no leading zeros, no surrounding whitespace. The regex is load-bearing, not pedantry (executed in the red-team passes): int() accepts +85, 8_5, 85, and non-ASCII digits, and an unflagged \d matches fullwidth digits, so any strip-and-int parser manufactures compliance the grammar does not grant. The parser is a total function over all strings, pure str (it never encodes, so a lone surrogate cannot raise), with five outcome codes and a final-line-first precedence, adjudicated in cycle 199 over red-team A's count-first alternative:

    • lines come from split("\n") with exactly one trailing empty element dropped (splitlines() is never used — it also splits on U+2028/U+0085 and would manufacture a final line);
    • a grammar-matching final line: ok if it is the only grammar line in the answer, multiple_lines if any earlier line also matches;
    • else a final line starting with Confidence: (exact) or confidence: (case-insensitive): malformed_line — even when an earlier line matched (the model's last act decides);
    • else any grammar line elsewhere: confidence_not_final;
    • else missing_line.

    The span is the answer with the compliant line removed for ok cells, and the whole answer for every other code. A cell's verdict is one definition everywhere: span_digest == expected_digest and boundary_found. Compliance and correctness are separate axes and the joint table keeps them apart: a correct answer that never stated a number is correct and non-compliant; the verdicts recorded on suffixed generations by the raw evaluator are meaningless (the suffix text is part of the answer), which is why every verdict here is re-derived from the span.

    Screens. A fixture is screened out of every elicitation population — its record carries the class and no cell, for every candidate identically — when: its expected answer is blank under strip() (expected_blank; a whitespace-only expected is "correctly" answered by whitespace, the vacuous pole); its expected answer already contains a grammar line anywhere (expected_confidence_shaped; red-team B's executed counterexample showed a final-line-only screen lets an honest generation gain a second grammar line through no act of the model's); its last user message is vacuous (vacuous_last_user; the suffix would be the whole turn, measuring suffix echo, not elicitation — red-team B forced the exclusion over red-team A's keep-or-exclude ambiguity); or it has no user turn (no_user_turn). Screens are computed by the stage and recorded in the protocol; the validator checks protocol/record consistency (see Residue).

    The generated pair. 24 planted-fact cases, derived from the run seed: each binds a synthetic key (key-<8 hex>) to a 6-hex value, pairwise distinct by construction. The knowable arm's payload states the asked key's true binding; the control arm's payload states the next case's key and value. The control is red-team B's donor-key construction, and what it replaced matters: red-team A's original repair replaced the stated value with a false one, and B executed a definitionally payload-faithful backend against it — the faithful model reads the only stated value, answers it at Confidence 95, and the falsification boolean convicts correct behaviour of theatre (95 ≥ 95 fires backwards). Donor-key states nothing false about the asked key; the asked binding is simply absent. Pre-registered expectation: a model whose stated number tracks knowability states a lower number on the control arm; a model confidently answering the donor value there is copying the only value it can see — genuine answer-independence, the ADVICE mechanism, measured from the prompt side. Stated values are unguessable (16⁻⁶) and unmemorizable (seeded hex), so the knowable arm measures reading and the control its absence. The generated cases are instrument cases, labelled as such in the report; they are not operator fixtures.

    The readouts¶

    Per candidate, over the operator population (unscreened search fixtures): completed and compliant counts with the compliance rate; the compliant × correct joint table; mean stated confidence over compliant cells, split by verdict, and their separation gap — descriptive only, printed beside the compliance rate and the joint table, and withheld with a named reason (no_compliant_cells, no_correct_compliant, no_incorrect_compliant) when a class is empty, because the three situations the bare None would conflate are themselves findings. The frozen four-bin table ([0,24] [25,49] [50,74] [75,100], edges in the protocol fingerprint) counts compliant cells per bin with per-bin accuracy (withheld at n = 0). Elicitation cost: verdict flips against the recorded native run, split by direction (pass_to_fail / fail_to_pass), over fixtures with a native verdict and a completed elicited cell.

    Per candidate, over the generated population, each arm gets the same block. Between the arms, the knowability readout: over cases where both arms are compliant, the paired means and falsified = control_mean >= knowable_mean. The boolean is withheld (insufficient_paired_compliant) below 6 paired compliant cases (placement's MIN_PAIRED_EVALUABLE floor precedent: floors gate readouts, never counts). Two scope statements ship with it, in the report text and here: the boolean conditions on joint compliance — it asks only "among cases where the model stated a number both times, did the number drop?" — and a control arm whose compliance collapses is not folded into the boolean; it is reported as its own per-arm compliance count beside it. The word "elicited" never names a population mean in this design (red-team B showed the brief's elicited_mean was ambiguous between operator and generated arms); the boolean compares the two generated arms only.

    Payload and diff validation¶

    Ground records are digest-bound: one record per (candidate, fixture) and one per (candidate, generated case). A cell carries answer_digest, span_digest, boundary_found, code, confidence, final_line, final_line_length, input_tokens, and the derived verdict. Red-team A's original record shape carried the confidence as a bare attested integer, and red-team B executed the forgery: editing only the integers moves the gap +5 → ±100 and flips the falsification boolean while passing every consistency check A proposed, because the residue was the probe's headline axis. The shipped binding (B2) closes it:

    • an ok cell carries its final line verbatim. The line is one of exactly 101 strings and a bijection with the scalar already in the record, so carrying it discloses zero additional information — and the validator re-runs the grammar over it and derives the scalar, rejecting any record whose scalar disagrees. Stage/validator parser divergence and internally inconsistent records die with it.
    • a malformed_line cell carries the first 32 characters of the final line plus the full line length — exactly what re-verifying the class takes (the classification prefix is 11 characters; a grammar line is at most 15).
    • every other code's final line is ordinary answer text and is never carried.
    • digest relations are code-dependent and unconditional: span_digest == answer_digest for every non-ok code, != for every ok cell — with no exception: red-team A's draft excepted the answer that is exactly one grammar line, and B and the verifier both executed the disproof (sha256("") ≠ sha256("Confidence: 50")).
    • scalar typing is strict: type(confidence) is int — a JSON true must not validate as confidence 1 (the tree's F2 discipline).

    The generated case set is regenerated from the protocol seed by the diff validator's own mirror and must match the protocol exactly (ids, key digests, expected digests, both payload digests), so the control arm's construction is bound, not attested. Caps are enforced before any recompute (2,400 operator records, 576 generated records, 12 MiB combined — the combined-bytes measure is the stage's own construction, repeated verbatim by the validator). Every derived value is recomputed from the records by the validator's independent mirror and compared type-strictly; the diff section reports per-candidate compliance/gap/flip drift, knowable and control mean drift, and falsification drift, with protocol or backend drift marking the comparison not comparable.

    Residue, named. What remains stage-attested: that the carried line was the generation's final line and the only grammar line (finality and multiplicity have no textual artefact that may be carried — the final lines of missing_line/confidence_not_final/multiple_lines cells are ordinary answer text); the span split itself; the screen classes (the fixture texts are not in the payload, so screens are checked for protocol/record consistency, not recomputed); and the digest↔text relation every digest-bound probe in the tree shares. The diff validator's standard is the answer-collision standard: everything with a checkable artefact is derived, and what cannot be is named here rather than silently trusted.

    Cost¶

    One full extra search evaluation (2,400 generations at the legal maximum: 24 candidates × 100 fixtures) plus the generated paired control (2 arms × 24 cases × 24 candidates = 1,152) — 3,552 extra generations in total, each on a suffix-lengthened prompt. (Red-team A's draft priced the generated arms as 576 records; generations and records carry different constants — placement.py's own comment — and B's recount, confirmed by the verifier, is the figure above.) No second checkpoint load, no teacher forcing. Defence in depth: the records caps above are also the budget the stage enforces before evaluating (confidence_cell_budget_exceeded).

    Guards and failure vocabulary¶

    The CLI wrapper adopts the choice-audit wrapper gate verbatim (the verifier confirmed the shipped wrappers' conditions): a failed, cancelled, or truncated native search gates the probe to zero forwards with native_search_incomplete. Stage unavailability details: confidence_screen_failed, confidence_cell_budget_exceeded, confidence_records_budget_exceeded, native_search_incomplete, cancelled. Per candidate, a failed evaluation degrades that candidate's cells alone (absent cells, never fabricated ones); a candidate with no completed cell anywhere is listed in unavailable_candidates, a set the validator re-derives exactly. Scope: the search split only — held-out fixtures are never treated; the probe never touches the decision, the selected candidate, or the exit code; and confidence cells are outside verify --determinism scope — the determinism certificate covers the recorded native search observations, and the report section states so in the same three places the multiturn/needle/placement/dateline sections state it (design doc, USAGE, section text).

    Tests¶

    tests/test_cycle199_confidence.py: the parser battery (24 named shapes, including every int()/regex trap and both contested precedence cases); a mutation gate over the parser — eight mutants (sign, leading zeros, non-ASCII digits, any-line, multiple-threshold, splitlines, loose-regex-plus-int, count-first precedence), each killed by a named battery case, with the unmutated control passing the whole battery; screens and surgery; case-generation distinctness/determinism/donor pairing; stage behaviour over scripted backends (faithful, theatrical, per-candidate failure, budget, cancel, suffix screen); a one-break-at-a-time validator tamper battery (19 breaks, including the scalar edit the carried line exists to catch); diff-section behaviour; the HTML section including a hostile candidate name through the C0/surrogate-stripping escape; the CLI wrapper gate; and one tiny-checkpoint end-to-end run. The diff-section blast radius is covered in test_cycle67_diffreports_audit.py (independent oracle trio, a fuzz builder that runs the real stage over scripted backends with hostile mutations and a coherent foreign fingerprint, the _identical_expected key set, and the flip-identical gate anchor extended in both its original and mutant texts — the same pass also repaired two sections the oracle's identical conjunction had silently omitted, placement and precision) and in test_cycle27_diffreports_verify.py (availability oracle recomputing through the producer's aggregation). CLI pins: test_cycle179_cli_dispatch.py (flag + helper-dispatch entries) and test_cycle44_validation_audit.py (source-file count 58 → 59).

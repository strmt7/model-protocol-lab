    1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects¶

    Each defect below is individually blocking. Attribution: R = researcher, A = red-team A, B = red-team B, V = verifier, C = coordinator.

    F1 — The rule's firing condition is mechanism-impure, and its calibration never simulates the instrument's own parser (A (g); C reproduced)¶

    The decision rule counts a bundled slot as not-pass for wrong_value or parse_failed alike (§3.3 of the candidate), so the label multiquestion_drop_observed — under a utility sentence about whether bundling "moves per-question verdicts" — can be fired by formatting events alone, in worlds with zero per-question knowledge difference. Executed:

    • Symmetric per-line slip null (each answer line acquires an extra word with probability s, identical behaviour in both arms; knowledge pass 0.9 given parse): fire 0.023 / 0.062 / 0.194 at s = 0.02 / 0.05 / 0.10, K=5 (A; C reproduced 0.024 / 0.061 / 0.189 with independent code). The mechanism is structural: a slip voids the whole bundled cell (all K slots not-pass) with probability 1−(1−s)^K, while a split cell's slip voids one slot. The candidate's §3.3 sentence — the word rule "applies identically to both arms, so it cannot manufacture discordance by asymmetry" — is executed false at cell level: the code is symmetric, the failure surface is not (V concurs, §6.2 of its brief).
    • Formatting-collapse worlds (bundled generations collapse to preamble/prose with probability q, per-question knowledge identical): the drop label fires 0.11 / 0.28 / 0.64 at q = 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.40 (A; C's whole-case-collapse variant fires 0.054–0.181 at c = 0.10–0.20). Every discordant slot in these worlds is a parse_failed, none a wrong_value.
    • The printed operating characteristics (fire ≤ 0.017 under every simulated null; family-wise ≈ 0.57–0.81 false labels per run) are all verdict-level nulls: none of them simulates the parser the headline runs through. Under the instrument's own mechanisms the family-wise expectation at a 10% per-line slip rate is ~9 false labels per run at K=5, an order of magnitude above the printed figure (A G3).

    The house standard is negation's: its joint rule counts only wrong_label, putting the echo/boundary decompositions inside the firing condition so a non-target mechanism cannot fire the label. Here the decomposition is merely printed beside a label that parse failures alone can fire — and the candidate's own parser section states that a parse-failed slot has "no slot verdict claimed", while the rule counts it as a verdict that moved. The researcher's defence (parse failure is production-faithful: the front-end gets no usable answer) is undercut on its own terms: the frozen parser is stricter than the production behaviour invoked — it voids five correct, correctly labelled answers over a one-line preamble (A G2). Curing F1 requires either a mechanism-pure firing condition (the rule changes) or a re-scoped headline — usable-answer loss under the frozen convention, with parser-in-the-loop nulls as the headline OC (the claim changes). Both are load-bearing elements; under this review's settlement neither may be minted here. F1 stands against the restated instrument as much as against the literal spec: the restatement (§3) keeps parse-failure inside the firing condition by design and its fresh OC grid still contains no parser-in-the-loop null.

    F2 — The headline population is selected by the treatment: liveness conditions on the bundled cell completing (A (d), B §D; unanswered by the restatement)¶

    A case enters the headline iff the bundled cell and all K split cells completed with boundary_found. The bundled cell is the treated generation: a candidate whose bundled behaviour is worst (rambling, restating, boundary loss — behaviours that grow with K) loses exactly those cases, and its decision is computed over the terse residue. Executed magnitudes under a stated correlated-attrition model (30% of cases "hard", true drop 0.6 there, bundled-side boundary loss on hard cases): fire collapses 1849/2000 → 305/2000 at K=3 with insufficient becoming the modal outcome (B §D). Two candidates' decisions are computed over different case sets, selected by each candidate's own bundled behaviour; printing the attrition counts does not unselect the population. This is the same defect class the candidate itself invokes to reject conditioning the headline on parse-ok cells (its §6(c): conditioning on a post-treatment outcome, answer-syntax F2) — applied to liveness, it is nowhere answered, in the spec or in the restatement. The restatement answers the magnitude question honestly (attrition degrades the decision to insufficient, never to a drop label — a manufactured null, not a manufactured drop) but not the selection question.

    Compounding inconsistency (A (d) point 2): a cap-truncated bundled reply and a parse-failed bundled reply are production-identical outcomes — the front-end gets no usable answer from either — yet the first is excluded from the headline as attrition while the second is counted as not-pass. The candidate's production-faithfulness principle is applied to one mechanism and not the other. Every cure re-opens a load-bearing element: counting truncated cells as not-pass revives slot-ordered cap censoring (rank-5 F5) inside the headline; conditioning liveness on anything else changes the population definition.

    What is NOT defective here (conceded by both red teams, C verified): slot-ordered censoring of correct replies is impossible by construction under the single-word screens — the worst legal correct K=5 reply is ≤ 49 bytes (R §2(d), V re-verified the bound), far under the 64-token cap (cli.py:198); the sketch's F5 manufacture does not survive.

    F3 — Two-family pooling cancels inside the case aggregate (B §C; A (c))¶

    The panel pools two families with different failure physics — planted-fact extraction (alphabetic pool words) and small arithmetic (digit strings adjacent to the marker space) — into one per-panel decision, aggregated as d_c = f_c − p_c summed across families within a case before the sign test. Executed (B §C, A's (c) in agreement):

    • An arithmetic-only drop (50% on split-pass arithmetic slots, extraction drop zero) fires the unqualified pooled label in 1982/2000 (K=3) and 2000/2000 (K=5) panels.
    • Opposite family effects cancel: extraction drops 45 points while arithmetic gains comparably → the pooled decision is below_threshold in ~1990/2000 panels at both K. Two large, real, opposite packaging effects are both invisible — not diluted, cancelled — by the aggregation. Density's case-aggregation settlement was derived over one task family; this population has two, and cases differ in family exposure (2F1A vs 1F2A alternating), so a family effect enters d_c with case-varying weight the sign test discards.

    The original spec froze no per-family readout at all (A (c) fatal as written). The restatement mandates per-family f/p, pass rates, and parse decomposition in print, and requires the section text to name a family-concentrated drop — that cures the reading, on the negation per-stratum precedent. It does not cure the decision's cancellation blindness: the label itself can be manufactured by one family and annulled by opposite effects, and no frozen rule states what the section may claim when the families disagree in sign. Per-family decisions would need their own derived rule and family-wise accounting (96 decisions) — a rule change, not a print.

    F4 — The literal spec is not executable as frozen: open screen, non-total grammar, unfrozen generator (A (a)/(b), B §A/§B, V §2.4/§3; C reproduced)¶

    Individually fatal to the candidate as written, each executed by at least three lanes:

    • Marker/sum collision (charter point (g)). Markers are m_j = density.digest_text(str(j)); §3.1 screens fact values away from "1".."5" but places no screen on arithmetic sums. A correct bundled reply whose arithmetic sum lands in {1..K} parse-fails the whole cell while its split cells pass (C: 1: ochre\n2: 3\n3: cobalt → extra_words, all slots parse_failed, f=3, d_c=+3 from one correct case). The breakage is arm-asymmetric and sign-aligned: it manufactures only drop evidence, never gain evidence (V §3.2, incl. the sum=1 case manufacturing f = K−1 via cell voiding). At population scale, a perfect model draws multiquestion_drop_observed in 69% (B) to 79% (V) of K=5 panels under the natural single-digit operand reading — and §3.1 freezes no operand distribution, so the design cannot bound its own false-fire rate within two orders of magnitude (A G6: 0.70 at U{1..9}, 0.00 at U{1..99}).
    • The class system is not total, nor disjoint. Under §3.3's literal class definitions, a segment holding exactly one marker-valued word satisfies none of ok / missing_marker / extra_words (V §2.4 executed the literal predicates; C's independent implementation had to invent the residual clause to classify 1: 1 at all), and the literal extra_words second clause overlaps missing_marker with no precedence frozen. The "total parser" the scorer rests on does not exist in the frozen text.
    • The generator is described, not frozen. K=5 rotation is "analogously" (two honest builders produce different panels; B §E exhibits a 15/9-skewed natural reading); the operand distribution, topic/value draw discipline, and seed/counter derivation are unstated — against the NEGATION standard, where the instrument's generator was shipped, cited code.

    The closures for all of F4 exist, were specified exactly, and were verified by execution this round (V §3.3, R §2(a)/(e)): exclude sums {1,2,3,4,5} in both panels (the researcher's operand floor a,b ∈ [3,99] ⇒ sums ∈ [6,198] is one valid freeze: 0/9,409 grid sums in the marker set; 1,891 generated correct cases parse ok bundled and split, 0 failures, re-run bit-for-bit by V); the residual-class clause (a segment word ∈ M ⇒ extra_words; missing_marker otherwise when the in-order scan fails) making the grammar total and disjoint; the K=5 pattern pair frozen as F,A,F,A,F / A,F,A,F,A by case parity (re-counted: every slot exactly 12F/12A, totals 60/60). The coordinator concurs with the verifier that these are negation-configuration completions of unfrozen parameters, not the ground of this NO-BUILD (§2). They are moot for the verdict because F1–F3 stand against the restated instrument that already contains them; they are recorded frozen in §3 for the next candidate.

    F5 — The candidate's operating-characteristics evidence is materially incomplete in both directions (all lanes; C reproduced)¶

    • §3.4's table reproduces exactly under its stated verdict-level worlds (V 100k trials/cell; A G0; C spot MC: random 0.0157, competent-null 0.0110, independent drop-0.25 power 0.9772/0.9991 — all within seed noise of the printed 0.0169 / 0.0118 / 0.9787 / 0.9993). The arithmetic is honest.
    • Its clustered-drop caveat — "a case-clustered drop is easier for this rule, so the power rows are not overstated" — is false. At the same marginal 25% drop, case-clustered power is 0.4165 (K=3) / 0.3401 (K=5) (V, marginal-matched model), falling to 0.18–0.28 at attrition-reduced N=18–20; under the researcher's collapse-rate-0.25 model it is 0.5803 / 0.5860 at N=24 (R, analytic cross-check 0.5767/0.5778 matching). The two figures differ by drop model; under every executed clustered model, power at the advertised drop is roughly half or less of the printed independent row. The mechanism: the sign test reads only the signs of d_c and the D≥6 gate only the count of discordant cases; clustering concentrates the same marginal drop into fewer cases.
    • No at-N characteristics exist in the candidate as written (the charter's point (b) from BUNDLE_DESIGN §3): power at live N=8 falls to 0.16 (A G4), while §3.3 makes attrition a designed outcome of the instrument. The restatement's fresh grid (§3 of the researcher brief) supplies at-N and clustered rows and is adopted into §3 of this doc as the next candidate's mandatory print — with F1's parser-in-the-loop rows still missing from every grid produced this round except the red teams'.

    F6 — Record binding: the two carried commitments are unbound (A (h); V (d) condition)¶

    The headline quantities recompute from the carried word-hash lists alone (A's attack failed there, conceded; V concurs) — the density discipline's core holds. But the record carries the hash list plus a raw answer_digest, neither derivable from the other, with no commitment_digest over the list — the binding density itself uses (density.commitment_digest) and the candidate claims to follow. Executed consequence (A H1/H2): a record pairing a passing answer's hash list with an all-wrong generation's digest passes every recompute the spec defines, so the byte-exact sensitivity — the reader's only check that the lenient headline is not a formatting artefact — can be made to disagree with the headline undetectably. Additionally id_length_delta, the bundle-disclosure evidence, is stage-attested only: §3.3/§3.5 carry no render-id digests or lengths (negation §3.4 required them carried). Fixable in the record shape; recorded as frozen requirements for the next candidate (§3). Not independently fatal; it compounds F1, whose printed decomposition is the mitigation this binding protects.

    Non-fatal, adjudicated¶

    • Rotation parity-collinearity (A (e), V §6.2): under the two-pattern freeze, family is a deterministic function of (case parity, slot parity); slots of the same parity carry the same family in 24/24 cases. Marginal balance — the design's stated claim — holds exactly (C counted 12/12 per slot per family at both K). The joint-structure residue is real and must be disclosed or designed out (richer pattern set) in the next candidate; it does not defeat the marginal claim.
    • Cap pinning unstated (B §D.3): the candidate does not say whether the probe pins its own generation cap (density pins MAX_NEW_TOKENS=64) or inherits the run's --max-new-tokens (negation inherits). A freeze item for the next candidate, with the ≤49-byte correct-reply bound stated beside it.
    • wrong_value is a new closed-field label (V §1.2.4): no tree occurrence; pattern-consistent with numform's wrong_label lineage and not a decision/detail/stage word. It goes through the build cycle's naming audit; not a vocabulary violation.
    • Class names misdescribe two events (A (b), B, V): out-of-order replies classify missing_marker, and an empty segment classifies extra_words; the printed decomposition inherits the misdescription. Cosmetic, headline-neutral (both are parse_failed), recorded for the next candidate's grammar text.
    • Undisclosed leniency (A via V §6.2): -7 passes for expected 7 (the minus sign is a word separator under normalize_words). Symmetric across arms; a disclosure line for the next candidate's leniency paragraph.

    2. Agenda settlements (the seven chartered points)¶

    (a) Parser grammar in full, incl. the sum/marker screen — OPEN as written (F4); closure specified and verified. The grammar's classes are non-total as written and the screen set is not closed; the verified closure (uniform sum screen {1..5} / operand floor [3,99], residual-class clause, frozen scan convention) is recorded in §3. Silence would have been NO-BUILD; the point was answered by execution on every lane, and the answer is that the frozen text does not contain a total parser.

    (b) Parser behaviour on swaps, omissions, duplicated slots, out-of-order replies — SETTLED. Three lanes' executions agree cell-for-cell (C's 25-case battery included): swaps under ok surface as wrong_value with the swap flag (the other slot's expected hash, well-defined under the distinctness screen); omission (either numbering), duplication, out-of-order, and preamble all void the whole cell to parse_failed with no slot misattribution — the rank-5 F6 positional-parser defect is cured by marker alignment. Marker-valued swaps are invisible, but that is F4's subdomain, closed by F4's screen.

    (c) One population or two strata — NOT SETTLED. The population definition itself is settled (one generated population per panel; extraction and arithmetic are rotating strata of it; the claim is scoped to the mixed panel; genuine recall is a different instrument and is not smuggled in — R §2(c), V §5(c)). The headline's behaviour over the strata is not: F3's cancellation inside d_c means the pooled decision can be annulled by opposite family effects, and no frozen rule governs family sign-disagreement. A print does not settle a decision.

    (d) K=5 cap discipline — NOT SETTLED. The F5 sub-point is settled by construction (correct replies ≤ 49 bytes; whole-case liveness removes slot-ordered censoring; both red teams concede the sketch's manufacture does not survive). The liveness discipline itself is F2: post-treatment population selection with executed magnitudes, an inconsistent truncated-vs-parse-failed discipline, and (as written) no at-N characteristics. The budget asymmetry (bundled 1×64 vs split K×64 across generations) is constitutive and disclosed; it is not the defect — the selection is.

    (e) Family×slot rotation completeness — SETTLED as completed. K=3 was frozen and exact in the candidate; K=5 was "analogously" (open as written). The frozen completion (F,A,F,A,F / A,F,A,F,A by case parity) is verified by counting on three lanes: every slot 12F/12A at both K, panel totals 36/36 (K=3) and 60/60 (K=5), imbalance zero. Parity- collinearity (§1 non-fatals) is the recorded residue.

    (f) No-selection-readout discipline — SETTLED, clean. Red-team A's attack failed by grep + full read; B and V concur; C concurs. No winner, selection, flip-against-primary, cross-K trend, or survival readout exists anywhere in the candidate or the restatement; every readout is per-candidate, per-panel. The numform settlement (experiments.py:11049; repeated at :10018 for negation) is honoured.

    (g) Naming audit for --multi-question — SETTLED, clean. Executed greps (R, B, V, C): multi-question / multiquestion / multi_question occur nowhere in code, USAGE, FAQ, CLI help, or module names — the only tree occurrences are in docs/BUNDLE_DESIGN.md itself. No --question-* flag exists. The sole near-neighbour is --multiturn (cli.py:565, module multiturn.py, USAGE §8.44): a different construct (one task sharded across turns vs K independent questions in one turn), a disclosure obligation for any future build's USAGE text, not a collision. The rejected name's collision is confirmed independently: pack.py:1, format id mpl-bundle/1 (pack.py:49), verify --bundle (verify.py:670-673, flag at :750), USAGE §13.1 (docs/USAGE.md:3320).

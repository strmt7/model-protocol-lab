    Round summary (coordinator #41, design round only — nothing built)¶

    • Tree at main f31e72e1ace4 (cycle 212 closed green, 8,439 tests, precise coverage 95.3455%). This round was READ-ONLY except for this doc and the briefs; no suite was run, no push was made, no tag was cut or discussed.
    • Specialists (blind to each other): researcher (~/workspace/agents_out/directive/research.md), red-team A (redteam_a.md), red-team B (redteam_b.md), verifier/claims auditor (verifier.md). All four briefs landed; the verifier resolved every externally checkable claim against primary sources, re-ran every surviving PoC, and recomputed all decision math with independent code; the coordinator re-executed the load-bearing claims itself (§4).     • Convergence and divergence, in one paragraph: all three blind briefs return NO-BUILD for the candidate as specified in the circulated sketch (three directive arms appended to the last user message, verdicts through core.evaluate unchanged, selection re-applied per arm, falsified by any pass-set difference or selection flip), and the verifier upholds that unanimously, fatal by fatal, by execution — four independent stub backends through the real core.evaluate agree on the central defect.     1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects of the candidate as specified¶

    Each defect below is individually blocking and was established by execution (attribution: R = researcher, A = red-team A, B = red-team B, V = verifier, C = coordinator).

    F1 — The byte-exact scorer × prose arms: compliance is punished, disobedience rewarded (the load-bearing fatal)¶

    core.evaluate has no answer extraction: the scored answer is the whole decoded pre-boundary span (core.py:939 answer_ids = output_ids if cut is None else output_ids[:cut]; core.py:966 answer = decoded), and the verdict is core.py:797: exact_match = status == "completed" and answer == case.expected. A brief/steps-compliant response is by the arm's own demand a strict superstring of the expected answer, so P(pass | full compliance, brief/steps) = 0 by construction. Executed through the real evaluate with stub backends (R poc1, A scorer_stub, B scorer_stub, V scorer_poc — four independent implementations; C re-ran A's, §4): a perfectly compliant model passes brief 0/4 and steps 0/4 — including the most charitable variant, the correct final answer alone on its own final line — while a model that ignores the directives entirely passes 4/4 in every arm. The manufactured share of the arms' discordance equals the candidate's compliance rate, which the instrument never measures. The sketch's conceded mitigation (a boundary-failure taxonomy printed beside flips) does not cover the mode: the compliant failures have boundary_found=True and land in non_label under the tree's decomposition — indistinguishable from genuinely wrong answers. No shipped machinery rescues it: confidence survives its suffix only because the suffix demands a severable grammar line and its verdict is re-derived on the span (confidence.py:400-410 cell_verdict; CONFIDENCE_DESIGN records that raw evaluator verdicts on suffixed generations are "meaningless"), and an explanation is not severable — executed: parse_verbalized_confidence on a brief answer returns the whole text as the span (missing_line), and scorerlens.rung_match recovers a compliant brief/steps answer at no rung 0–4 (the ladder has no label-strip operation, scorerlens.py:53,133). Negation survived this trap by construction: a byte-identical answer-only reply contract in every arm (negation.py:125-137). The sketch's arms have different reply contracts, two of them unsatisfiable under its own scorer. This is rank-4's F3 shape (a perfect XML answer scoring schema_failed 10/10) arriving through the verdict path.

    F2 — There is no decision rule: the any-difference falsifier fires on noise with probability 1¶

    The sketch's falsification is "any candidate whose pass set differs between arms, or a selection flip in any arm" over 7,200 cells. Executed (A, B, V; C independently, §4): under a null of per-cell verdict jitter ε, P(any pass-set difference in the panel) = 1.000 at every ε from 0.001 to 0.05 (analytic E[discordant pairs] = 71.6 at ε=0.005, matching Monte Carlo exactly), and conditioned on a native winner existing — the only case where a selection flip is observable — P(winner loses all-pass status in ≥1 of 3 arms) = 0.951 at ε=0.01 (analytic 1−(1−p)^300, Monte-Carlo confirmed). A rule that fires with certainty under any nonzero jitter is a discordance printer wearing a falsification label (the payload / answer-syntax F2 pattern), and its two headline tests are degenerate in opposite directions: pass-set difference fires always, while at typical pass rates there is usually no native winner to flip at all. Every surviving probe in this tree pairs discordance with a derived floor and printed operating characteristics (negation (8,5), leading (6,2)); the sketch has none, and none can be borrowed — its population is the user's fixtures, whose pass distribution is data-dependent (the borrowed-floor trap of answer-syntax F5 / instruction-language F4; the researcher's poc3 confirms the borrowed (6,2) row fires 0.94–1.00 on this panel's nulls too).

    F3 — There is no ladder: the arms are incomparable bundles, and native is not a rung¶

    By construction from the arm texts (R §2, A F3, B F3, V P5 — unanimous): on demanded pre-answer prose, native = direct (0) < brief (one sentence) < steps (unbounded); on output restrictiveness, steps (none) < brief (one sentence) < direct ("only"); on suffix length, direct (34 chars) < steps (47) < brief (unfrozen, see F4). Brief and steps swap rung order depending on the property measured; direct is a sign flip on prose (forbidding it), not a smaller dose of it; native is a control condition, not rung 0 — under native a model may emit more prose than brief's cap allows. No single named dimension orders the arms, so "ladder" and any rung/trend language attribute a bundle (contract strictness, prose budget, prose function, length) to a dose — payload F3 in miniature. The tree's one real ladder, scorerlens, is a pipeline in which each rung consumes the previous rung's output and monotonicity is a theorem; nothing here is cumulative.

    F4 — The brief arm is not even a frozen string¶

    The sketch quotes direct (34 chars) and steps (47 chars) verbatim and gives brief only as a paraphrase ("a one-sentence explanation, then the final answer"). The three briefs each had to invent a different brief string (64, 60, and 55 chars) — the demonstration itself. An arm that exists only as a paraphrase cannot be audited against the tree's frozen-instrument discipline (special-token screen, length accounting, material digest): the instrument as specified is not buildable without inventing load-bearing text.

    F5 — Token-cap censoring is ordered by arm, and counting it does not fix it¶

    The run's default cap is 64 tokens (cli.py:198), and the recorded native run was generated at that cap. The arms place the answer at treatment-dependent positions — token 0 for native/direct, after all demanded prose for brief/steps — so the effective answer budget under one shared cap is ordered steps < brief < direct = native. Executed (A, V re-run; codepoint stub): a compliant steps output needing 78 tokens before its boundary records answer_len=64, boundary_found=False, status still completed — censored, not measured, and retained in the "common completed" population as an ordinary failure. Raising the cap for the treated arms breaks comparability with the recorded native run, which cannot be re-generated at a new cap; keeping it manufactures steps failures in proportion to explanation length. The verbosity convention the sketch echoes (a capped length is a lower bound, reported as a share) is a descriptive label over recorded runs; here the unobserved quantity is the verdict itself.

    F6 — Conditioning is outcome-dependent, the native arm is asymmetric, and the population scope is unstated¶

    (a) core.evaluate aborts the whole report at the first failed/cancelled cell: executed (A, V re-run), one arm-only backend error (context_limit_exceeded — exactly the error a longer treated input invites) at fixture 1 of 4 yields a failed report with 2/4 observations, aggregate_eligible=False. The "common completed set" after such an error is a prefix selected by the treatment (answer-syntax F2 verbatim), while truncated cells (F5) do the opposite damage and stay in as failures — the population split is decided by backend error mechanics, not by the design. (b) The native arm is the recorded run: held-out fixtures are evaluated in run_experiment only for the selected candidate, so a directive-arm "selection flip" can never be followed through to a held-out verdict for the new winner within the stated budget — the flip readout is a search-only hypothetical and the sketch never labels it so. (c) The sketch says "100 fixtures" without stating search-only scope; every sibling stage is search-only, and silence on population scope in a selection probe is a defect, not a detail — treated held-out fixtures with re-applied selection would be held-out contamination of exactly the kind the Split discipline exists to prevent. (d) The sketch also never states the same-run / configuration-digest obligation for the reused native baseline (the cycle-207 _require_native_match / _configuration_digest machinery, experiments.py:3884 / :3940, exists because a baseline from another configuration was silently compared before) — a spec gap, recorded as such, not an executed defect.

    F7 — The tree's only concrete population already carries the treatment's contradiction¶

    Executed (B fixture_conflicts; C re-ran it and read the file directly, §4): all 4 shipped example fixtures (examples/fixtures.json, 2 search + 2 held-out) carry an answer-only directive in the last user message — "Reply with exactly BLUE and nothing else.", "Now reply with exactly GREEN and nothing else.", "Return only the numeral 7.", "Replace that answer with the single word SOUTH." On such fixtures direct is a near-duplicate paraphrase of the incumbent instruction (treatment ≈ no-op), and brief/steps contradict it: the model must disobey one instruction to obey the other, so discordance measures instruction-conflict resolution — hierarchy's construct — unscreened, uncounted, and varying by fixture. Confidence's screens (confidence.py:225-247) cover blank / confidence-shaped expected answers and vacuous turns, not directive-bearing turns. The shipped example set is small (2 search fixtures), but it is the lab's own fixture style, and any realistic answer-only fixture set is affected in proportion to how well its fixtures are written. No screen is specified.

    F8 — No neutral-suffix control: discordance cannot be attributed to directive content¶

    Appending anything to the last user message changes the token sequence and can flip a greedy path. The sketch's only contrast is native vs directive-suffix, so its discordance pools (a) directive semantics, (b) suffix presence/length (+34/+47/+60 chars), and (c) recency effects. The tree already treats the control as mandatory: repetition.py ships a pad arm matched per (candidate, fixture) on rendered token length precisely so the repetition effect is separable from the length effect (repetition.py:66-71: ARMS ("x1", "x2", "pad"), CONTRASTS length_effect / repetition_effect). Without a length-matched neutral arm, even a scorer-fixed headline could not say the directive did it.

    F9 — Single-phrasing collinearity kills the class-level claim (instruction-language F1 pattern)¶

    One frozen string per directive makes treatment and rendering perfectly collinear: every discordance is an effect of these strings, with this joiner (which the sketch does not even specify). The claim that survives scoping — "appending these exact strings changed these candidates' pass sets on these fixtures in this run" — does not clear the Monday-morning bar the sketch sets itself (a prompt engineer choosing a directive for their prompt learns nothing about step-by-step directives as a class, and nothing about whether steps improves the answer, the decision-relevant quantity F1 makes unmeasurable). String scoping is the negation/instruction-language settlement and the resubmission adopts it (§3); the sketch's claim is written at class level, and at class level it is false advertising.

    Non-fatal, adjudicated (the sketch's defence, and what passed)¶

    • Duplication. The brainstorm's defence — "confidence never re-applies selection per arm" — is literally true of confidence (V §1 S7: confidence_stage copies primary_decision / primary_winner from the native result) and refuted as a novelty claim about the tree: prefill re-applies selection per condition (experiments.py:2585 prefill_adherence; prefill's pre-registered claim 1 is a winner-under-condition contrast), canonical-form re-applies it per arm over a restricted shared population (canonicalform.py:599 arm_selection, :641 winner_headline), and confidence already ships the elicitation-cost flips readout (pass_to_fail / fail_to_pass vs the recorded native run). Surgery, native reuse, flips, and per-arm selection are all shipped measurements; the only unshipped element is the treatment strings — and F1 makes the treatment unmeasurable for two of its three arms. Sustained against the defence; not independently fatal to a future instrument whose novelty is argued on treatment alone (researcher §9 concedes exactly this, and §3 item 6(e) puts the judgment to the fresh review).
    • Selection re-application on user fixtures is legitimate (R §4, upheld by V as dispute D4): the numform settlement (experiments.py:11049-11051) bars winners over synthetic sets; this population is the user's own search fixtures — the same contest — with prefill/canonical-form as shipped precedents, under the conditions of §3 item 2 (restricted-set recompute, search-only labelling, primary decision untouched).
    • Passed checks (not findings): the suffix lands inside the last user turn for every catalog family (executed renders, chatml/llama3 checked explicitly; Format.render, core.py:76); the special-token audit over all 29 catalog candidates returns 0 findings for all three suffix strings; per-fixture seeds are id-derived (core.py:371 request_seed_for), so id-preserving surgery keeps arm seeds identical to native — an obligation the design must pin (the prefill id-prefixing pattern would silently break it), not a defect found; the 7,200-generation arithmetic is consistent with the tree's caps (24 candidates × 100 search fixtures, determinism.py:32-38, experiments.py:4014).
    • Naming. directive has zero hits in the package, docs/USAGE.md, README.md, and docs/FAQ.md (R, V, and C greps concur); the only hits anywhere are prose "owner directive" in docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md:3814, :5596, :5755. --directive is therefore free as a string but over-broad as a name (it names any instruction — hierarchy's whole territory), the payload §8.46 failure shape; the resubmission proposes --response-directive (§3 item 5).

    2. Why the redesigned instrument is a resubmission, not an amendment¶

    The negation round's BUILD verdict rested on a stated test: every defect was corrected by amendment to the same instrument — same population, same arms, same scorer, same joint-rule shape — whose population and arms were already frozen and executable before the round began. The payload and answer-syntax rounds rest on the complementary test: where the amendments replace an undefined or unexecutable instrument with a different one, the result is a resubmission and must be reviewed as a new design before it is built.

    Measured against that test, the researcher's redesigned instrument changes: the arms (new texts carrying a frozen final-line contract; the panel is categorical and the "ladder" framing is dropped); the headline scorer (span verdict — final line equals expected, conjoined with boundary — with byte-exact demoted to a beside-the-headline elicitation-cost readout); the population discipline (fixture screens, the restricted common-completed-set selection recompute, search-only labelling); the decision rule (none in the sketch → a derived joint rule, §3 item 3); and the name (--directive → --response-directive). The sketch's instrument was never executable as a measurement in the first place: its brief arm has no string (F4), its scorer inverts its headline (F1), and its falsifier is a tautology (F2). This is the payload configuration, not the negation one.

    The verdict is therefore NO-BUILD for this round. This is explicitly not a finding that the redesigned instrument is unsound: the verifier reproduced every one of its operating characteristics by independent exact enumeration (and the coordinator re-ran the enumeration, §4), it answers F1, F2, F3, and F9 by construction rather than by disclosure, and its author states its central risk himself (§3 item 6(e)). What it has never faced is a blind red team of its own: its novel machinery — the span parser over prose arms, the frozen decomposition classes, the per-run-N operating-characteristics printing, the not-yet-designed directive screen of F7, the not-yet-settled budget discipline of F5 — was claim-verified and math-verified in this round, not adversarially attacked. The program does not build an instrument on verification alone.

    Two alternative residues were also recorded by the red teams and are noted here so the fresh review can weigh them against the main candidate, not silently lose them: (a) red-team B's directive-phrasing probe — every arm demands the same answer-only reply contract under different frozen phrasings, plus a length-matched neutral control, scoreable under core.evaluate unchanged (a different, smaller instrument measuring phrasing sensitivity, not production-style effects); (b) red-team A's residue — a confidence-style descriptive flip readout for the direct arm alone, the only arm whose compliant behaviour the byte-exact scorer can pass, as an extension of an existing instrument family rather than a new probe.

    3. Resubmission specification (NOT a build order)¶

    A resubmission is reviewable as a NEW design only if it satisfies all of the following in full. Partial compliance is still NO-BUILD. The researcher's redesigned instrument (~/workspace/agents_out/directive/research.md §5–§11) is the resubmission candidate and is incorporated by reference, subject to items 1–5 below; the fresh review's job is to attack the frozen machinery itself (the arm texts, the span parser, the decomposition, the screens, the rule at realistic N), not to re-litigate the sketch, which is dead.

    1. Corrections applied before review. Tree line ranges as corrected in §5 (JOINER confidence.py:78; parser def confidence.py:177, body to :205; SUFFIX confidence.py:85-89; answer span core.py:939 / :966); the first author of arXiv:2511.04108 is Srivastava (Saurabh Srivastava et al.; no author named Qiu appears in the current arXiv record) and the paper is dropped as load-bearing in any case (§5); the RESEARCH_LOG naming claim is three prose hits (:3814, :5596, :5755), not two; the rule table's "0.0000" per-decision rate at N=100 is ≈2.4e-5 and must never be quoted as zero.
    2. Frozen items that carry verbatim in substance: the population (the run's search fixtures only, post-hoc stage, native arm = the recorded run never re-executed, held-out never treated; the selection-flip readout labelled search-only wherever it appears); the arms (terse / brief / steps with the researcher's §5 frozen texts and the "\n\n" joiner, every prose arm mandating the final answer on its own final line; special-token screen over every suffix for every family before generation); the headline scorer (span verdict) with the byte-exact verdict printed beside it as elicitation cost, never conflated; the frozen decomposition (span_pass / answer_line_not_final / answer_embedded / answer_absent, boundary-only discordance decomposed out first, preanswer_present counts printed); the decision rule of item 3; the selection readout (canonical-form winner_headline transplanted per two-arm contrast, selections recomputed on the restricted common completed set, withheld when sets differ or are empty, selection-vs-abstention never a winner change, the primary run's decision/winner/exit code never touched); the fail-closed vocabulary of research §8 (stage details {cancelled, directive_record_budget_exceeded, directive_screen_failed}, per-cell {"", arm_context_exceeded, arm_evaluation_failed}, whole-stage directive_unavailable, section labels directive_drop_observed / below_threshold / insufficient); token-ID divergence reported, not gated (negation disposition; same-ids = rendering anomaly, same-ids flip = determinism finding); digest-only records with the span split named as stage-attested residue (no carried final line — it is the answer); the validator mirror obligation (suffixes, joiner, screens, span parser, decomposition, rule, restricted-set selection — re-implemented, never imported).
    3. Decision rule (as derived in research §7, verified twice independently): per candidate, per arm-vs-native contrast, over the common completed set — D = native passes, J = of D, arm span-verdict fails, R = reverse discordants printed beside J; fire directive_drop_observed iff D ≥ 10 and J ≥ 5 and J ≥ D/4 and McNemar exact one-sided p(J vs J+R) < 0.05; D < 10 → insufficient; otherwise below_threshold. Operating characteristics (exact enumeration; researcher, verifier, and coordinator agree cell-for-cell): random labeller fires 0.0377 (N=100) / 0.0304 (N=30); no-effect competent rows 0.0000–0.0071; power at a true 25% drop is 0.5027 (N=100) / 0.3441 (N=30) — a coin flip; at a 50% drop, 1.0000 / 0.9490. Because the panel size is the user's own fixture count, the section must print the operating characteristics at the run's own N (no single frozen paragraph), and the family-wise expectation with them (at N=30, ≈0.51 expected false labels per 72 decisions, P(≥1) ≈ 0.40; at N=100, P(≥1) = 0.002). below_threshold is weak evidence and must say so; small user sets (the shipped example has 2 search fixtures) read insufficient — correctly.
    4. Monday-morning utility (resubmission, string-scoped): a prompt engineer who has picked a template family and is about to ship a response-style directive in the user turn uses this, per candidate, to decide whether that exact directive string can ride along without changing the lab's winner on their own fixtures — before the choice silently flips a selection they already made. Where the verdict is insufficient or below_threshold, the honest deliverable is "this panel cannot distinguish them; choose on other grounds", and the design must say so in those words' substance.
    5. Name: --response-directive (module responsedirective.py), per research §10's in-design audit — --directive is free as a string but names any instruction and overclaims the scope; "ladder" is dropped entirely. The name ships only after the build cycle's USAGE naming audit (the rank-2 §8.46 precedent) regardless.
    6. What the fresh review must settle head-on (silence on any of these is NO-BUILD): a. Budget discipline at the recorded cap (F5). At the recorded --max-new-tokens the steps arm's span verdict still grades a censored population (truncated cells are completed, answer never emitted). The resubmission's per-arm n_limit counting and decomposition label the censoring; the fresh review must decide whether labelling suffices, whether truncated cells leave the headline population under a pre-registered rule (and what that does to the conditioning, F6), or whether the instrument needs a different budget regime — argued against the native comparability constraint, not defaulted. b. The directive-bearing-fixture screen (F7). A frozen, decidable screen for fixtures whose messages already carry an answer-style directive (in any turn, not only the last — the shipped example carries it in the last turn, but nothing in the fixture format confines it there), with screened fixtures counted and classed pre-generation, never silently dropped. The researcher's §4 screen list (multiline / blank / vacuous / no-user) does not include this class; without it the span verdict on such fixtures measures conflict resolution, not directive sensitivity. c. The neutral-suffix control (F8). Include a length-matched neutral arm (the repetition.py pad precedent), or argue — against F8, not around it — why the span-verdict design attributes discordance to directive content without one. Suffix-presence is a real channel: any appended text can flip a greedy path. d. Panel power at realistic N (F2/item 3). The rule is coin-flip powered at a 25% drop even at N=100, and most real user sets are far smaller. The fresh review must either accept this with the at-N operating characteristics printed beside every verdict, or change the panel/rule — and argue the choice, not default to it. e. The confidence-overlap judgment. Machinery reuse from confidence is total (surgery, span verdict, flips, wrapper gate); the treatment difference is the whole claim to novelty (research §9, conceded by its author). The fresh review decides whether that difference justifies a new probe, an extension of confidence, or nothing — with red-team B's phrasing variant and red-team A's direct-arm residue (§2) in front of it as the competing smaller instruments. f. Neighbour re-survey. The uniqueness pass's neighbour set (LM Eval Harness, HELM, PromptPerfect, OpenAI evals, DSPy) was NOT re-surveyed this round (research §11, UNEXECUTED; the cycle-199 survey covers the confidence combination). The fresh review re-runs it before any build; the claim stays combination-scoped to surveyed sources until then.
    7. Counts (as frozen in the brief): 3 treated arms × ≤100 search fixtures × ≤24 candidates = 7,200 generations maximum (native reused, 0); ground records = 24 × 100 = 2,400 multi-cell records; caps frozen in the module with derivations (confidence's 2,400-record precedent). The 7,200 figure must never be quoted without its search-only and recorded-cap qualifications (§5).
    8. Sources are limited to §6 of this doc, with their fencing. No "first" is claimed anywhere; the contribution, if the resubmission is ever built, is the combination stated in research §11 (per-template crossing of frozen response-directive strings on the user's own fixtures, span-verdict discordance plus byte-exact elicitation cost, conservative selection re-applied per arm on a common completed set), scoped to surveyed sources.

    Confusable-skeleton substitution — design (ADJUDICATED: BUILD-as-amended)¶

    Round summary (coordinator #74, wave-12 design round 2, 2026-10-30)¶

    Verdict: BUILD-as-amended. The candidate's three load-bearing structures survived adversarial execution: the completion guard — transferred from the invisible-chars round with its amendments folded in — was re-attacked with an independent engine and the coordinator's own state-space enumeration (zero manufactured fires, zero unstable acquittals over the reachable (D, J, k) space); the population is real — the frozen 16-letter table, the zero-eligible audit, and skeleton invariance reproduce under three independent implementations of UTS #39 §4 against the pinned 15.0.0 data file, byte-identical (SHA-256) to the publisher's; and the exact operating characteristics reproduce digit-for-digit against the invisible-chars round's adjudicated marginal grid, as they must for the shared rule shape.

    What failed is the freeze, in this tree's ordinary classes, plus one aggravated delivery defect: the delivered candidate file was damaged — a 17,429-character span of its §2 was missing, replaced by a literal truncation marker, and with it the entire textual definition of the rarity-control arm; the executed rarity assignment then contradicted the surviving text's script-matching rule through a code-point range proxy; the rarity arm's form_only class was undecidable as written; the paired column's Monte Carlo understated the exact paired price; the cap's printed state count ranged over unreachable states; and §8's isolation reading extended a dose-matched argument to an arm whose dose it does not match. Every defect is repaired by the amendments below, which leave the decision rule, the floors, the panel, the population rule, the headline scorer, and the §7 claim's falsification condition untouched. This consolidated doc — not the damaged candidate file — is the build's governing text.

    The amendments (folded into the text at the sections named)¶

    • A1 (§2). This doc contains the rarity definition in full: controls U+0434 CYRILLIC SMALL LETTER DE (д) for the Cyrillic seat and U+03B6 GREEK SMALL LETTER ZETA (ζ) for the Greek seat; assignment per treated word by the script of that word's single_site frozen replacement, script determined by the unicodedata.name prefix (CYRILLIC / GREEK); the per-letter control table printed beside the frozen replacement table. The build verifies the definition's presence in this doc with a named test — the candidate file's damage is the reason the check exists.
    • A2 (§2, §3). The code-point range proxy is replaced by the name-prefix rule in the generator; the rarity populations are regenerated under the corrected assignment (they differ from the design-phase scripts' rarity contents on 6 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 cases per seed — the corrected populations are the frozen ones); the build's freeze tests assert, per treated site, that the control's script (by name prefix) equals the frozen replacement's script.
    • A3 (§5). The rarity arm's form_only is pinned position-aware: the answer has the same code-point length as the expected value, contains at least one control-set character, and replacing every control-set character by the expected value's character at the same position yields exactly the expected value. The grammar battery pins the witness дlder-1234 (vs alder-1234) to form_only.
    • A4 (§6). The cap figure is 264 completion-stable fire states suppressed at k > 6, over the reachable state space D + k ≤ 24; the audit's bound is stated in the text.
    • A5 (§8). The skeleton-specific isolation reading is licensed for single_site (the dose-matched arm). For whole_word, a solo fire reads: "skeleton-class substitution at whole-word dose disrupts this candidate; rarity at that dose is unmeasured by this design" — printed wherever the isolation reading is stated.
    • A6 (§6). The paired column prints the exact bivariate values — competent null 0.0044 (0.004442), drop-25 0.6981 (0.698078) — and the family-wise paired row is computed from the exact rate: E = 0.3199, P(≥ 1) = 0.2743 over 72 comparisons. The design-phase Monte Carlo (0.0036 / 0.6918) was a faithful execution of its stated model but understates the exact price; it is not printed.
    • A7 (§2). The control property is restated as the one relied on: each control is not a source in the pinned file, its skeleton is itself, and it is skeleton-distinct from every panel letter and every frozen replacement — with ζ's six-member skeleton class (itself + five mathematical variants U+1D6C7, U+1D701, U+1D73B, U+1D775, U+1D7AF) disclosed. The build's population verification asserts exactly these properties (not absence from the file).
    • A8 (§5, §6, §7). (i) §6's worlds bullet carries the caveat: design-phase evidence ranged over all incomplete cases (no ids dimension — the conservative direction); the build's guard battery re-executes the worlds under the pinned domain. (ii) The completion sentence discloses that the (D+1, J+1) counting is a blanket over-count ranging over observation-inconsistent completions for partially-observed cases, deliberately conservative; the computation is unchanged. (iii) §7's claim sentence gains "under the §6 guard". (iv) §5's form_only gloss states the frozen-table scope; echoes in non-frozen skeleton-equal letters decompose as non_answer, disclosed, never counted toward J. (v) The MIN_D plateau sentence gains its exactness qualifier (identical at printed precision over [1, 14]; exact plateau [1, 6]; the selected pair trails the exact maximum by < 3e-11).
    • A9 (§3, §13). The build's generator freeze tests include a constructed colliding draw that forces the redraw path, pinning: slots 0+1 redraw at counter+1, neutrals remain at counter 0, and the give-up bound's counting as the module implements it. The build's OC script reproduces every §6 figure from its own code, including the exact paired values and the N-sweep.

    Framing, stated once and binding on every section: the honest claim is "skeleton-class substitution" — substitution inside a standards-defined equivalence class (UTS #39 §4 skeleton equality). The model never sees glyphs. Nothing in this design claims a model is "fooled like a human", and uniqueness is claimed for the combination only, never "first".

    The design as amended¶

    1. Monday-morning utility¶

    An eval owner deciding, Monday morning, whether frozen benchmark fixtures and ingested documents need a confusable-screening pass (skeleton-based detection at ingest) gets, per candidate (template family) and per substitution arm, a number: how often replacing Latin letters with their UTS #39 skeleton-equivalent Cyrillic/Greek letters in ordinary generated fact-recall text changes what a local model answers. If skeleton-class substitution never moves answers at the frozen floors, the screening pass is deprioritised for that family; if it fires, the pass — which is cheap and deterministic, unlike a sanitizer whose cost the sibling design had to scope away — has a measured reason to exist.

    Scope: the probe measures the effect of the substitution, not the accuracy of any particular screening implementation, and not human visual confusability. Both exclusions are in the §7 claim's wording.

    2. The population definition for "confusable" (the novel point)¶

    Version pin — decided and justified. The population is derived from confusables.txt Version 15.0.0 (downloaded from unicode.org/Public/15.0.0; SHA-256 2b10130885c3370b101c52d7baedc452ab7f0e257b86c1e52ee657ecfc29ce64), with the DICP set of skeleton step 2 taken from DerivedCoreProperties.txt 15.0.0 (SHA-256 d367290bc0867e6b484c68370530bdd1a08b6b32404601b8c7accaf83e05628d; executed DICP set size 4,174 code points). Reasons, in order of weight:

    1. Same-version coherence. The interpreter that runs the build has unicodedata.unidata_version = 15.0.0 (Python 3.12.3). Skeleton steps 1/4 (NFD), the script classification below (character names), and categories all come from that local UCD. Pinning the confusables data to the same version makes the whole derivation single-version; a 15.1.0 or 18.x data file against a 15.0.0 NFD/name table is a mixed-version population no single standard defines.
    2. The cited text and the data are different artefacts. UTS #39 rev 27 (Version 15.1.0 draft 5, Proposed Update, 2023-08-07) is cited for the algorithm — §4's four steps. The algorithm text is what the design relies on from rev 27; the data the algorithm consumes is pinned separately, to 15.0.0, per reason 1. §4 of rev 27 itself warns "The data may change between versions" — skeletons are not version-stable, which is exactly why the pin must be stated and why a future Unicode bump is a population change requiring re-derivation, not a silent upgrade.
    3. Latest (18.0.0) rejected: it would maximise reason 1's incoherence for zero design benefit — no arm, floor, or claim depends on post-15.0 additions.
    4. Sibling precedent: the invisible-chars design pinned its property claims to UCD 15.0.0 for the same interpreter reason (adjudicated sound).

    Runtime posture (build clarification, cycle 224). The derivation above happened once, at design time, under UCD 15.0.0; the shipped module carries the population as frozen literal tables and file digests — it does not re-derive anything from the host's UCD at runtime. The runtime audit records the host's unicodedata.unidata_version as read and checks the frozen characters' names, categories and normalization forms, every one of which was assigned long before Unicode 14.0 and is unchanged in 14.0, 15.0 and 15.1; the audit's verdicts are therefore the same on every supported interpreter, and only the recorded version string differs. A future Unicode version that changed one of those frozen characters' properties would be a population change under reason 2, caught by the audit going red.

    The skeleton function (UTS #39 rev 27 §4, verbatim): skeleton(X) = (1) NFD; (2) remove Default_Ignorable_Code_Point characters; (3) concatenate per-character prototypes from the pinned confusables data (single application — §4: mappings are idempotent, implementations "do not have to recursively apply" them); (4) NFD. X ≅ Y iff skeleton(X) = skeleton(Y). Executed checks of the standard against itself: §4's own examples verify — skeleton("paypal") = skeleton("pаypаl") (mixed-script, U+0430) and skeleton("scope") = skeleton("ѕсоре") (whole-script) are both True under the pinned 15.0.0 data. Data-file shape, executed: 6,311 entries; every source is a single code point (multi-code-point sources = 0) — so the single-code-point-source restriction below excludes nothing from the file; the restriction that bites is on the target side and the script rule.

    Eligibility rule (frozen). A code point ch is an eligible replacement for a panel letter L iff all of:

    • skeleton(ch) == skeleton(L) under the pinned data (normative membership — not visual judgement; see the r/y rows below);
    • unicodedata.name(ch) starts with CYRILLIC SMALL LETTER or GREEK SMALL LETTER (the slate's cross-script class — UTS #39's own worked examples are Cyrillic/Greek; category is Ll by construction of the name, verified in the audit output);
    • NFC(ch) == NFD(ch) == ch (no canonical decomposition — the canonical-form boundary, below);
    • NFKC(ch) == ch (not a compatibility fold — the candidate-3 boundary, below).

    Freeze rule: one replacement per letter = the smallest code point satisfying the rule. Deterministic, reproducible from the pinned file alone.

    Executed audit over the whole panel alphabet. The 24 topics + 24 WORDS use 25 distinct letters (a–z minus x). For each: skeleton-equal sources in the pinned file range from 13 (k, t) to 75 (o) — raw skeleton equality admits digits, capitals, math alphanumerics, symbols, and a dozen scripts.     • Skeleton invariance, proved: for every topic and value word, skeleton(treated) == skeleton(original) under both substitution arms — 48 words × 2 arms, 0 failures (rarity arm: skeleton differs, also executed — §4). Code-point length is preserved by substitution (asserted per word).

    The rarity control — the definition in full (amendment A1). The rarity arm isolates rarity from skeleton class: at exactly the single_site sites, it substitutes a same-script, non-confusable letter instead of the frozen confusable. There are exactly two control characters, one per script seat:

    • U+0434 CYRILLIC SMALL LETTER DE (д) — the control for the Cyrillic seat;
    • U+03B6 GREEK SMALL LETTER ZETA (ζ) — the control for the Greek seat.

    Assignment rule (amendment A2): the control for a treated word is chosen per word, by the script of that word's single_site frozen replacement — that is, by the script of the frozen replacement of the word's first eligible letter. Script is determined by the unicodedata.name prefix (CYRILLIC / GREEK) — §2's own classification method, applied to the frozen replacement character. (The design-phase generator's code-point range proxy — U+0400–U+04FF ⇒ Cyrillic — is wrong and is not the rule: the Cyrillic Supplement replacements ԁ U+0501 and ԛ U+051B fall outside that range and must take the Cyrillic control д, not ζ. The corrected assignment is the frozen one.)

    How the controls were chosen (executed search): per script, the smallest code point in the script's small-letter range whose name carries the script's … SMALL LETTER prefix, that is not a source in the pinned file (its prototype is itself), is category Ll, encodes to exactly 2 UTF-8 bytes (matching every frozen replacement's byte length, so the rarity arm's byte-length delta equals the confusable arms'), and satisfies NFC == NFD == NFKC == identity, with skeleton(control) distinct from the skeleton of every panel letter. The Cyrillic search lands on U+0434; the Greek search on U+03B6.

    The property relied on (amendment A7), stated exactly: each control (i) is not a source in the pinned file — no entry maps from it, so its prototype is itself; (ii) its skeleton is itself (skeleton(д) = д, skeleton(ζ) = ζ, executed); and (iii) it is skeleton-distinct from every panel letter and every frozen replacement — so a rarity-treated word's skeleton differs from the original's (executed), which is the arm's whole point. Two disclosures the property does not hide: U+0434 is wholly absent from the pinned file (neither source nor target — verified), while U+03B6 is the target of five entries — the mathematical zeta variants U+1D6C7, U+1D701, U+1D73B, U+1D775, U+1D7AF — so ζ is the prototype of a six-member skeleton class under this design's own definition of the relation. The class mates are mathematical alphanumerics, in no arm and no panel word; the operative separations (i)–(iii) all hold and are executed. The build's population verification asserts exactly (i)–(iii) and the stdlib-decidable parts of the choice rule — never bare absence from the file, which is false for ζ as a target. An earlier draft's summary ("the controls are non-confusable") is not the claim and must not be repeated: the claim is (i)–(iii).

    What the control matches, and what it does not (§9 R2): the control matches the frozen replacement on script, UTF-8 byte length (2 bytes), category (Ll), and the (i)–(iii) separation properties — all checkable, all checked. It does not match corpus frequency or tokenizer merge rank, quantities this tree does not measure; the residual frequency confound is disclosed in §9, not claimed away.

    The sequence question, executed. All 6,311 pinned sources are single code points, but targets are not all single: the file maps U+006D (m) to the two-character prototype "rn", and executed skeleton("rn") == skeleton("m") == "rn" is True — sequence-level skeleton equivalence exists inside the normative class. Design consequence: v1's population is single-code-point → single-letter substitution only, so rn→m / m→rn sequence substitutions are outside this probe's population (they change code-point count — a length-changing mechanism, a different dose axis). Stated here so no reader infers the probe covers the full skeleton equivalence relation: it covers its single-letter, cross-script (Cyrillic/Greek) slice, frozen above.

    Territory boundaries — each verified by execution:

    • (a) vs canonical-form (§8.54): every frozen replacement and control satisfies NFC(ch) == NFD(ch) == ch and has an empty canonical decomposition field. The confusables in this population have no canonical decomposition; no arm is an NFC/NFD arm.
    • (b) vs the NFKC leg (wave-12 candidate 3, unbuilt): NFKC(ch) == ch executed for every frozen replacement, both controls, and every panel letter. No arm is silently an NFKC fold, and — the regime consequence — an NFKC-normalizing pipeline (e.g. SentencePiece nmt_nfkc) does not fold these characters. This probe's ids gate (§6) is therefore expected to find divergence where candidate 1's DICP arms found identity — a difference between the sibling probes' regimes, pre-registered in §6.
    • (c) vs invisible-chars (§8.60): disjoint mechanisms and disjoint code points, executed: the frozen replacements are all category-Ll visible letters; their code-point set's intersection with {U+200B, U+FE0F, U+E0001, U+00AD, U+2060} is empty; substitution preserves code-point count where insertion adds one per site. (Skeleton step 2 removes DICP characters — the two probes sit on opposite sides of the skeleton function: candidate 1 inserts what skeleton deletes; candidate 2 substitutes what skeleton equates.)
    • (d) vs perturbation's unicode_variant (fullwidth): executed — 14 fullwidth sources in the pinned file are skeleton-equal to a panel letter (e.g. U+FF41 → a). They are excluded by the script-prefix rule (their names start FULLWIDTH, not CYRILLIC/GREEK SMALL LETTER), verified. Reason: fullwidth forms are compatibility forms (NFKC-foldable), owned by perturbation's operator and by candidate 3's territory; admitting them would smuggle the NFKC axis into a skeleton probe and break boundary (b).

    3. Frozen generated material¶

    The sibling panel, unchanged in shape (shared with canonical-form and invisible-chars; the slate expects shared panel machinery):

    • Cases: 24, stage-local, generated only, never the user's Split. Case i: topic = TOPICS[i] (harbor, summit, quarry, tundra, granite, willow, copper, saddle, walnut, velvet, cobalt, marble, cinder, falcon, garnet, saffron, indigo, juniper, lagoon, magnet, nickel, pepper, quartz, birch). Value = WORDS[draw] + "-" + number, WORDS the canonical-form 24-word ASCII pool (alder … yew, read from canonicalform.py in the tree), number ∈ [1000, 9999], pairwise distinct by redraw. Two distinct neutrals from density's NEUTRAL_STATEMENTS (24 ASCII statements). Fact = The {topic} inspection code is {value}. Question = What is the {topic} inspection code? Content = {neutral1} {fact} {neutral2} {question}. Expected = value.
    • Scorer pin: every expected value pure ASCII (asserted in the evidence run on all seeds); verdicts are core.evaluate's byte-exact answer == case.expected (the exact_match conjunction, core.py:827 in the current tree), conjoined with boundary presence as in every sibling stage. Substitutions live only in carrier text (the fact sentence); the question, both neutrals, and the expected value are byte-identical across arms — a model echoing the treated surface is separated by §5.
    • Draws — conversion pinned in text: material f"{seed}|confusableskeleton|{index}|{slot}|{counter}"; integer int.from_bytes(sha256(material).digest()[:8], "big"); word = draw % len(WORDS) (slot 0); number = 1000 + draw % 9000 (slot 1); neutrals = draw % len(NEUTRAL_STATEMENTS) (slots 2, 3; second advances +1 mod len on collision). A value collision redraws slots 0+1 at counter+1; give-up bound 1,000 consecutive redraws, exhaustion fails the stage closed (confusable_skeleton_screen_failed). The counting is as the module implements it: the counter increments once per colliding redraw, and the bound trips when the counter exceeds 1,000 — i.e. after 1,001 colliding draws for one case (amendment A9: the build's freeze tests force a collision and pin the redraw at counter+1, neutrals at counter 0, and this counting).
    • Namespace: NEW (confusableskeleton), not the sibling's invisiblechars. Each shipped probe's material names its own probe (canonicalform / invisiblechars precedent), so panels are independent draws: sharing the namespace would make this panel case-identical to candidate 1's at the same seed and invite cross-probe per-case comparisons the designs never licensed.
    • Panel-size derivation (executed): under this candidate's own null and floors (§6), exact marginal power at the 25-point target is N=16: 0.3904, N=20: 0.6395, N=24: 0.8159 — 24 is the smallest of the three house sizes reaching the design's 0.80 criterion, and it is the shared sibling size. The target effect (25 points) is the decision threshold of §1's utility: a screening decision does not turn on a 3-point wobble; smaller effects read below_threshold (§9), never "no effect".

    Generator determinism evidence. The design-phase generator implemented the frozen spec twice — string slicing and code-point-list rebuild (independent site logic) — with byte-identical populations for seeds {0, 1, 7, 42, 20261030} on the control, single_site, and whole_word arms (red team B's from-text implementation matched all three on all five seeds); the rarity_control arm's populations are the ones regenerated under amendment A2's corrected assignment, and the build's freeze tests pin those. Byte-divergence: every treated arm differs byte-wise from control on 24/24 cases, every seed. Substitution counts per case (topic + value word): single_site exactly 2 (min = max = 2, every seed — both words always have an eligible letter, §2); rarity_control exactly 2; whole_word min 4, max 10, mean 7.04–7.54 across seeds. Exclusions: none, and none silent.

    4. Frozen substitution sites and dose¶

    Arms (4, fully crossed):

    arm treatment (fact sentence only)
    control verbatim
    single_site the first eligible letter of the topic and the first eligible letter of the value word replaced by its frozen confusable
    whole_word every eligible letter occurrence in both words replaced
    rarity_control exactly the single_site sites, replaced by the per-letter rarity control (§2)

    Dose, stated honestly. Per case: single_site and rarity_control substitute exactly 2 code points (1 per treated word, 2 treated words). whole_word substitutes every eligible occurrence: executed range 4–10 per case; as a fraction, on seed 0's drawn panel 171 of 249 letters in treated words are substituted (0.6867) — the remaining 31% are the zero-eligible letters of §2, which no arm can substitute. The arm is "whole-word" in site scope (both words, all eligible sites), not a claim that every letter changes, and not UTS #39 whole-script confusability (a treated word is mixed-script by construction whenever it contains a zero-eligible letter). Dose escalation in response to a null is forbidden (§9).

    Sites. Substitutions are inside the fact sentence only, in the topic word and in the value's word part (before -). The question and neutrals are byte-identical across arms by construction (asserted). First-eligible position is a deterministic function of the frozen table and the word — a second implementation reproduces it byte-identically. The panel is pure ASCII before treatment, so positions are code-point indices equal to byte indices there; substitution preserves code-point count and changes UTF-8 byte count (+1 byte per substituted letter: every frozen replacement and both controls are 2 UTF-8 bytes vs 1 for ASCII — executed; that byte-length delta is part of the treatment and is matched by the rarity control, §2).

    Budget (house pattern): 24 cases × 4 arms × up to 24 candidates = 2,304 generations worst case; 576 ground records; house 12 MiB records cap.

    5. Decomposition (ordered decision list; total)¶

    The byte-exact scorer is the instrument Hua et al. (arXiv:2509.01790, opened) warn about; no discordance is reported undecomposed. Classes are an ordered decision list (the literal predicates overlap, so order is part of the definition): each class is reached only when every earlier predicate fails.

    Cell decomposition — exactly one class per completed cell:

    1. pass — verdict passed.
    2. boundary_only — answer digest == expected digest; the boundary conjunct alone failed.
    3. wrong_value — the answer parses under the frozen value grammar ^[a-z]+-[0-9]{4}$ with word ∈ WORDS and number ∈ [1000, 9999], and is not the expected value. The only class counting toward the headline J (§6). Scope disclosed: J counts wrong values inside the frozen grammar; a value change outside it (out-of-pool word, leading-zero number) decomposes as non_answer, is printed per arm, and never counts toward J; the OC's q is the in-grammar wrong-value rate wherever it appears.
    4. echo_confusable — scripted per arm. For the two confusable arms: the answer equals the expected value with that arm's own treatment applied to the value word (for single_site, its first eligible letter substituted; for whole_word, every eligible letter). For rarity_control the analogous class is named echo_control (the arm's control-treated expected). For control both are empty by construction. The model reproduced the treated surface; the value it read was correct.
    5. form_only — additionally requiring answer ≠ expected. For the confusable arms: mapping every character of the answer that appears in the frozen replacement table back to its Latin letter yields exactly the expected value, and the answer is not in class 4. Gloss scoped (amendment A8(iv)): this is the frozen-table scope — an echo in a non-frozen skeleton-equal letter (e.g. U+0430 for a — eligible, not the frozen pick) is not mapped back and decomposes as non_answer, disclosed here, never counted toward J. For the rarity arm, class 5 is the position-aware predicate (amendment A3): the answer has the same code-point length as the expected value, contains at least one control-set character (д or ζ), and replacing every control-set character by the expected value's character at the same position yields exactly the expected value. (A fixed-inverse reading is impossible for the rarity arm: д stands for the 9 Cyrillic-seat letters and ζ for the 7 Greek-seat letters across cases; neither has a Latin letter to map back to.)
    6. non_answer — everything else, including empty answers, answers with no parseable value, an answer carrying a substituted letter inside an otherwise malformed value, and out-of-grammar value changes (class 3's scope note).

    Grammar battery (frozen wrong shapes the tests must pin): the correct shape; a wrong value in the right form (wrong_value); the arm-site echo and an off-site/mixed echo (classes 4/5 — including an answer echoing the whole-word surface under the single-site arm, which is class 5 there, not 4); the rarity witness дlder-1234 against expected alder-1234 (class 5, form_only, under amendment A3's position-aware definition — and non_answer under the single-site arm, whose inverse is the frozen table); an answer that is another case's value (still wrong_value — the grammar defines the class); empty and boundary-less outputs (non_answer, and §6 incompletion when no span exists); an answer mixing scripts in the word part with a wrong number (non_answer — reverse mapping yields a non-matching or out-of-grammar string); a value with a Cyrillic lookalike digit position (digits are never substituted; any non-ASCII digit fails the grammar → non_answer). Precedence witnesses: the exact expected answer (class 1, not 5 — reverse mapping is the identity on ASCII); a digest-equal answer with a boundary failure (class 2 before all form classes).

    Pair decomposition — for discordant pairs, the sibling total partition: answer digests equal → boundary_only; digests differ, both boundaries found → exact_only; otherwise → mixed. Cross-tabulated with the failing cell's class.

    6. Headline statistic, decision vocabulary, floors, guard¶

    Regime gate first. Id divergence measured at encode time on the FULL render id sequence per (case, candidate), per arm against control (canonical-form invariant 9 discipline). Per-arm reporting: n_ids_identical, n_ids_divergent, the per-case id_length_delta list, max_abs_id_delta, and n_same_ids_verdict_flips, per (candidate, arm). Pre-registered regime expectation (stated as expectation, discovered by measuring ids, never asserted per checkpoint): unlike candidate 1's DICP arms, these characters are not NFKC-foldable (executed, §2b) and not default-ignorable, so both NFKC-regime (SentencePiece-class) and byte-level-BPE pipelines are expected to encode treated text divergently; a family landing ids-identical has a skeleton-screening or confusable-normalizing tokenizer — itself the §1 decision's most direct evidence — and is reported instrument-inapplicable for that arm, never as a pass.

    Headline population per (candidate, arm): cases completed in both control and arm and id-divergent for that arm. D = control passes in the population; J = of those, arm cells decomposed wrong_value.

    Decision vocabulary (total function of D, J, and the guard) — the tree's existing sibling set only, no invented words:

    • insufficient — D < 10, or the guard withholds.
    • no_discordance_observed — D ≥ 10, J = 0, guard passes.
    • discordant_asymmetric — D ≥ 10, J ≥ 6, guard passes. (Fires: the §7 invariance claim is falsified for this arm.)
    • below_threshold — D ≥ 10, 0 < J < 6, guard passes.

    The population is conditioned on control passes (the negation / instruction-distance / history-frame / invisible-chars D-conditioning precedent), so discordance has one direction by construction and discordant_balanced is structurally unreachable. Reverse discordants are counted and printed beside every label as n_reverse, never pooled into J. The two-directional canonical rule was rejected by execution in the sibling round (transplanted floors fire at 0.8897 on the competent independent null at N = 24 — invisible-chars round, executed there and reproduced in its adjudication); this design does not re-litigate it. No sign test is used anywhere (the tree's density.sign_test_p is two-sided and not completion-monotone — the sibling settlement); a descriptive McNemar figure, if printed, uses confidentiality.mcnemar_exact_p over completed pairs only and is never a decision input.

    Floors — derived under this candidate's own null, frozen here. MIN_D = 10, MIN_J = 6, from this candidate's floor search (exact enumeration over D ~ Bin(24, pc), J|D ~ Bin(D, q); grid MIN_D ∈ 1..16 × MIN_J ∈ 2..8, requiring competent-null fire ≤ 0.02 and maximal target power). MIN_J = 6 is set by the search (in a stronger form than claimed: every MIN_J ≤ 5 pair breaches the competent-null bound at every MIN_D — minimum null 0.0591); MIN_D = 10 is selected from a plateau: at printed (4 dp) precision the competent-null and target figures are identical for every MIN_D ∈ [1, 14] (executed), and 10 is chosen from that plateau as the sibling-consistent point — stated as a plateau selection, not a point derivation. Exactness qualifier (amendment A8(v)): the plateau is exact only on [1, 6]; in exact arithmetic the (10, 6) pair trails the plateau maximum by 3.3e-14 (build-phase correction, verified by exact rational arithmetic in the cycle-224 test module: the adjudication's 2.8e-11 did not reproduce under either q convention; the executed gap is 3.329e-14). No floor is borrowed: the search was run against this candidate's rule and null, and its output happens to land on the sibling's pair.

    Completion guard + incompletion perimeter. Two channels, as settled in the sibling round and transferred with its amendments folded in from the start:

    • (a) Hidden slots — completed generations whose span does not parse are completed cells, decomposed non_answer (§5), inside the perimeter; n_non_answer per arm is printed, and §9's pre-registered reading governs high-non-answer discordance. They never count toward J.
    • (b) Incompletion — a generation producing no boundary-delimited span at all (or a missing cell) has no cell; the case leaves the paired population. The guard brings it inside the decision perimeter by construction:
    • Domain pinned: the worst-case completion and the cap range over exactly the incomplete cases in the pair's population domain — cases whose control and arm renders are id-divergent for that arm (an encode-time property, decidable whether or not the generation completed) and which have at least one missing cell. Ids-identical cases never enter the completion, in either direction.
    • Fire cannot be manufactured: J and D are absolute counts over completed cases; no completion raises observed J, and the guarded fire uses observed counts only.
    • Acquittal cannot be manufactured: an acquittal label (no_discordance_observed / below_threshold) issues only if stable under the worst-case completion — every incomplete case in the domain counted as control-pass + arm wrong_value (D+1, J+1 each). Disclosure (amendment A8(ii)): this (D+1, J+1) counting is a deliberate blanket over-count — it ranges over observation-inconsistent completions for partially-observed cases (a case with an observed control-fail cell is still counted as completing control-pass); it is conservative in exactly the direction the guard exists for, and the computation is the one all the evidence ran. Consequences disclosed: fires issue on observed counts (incompletion present does not by itself withhold a fire); no_discordance_observed issues only at n_incomplete = 0; below_threshold survives only while J + n_incomplete < 6.
    • Cap, derived not copied: if incomplete cases in the domain exceed 6 of 24 for a pair, the label is insufficient regardless. Derivation: 6 is the largest incomplete count leaving paired ≥ 18 — 18 being the smallest paired count whose operating characteristics this design prints (the sensitivity rows above). The cap is a paired-population quality floor; its only marginal effect in the exhaustive audit below is suppressing completion-stable fires, disclosed as such.
    • Executed attack on this guard: exhaustive (D, J, k) audit over the reachable state space — 0 manufactured-fire violations, 0 unstable-acquittal violations; fires issue at every k ∈ [0, 6]; the cap suppresses 264 completion-stable fire states at k > 6 over the reachable state space D + k ≤ 24 (amendment A4; a wider D + k ≤ 25 bound counts 345 but ranges over 81 unreachable states) — its only marginal effect. World A (zero-effect null; incompletion concentrated on concordant pass/pass cells, removed w.p. 0.5): guarded fire 0.0055, unguarded 0.0186 (≈ the exact null 0.0173 — manufacture fails); guarded acquittals collapse into withholding (insufficient 0.9923). World B (true drop, q = 0.50; 60% of would-be J cells removed): unguarded fire 0.2551 with below_threshold at 0.7277 — a manufactured acquittal; guarded fire 0.1731 with below_threshold at 0.0117 and 0.8152 withheld — displaced mass is withholding, not acquittal. World C (same drop, flat incompletion 0.15): guarded fire 0.8893 vs unguarded 0.9399 — the guard costs little when incompletion is not sign-concentrated. (10,000 trials per world, seed 20261030.) Caveat (amendment A8(i)): these design-phase worlds range over all incomplete cases — they carry no ids dimension, so their k counts a stricter guard than the pinned text's (the conservative direction); the build's guard battery re-executes the worlds under the pinned id-divergent domain.
    • Cost, printed: in high-incompletion worlds the guard withholds aggressively (World A guarded insufficient 0.9923). That is the chosen direction: incompletion buys silence, never a manufactured verdict in either direction; the precise label behaviour is the bullets above, not the slogan.

    7. Pre-registered falsifiable claim¶

    Over generated fact-recall cases (§3) whose control and treated renders are id-divergent (§6 gate), for a given candidate and substitution arm, the arm's verdicts on control-passing cases show no in-grammar value-changing discordance at or above the frozen floor: conditioning on the id-divergent paired population, J < 6 whenever D ≥ 10, under the §6 guard (amendment A8(iii) — at k > 6 the sentence's antecedent labels are withheld without falsifying).

    • Falsified for a (candidate, arm) exactly when its guarded label is discordant_asymmetric. That is the whole falsification condition; a fire proves only that this arm's skeleton-class substitutions moved this checkpoint's answers to wrong in-grammar values on this generated population — nothing about human visual confusion, weights, attacks, or other scripts/code points.
    • Not falsified ≠ robust. below_threshold and no_discordance_observed acquit only at the printed OC: a 10-point marginal drop fires only 0.2542 of the time. Ids-identical arms are instrument-inapplicable for that family, reported as such, never as passes.
    • The claim is per-arm and per-candidate. No pooled headline and no winner readout in v1 (sibling precedent).

    8. Isolation rows¶

    • The rarity_control arm is generated and classified under the identical rule and receives its own label (it is counted in the §6 family-wise table for exactly that reason). Its null behaviour is an isolation row: under the null process its fire rates are the §6 table's rows, printed per candidate in the report.
    • Pre-registered isolation reading (scoped by amendment A5): a single_site fire is read as skeleton-class-specific only if rarity_control does not also fire for the same candidate — the rarity arm matches single_site's dose exactly (2 substitutions), so the reading is dose-matched there. If both fire, the reading is "non-Latin substitution at these sites disrupts this candidate" (a rarity/tokenization finding), and the confusable arm adds no skeleton-specific evidence. If the rarity arm fires and no confusable arm does, the panel has measured disruption that skeleton equivalence avoids — also decision-relevant for §1, printed as such. For whole_word the isolation reading does not extend: its dose (4–10 substitutions) has no dose-matched rarity baseline anywhere in this design, so a whole-word solo fire reads exactly: "skeleton-class substitution at whole-word dose disrupts this candidate; rarity at that dose is unmeasured by this design" — printed wherever the isolation reading is stated, including the report section.
    • Dose isolation: single_site vs whole_word is a dose contrast at fixed mechanism (2 vs 4–10 substitutions, same table, same words); a fire only at whole_word is read as dose-dependent, not as a different phenomenon.
    • Non-target arms/families under the null process: every arm notionally treated with a null substitution behaves as the competent/paired rows; the report's per-arm table (3 treated arms × candidates, each with label, D, J, n_reverse, n_non_answer) is itself the isolation grid. Cross-arm comparisons use only the uniformly-defined classes (wrong_value, non_answer, boundary_only); echo/form classes are reported per-arm as descriptive (the rarity arm's echo class is echo_control, not echo_confusable).

    9. Risks and pre-registered readings¶

    • R1 — Attack endpoints ≠ benign effect. Boucher et al. measure attacks (a single imperceptible injection significantly reduces vulnerable systems' performance; three break most); Sarabamoun's homoglyph figures (§14, caveated) are jailbreak ASR under a heuristic criterion. This probe substitutes unoptimized, standards-frozen letters on benign recall tasks. The benign effect size is genuinely unknown; a null is a legitimate pre-registered outcome, and dose escalation in response to a null is forbidden: no "add Armenian/Cherokee letters and retry" inside this probe — the zero-eligible letters stay zero-eligible in v1; broadening the script rule is a new design.
    • R2 — Rarity confound, honestly bounded. The §2 control matches script, UTF-8 byte length, category, and the amendment-A7 separation properties — all checkable. It does not match corpus frequency or tokenizer merge rank, quantities this tree does not measure; "comparable rarity" in this design means the checkable properties, and the residual frequency confound is disclosed here rather than claimed away. The §8 isolation reading is written to survive it: a joint fire is reported as rarity/tokenization, not skeleton.
    • R3 — Mixed-script side effects. Whole-word substitution changes script-run structure; some tokenizers treat script runs specially. Reported per family via the ids gate; never pooled blindly — there is no pooled headline to pool into (§7).
    • R4 — Partial whole-word dose. §4's 0.6867 letter fraction (seed 0) is printed, not hidden: if whole_word nulls, the reading is "skeleton substitution at ~69% of letters in two carrier words did not fire", not "words are robust to confusables". The fraction is a per-panel executed quantity and is printed in the report beside the arm.
    • R5 — Version instability. §2's pin is load-bearing: a Unicode version bump changes the population (UTS #39's own caveat). The build pins the data version in its case material (the table digest binds the frozen table and the pinned files' SHA-256 digests, §10) so a silent data change is a digest change, not a result change.
    • R6 — Scorer artefact. Without §5 this probe counts a model echoing Cyrillic surface as a capability change; with it, the headline counts only wrong_value. The residual risk is masking via silence (non_answer): printed per arm, with the pre-registered reading that an arm whose discordance is dominated by non_answer is reported as "substitution disrupts output form", a distinct finding — never silently acquitted, never pooled into J.
    • R7 — Fully specified panel. The panel's instruction / question / contract strings are frozen verbatim (§3) so the instrument measures the substitution, not ambiguity (the slate's framing result for all wave-12 candidates).

    12. Rejected alternatives¶

    • Broadening the script rule (any non-Latin small letter): executed consequence — f, g, n, z gain Armenian/Cherokee replacements (U+0584, U+0581, U+0578, U+AB93), b/k/l/m/t stay zero-eligible anyway, and the population stops being the class UTS #39's own examples and the slate define (Cyrillic/Greek), mixing scripts whose rarity/tokenization profiles differ from the frozen class. Rejected for v1; R1 forbids adopting it as a dose-escalation response. (Completeness note from the review: under the broadened rule j's smallest pick would also shift, to U+03F3; the list above is illustrative of the consequence class, not exhaustive.)
    • Sequence substitutions (rn↔m): normatively real (executed, §2) but length-changing; a different dose axis. Outside v1's population by the single-code-point rule.
    • Mathematical alphanumeric / fullwidth confusables: skeleton-equal in the file (dozens per letter) but they are compatibility forms — NFKC territory (candidate 3) and perturbation's width axis; boundary (b)/(d) discipline excludes them.
    • Uppercase arms: the panel is lowercase by construction; capital-letter confusables are a second population (several panel letters' richest sources are capitals — l's 73 include U+0049/U+0406 class sources). A capital arm doubles the family-wise price to answer a question §1 does not ask.
    • Two-directional canonical classification: rejected by execution in the sibling round (0.8897 transplant fire, §6).
    • Borrowed floors: forbidden by the slate's method note; §6's floors are derived under this candidate's own null.
    • Sign-test headline: two-sided, not completion-monotone; this design needs no test (§6).
    • A screening/detection arm in v1 (run a skeleton screener over the treated text and measure it): that measures an implementation, not a model; §1's decision needs the model effect first. Separate candidate if ever.
    • Winner readout: §7 — not consumed by the utility; abstention-dominated at these rates.
    • A whole-word rarity arm (considered in review, rejected): it would make §8's isolation reading dose-matched at whole-word dose, at the price of a fourth labelled arm and a changed family-wise count — fixing a prose scope by changing the instrument. Amendment A5 scopes the reading instead.

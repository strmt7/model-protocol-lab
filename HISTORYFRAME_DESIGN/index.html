    History-frame probe — design of record¶

    Status¶

    BUILT in cycle 219 as run --history-frame, under the fresh review's BUILD-as-amended verdict (coordinator #61, Part III below): amendments A1–A10 are incorporated into Part I, which is the instrument as built. Part II is the round-1 adjudication (coordinator #44, NO-BUILD) whose §3 resubmission specification the fresh review judged; the candidate text of record is research §6 (~/workspace/agents_out/historyframe/research.md), of which Part I is the as-amended form. Endpoints (the four arms, the single k=6 prepend, the frozen chatml/llama3 donor pair), the population (the run's search fixtures preceded by the frozen synthetic history), and the headline scorer (byte-exact ∧ boundary) are frozen — an amendment touching any of them would have been a resubmission, not this build.

    Part I — The instrument as built (candidate text as amended)¶

    1. What exactly varies — the treatment dimension¶

    The framing strings of the prepended history segment only: per-turn role prefixes, per-role turn terminators, and the history segment's internal repeat_bos behaviour, taken from a Format's fields. Message contents are byte-identical across arms by construction; the final segment (the fixture's own messages), the prompt-initial bos, and the open suffix are always the candidate's own. Token length, seam shape, and marker encoding are consequences of the framing strings, reported per case — the claim subject is the named frozen framing pair, never "history" or "migration" in the abstract. Arm-level batch composition and numerics channels correlated with the foreign prompts' length are named residuals inside the string-scoped claim (amendment A8, §10 below).

    2. Population and frozen texts¶

    • The run's search fixtures only (≤100), post-hoc stage on the recorded run; held-out fixtures are never treated and the primary decision/winner/exit code are never touched.
    • Every search fixture is prepended with the shipped frozen history: synthhistory.build_history_fixtures(cases, condition="history_6"), i.e. SYNTHETIC_TURNS (6 turns, 3 exchanges), reused, never copied or re-authored. The prepended segment is the reframed object (k = 6). The fixture's own messages — including any native history they already carry — are the tail and are always framed by the candidate's own family. Construction pin (amendment A5): the stage takes the built fixtures' messages and re-wraps them under each fixture's original id, its original expected, and the module's frozen group label history_frame; the built fixtures' namespaced ids (synthhistory/history_6/<id>) are never stage fixture ids, because ids feed request seeds (§3).
    • The reframed history is scripted and benign and task-free, so the claim is scoped to this frozen transcript — exactly the scoping synthhistory already ships — and says nothing about task-bearing or self-generated histories. Screen: none beyond §4 (a closed-book screen is meaningless here — the history carries no task content that could be removed; the fixture's task is in the tail, held constant across arms).

    3. Hybrid-render semantics (frozen formal definition)¶

    For history Format A, final Format B, message tuple msgs (the prepended fixture's messages), split point k = 6:

    seg(F, m, i)  = (F.bos if i > 0 and F.repeat_bos else "")
              + (F.user_prefix if m.role == "user" else F.assistant_prefix)
              + m.content
              + (per-role end of F for m.role, falling back to F.turn_end)
open(B)       = (B.bos if B.repeat_bos else "")
              + (B.open_suffix if B.open_suffix is not None else B.assistant_prefix)
hybrid(A,B)   = B.bos
              + concat(seg(A, msgs[i], i) for i < k)
              + concat(seg(B, msgs[i], i) for i >= k)
              + open(B)
verbatim(A,B) = A.bos + (hybrid with only the leading B.bos removed)

    Pins, each load-bearing:

    • BOS is B's in every headline arm. A's bos appears in no headline prompt. The verbatim arm differs from its headline twin only in the leading bos string, so headline-vs-verbatim discordance is the BOS-substitution share, printed descriptively, never a decision input. The removal is leading-only (amendment A1): a repeat_bos candidate's internal bos occurrences stay.
    • A's open suffix never appears. The history segment ends at the last history turn's A terminator; the next bytes are the tail's first B segment. No separator is inserted at the seam, ever.
    • repeat_bos follows the segment's family, with global message indices (so the tail's first message, at index 6, takes B's repeat when B.repeat_bos). Under A == B this reproduces Format.render exactly (verified by the review at 354/354 over the candidate Formats and by the build's property test over the catalog plus local repairs).
    • Assistant-history turns use A's assistant_prefix (with its think scaffolds where the family defines them), never A's open_suffix; the generation prompt is B's open(B). Behaviours no Format field represents (auto-injected system blocks, reasoning-content history) are out of scope for every arm equally and are named as such in the section.
    • Evaluation path: one core.evaluate call per (candidate, arm, fixture) through a carrier Format(B.name, "", "", "", "") holding the hybrid text as a single user message, with B's resolved boundary ids, following the _canonical_form_cell pattern: ids precomputed by backend.encode(hybrid_text), context check before generation, aggregate-eligible report → cell, else the per-cell detail. The stage fixture carries the fixture's original id in every arm (request-seed identity across arms, core.py:371). Records key the fixture by that id.

    Controls and what each separates:

    5. Eligibility, divergence, and the encoding stratum¶

    Per (candidate, headline contrast), per fixture:

    • Render-divergent iff the A history segment bytes differ from the B history segment bytes for that fixture (the segment depends only on the frozen turns, so this is constant across fixtures within a candidate — but it is recorded per fixture, as segment digests, so the validator recomputes the same population). Render-identical fixtures are excluded from the contrast, counted as n_render_identical; a contrast render-identical on every fixture is inapplicable for that candidate: no classification is emitted for it, and the section prints the inapplicable share, never a robustness reading.
    • Ids-identical (byte-divergent renders encoding to the same id sequence under the run's backend) cases are likewise excluded from the headline population and counted; a same-ids verdict flip is recorded as a determinism finding, never as frame fragility (canonical-form discipline). The gate is digest-derived (amendment A4): records carry per-arm token-id digests (the negation digest_ids pattern), and both the producer and the validator derive ids-identity from digest equality — the candidate text's carried divergence flags are not used, because the review executed a label flip through them. Per contrast the section prints the id_length_delta list (own ids − foreign ids, fixture order) and max_abs_id_delta.
    • Verbatim applicability (amendment A1): when the candidate's bos equals the chatml donor's bos, the verbatim arm is byte-identical to its headline twin (55/118 candidate Formats in the review's census, chatml's bos being the empty string) — the readout is recorded inapplicable for that candidate: counted, printed, its discordance never pooled, and never read as a BOS share of zero. Applicability is likewise derived from the recorded bos digests.
    • Encoding stratum (run-level, per foreign family): for each distinct donor history framing literal, token_real iff backend.encode(literal) yields exactly one id (the deliforge operational definition; the stage has only the Backend protocol, so registration/id-consistency in core.template_tokenizer_compatibility's sense is not claimed). Stratum = all_single / all_fragmented / mixed, with the per-literal table printed when mixed. The stratum is constant within a run (one tokenizer), is printed beside every contrast's classification, and is a compared field in diff-reports so cross-checkpoint comparisons never pool strata silently. No checkpoint fact is asserted in this design: which stratum a given checkpoint lands in is a run-time measurement.

    6. Scorer, decomposition, decision rule, selection¶

    • Cell verdict: exact_match and boundary_found from the carrier evaluation (§3), i.e. core.py:797 byte-exact conjoined with boundary presence. No stripping, parsing, or normalisation is added anywhere on the headline path.
    • Decomposition of every discordant pair (negation §4.2 pattern, whose boundary_only disposition the parent accepted for this tree): boundary_only when the arms' answer digests are equal (the discordance is a boundary event, never a frame effect on the answer), exact_only when digests differ and both boundaries were found, mixed otherwise. All three counts printed per contrast; the classification counts verdict discordance as recorded (boundary-only discordance is printed separately so a reader can see how much of a classification is boundary-carried — it is not silently removed from the counts, because the tree's verdict is the conjunct).
    • Classification (per candidate, per headline contrast), over paired completed render-divergent ids-divergent cases: n = paired count, a = own-only passes, b = foreign-only passes, d = a + b. Total function: n < 10 → insufficient; d = 0 → no_discordance_observed; d < 6 → below_threshold; a == b → discordant_balanced (descriptive); else McNemar two-sided exact p (confidentiality.py:293) < 0.05 → discordant_asymmetric, else below_threshold. The discordant floor of 6 is forced by α = 0.05 (a unanimous 5–0 split gives two-sided p = 0.0625; 6–0 gives 0.03125) — derived, not borrowed. The n-floor of 10 stands as a frozen conservative constant (amendment A3): the candidate text's derivation was false — the smallest interior-attainable n for a 6–0 split is 8 — and the operating-characteristics table, derived with the floor in place, is the floor's justification. Falsification boolean := classification == discordant_asymmetric (direction read from a vs b), with the exact random-labeller fire rate at the run's own n printed beside every classification and the family-wise expectation computed from those rates over the run's decisions (amendment A2: at uniform n = 100 over 48 decisions the exact expectation is 1.76 false labels with P(≥1) ≈ 0.83 — the candidate text's printed 1.44 / 0.77 was 48 × a rounded rate and is corrected everywhere it rendered). Under symmetric two-arm jitter the fire rate is 0.0004 / 0.0048 / 0.0213 at ε = 0.01 / 0.02 / 0.05 (D-invariant), printed in the section (amendment A6); one-sided jitter variants are arm-asymmetric perturbations — inside the alternative, not the null. The review's PoC5 correlated column is dropped (amendment A7: no reproducible reading); the printed bounds stand — random-labeller fire ≤ 3.6 percent and competent-null fire ≤ 3.1 percent at every tabulated N. Discordance is never pooled across candidates or contrasts.
    • Selection readout: per arm, the conservative selection (exactly one candidate passing every fixture in that arm wins) recomputed on the common completed set across candidates for that arm; withheld with the canonical-form detail selection_withheld_mismatched_sets when the restricted completed sets differ or are empty. Winner headline per headline contrast via canonicalform.winner_headline semantics: winner_changed only when both arms select and winners differ; selection-vs-abstention is winner_abstention_asymmetric, never a change; withheld on either side → winner_undefined. Flips against the primary run are printed separately, labelled as pooling history presence with frame (the synthhistory flips pattern, with its verdict mapping), and are never the headline.
    • Per-arm attrition (n_context_exceeded, n_evaluation_failed, n_render_identical, n_ids_identical) printed beside every paired rate; ungated discordance (all paired completed cases, before divergence exclusions) printed as a sensitivity wherever gating applies.

    7. Fail-closed vocabulary (frozen; no new verdict words)¶

    Following the sibling pattern exactly (negation §4.3):

    • Stage details exactly: {cancelled, historyframe_record_budget_exceeded, historyframe_screen_failed}, the latter bound to screens S1/S2 of §4.
    • Per-cell details exactly: {"", arm_context_exceeded, arm_evaluation_failed}.
    • Whole-stage failure (any renderer/serializer exception, non-UTF-8 payload): {"available": False, "detail": "historyframe_unavailable"} — the stage never fails the run (the CLI _run_*_probe except-pattern).
    • Section classifications exactly: insufficient, no_discordance_observed, discordant_balanced, discordant_asymmetric, below_threshold; winner headlines exactly winner_changed, winner_stable, winner_abstention_asymmetric, winner_undefined. The only new strings are the module-prefixed stage details, which is the established pattern, not a new verdict word.
    • Model parse failure is scored wrong, never a detail.

    8. Counts (frozen, with derivations)¶

    • Generating arms: 4 (own, foreign_chatml, foreign_llama3, verbatim_chatml descriptive). Legal maximum: 4 × 100 search fixtures × 24 candidates = 9,600 generations; headline arms only: 3 × 100 × 24 = 7,200. (A run's actual candidate count is one family's variants — typically ≤ 8 — so the legal maximum is a cap, not an expectation; it is stated as a cap, per tree convention.)
    • Ground records: 24 × 100 = 2,400 multi-cell records (one per candidate × fixture, one cell per arm — the canonical-form record shape), MAX_RECORDS_BYTES at the house 12 MiB pattern. Caps are frozen constants in the module with derivations in CLI help, enforced by the stage and re-derived by the validator.
    • Context reality check: the prepended native render adds the frozen history's tokens to every fixture; long foreign framings will push some (candidate, fixture) cells over --max-context into arm_context_exceeded. That attrition is arm-ordered by construction and is exactly why §6 recomputes selection on common completed sets and prints attrition beside every rate; it is disclosed, not hidden.

    9. Records and validator mirror¶

    Records carry digests only: per record, the fixture id, the expected-answer digest, the candidate/donor bos digests, each arm's history-segment digest, hybrid-text digest, and token count, the per-arm token-id digests (amendment A4), the four cells (verdict, boundary_found, boundary_text_match, input_tokens, answer_digest — the canonical-form cell shape), and cell_details. The payload's protocol object binds the donor pair, k = 6, the fixture ids, the frozen history material's digest, the segment rule's digest, the run's seed-bearing backend fingerprint, and the frozen floors and budgets. The diff validator mirror re-derives — never imports — the eligibility accounting, the classification, the restricted-set selection, the winner headlines, the primary flips, and the family-wise expectation from the records, and rejects flag/count inconsistencies and any bos-pair inconsistency across a candidate's records. (The mirror cannot re-render the hybrids: the population is the run's own fixtures, which are not regenerable from the payload — the render is pinned at stage time by screen S1 and in the protocol by the segment-rule digest. Everything decidable from the records is recomputed; the review's decidability executions re-proved exactly that set.) Reports render counts/rates only, no prompt/answer text, every payload-derived string through _escape_text, never bare escape().

    10. The claim (only falsification-class sentence)¶

    "On this candidate, on this run's search fixtures preceded by the frozen synthetic history, rendering that history's turns in the frozen chatml [resp. llama3] framing instead of the candidate's own framing produced verdict discordance at or above this panel's derived floor, asymmetric by an exact McNemar test — a history-frame discordance for these named framings and this frozen history, on this checkpoint." Every word of scope in that sentence is load-bearing; no sentence in the report may generalise it to histories, migrations, or templates in general. Named residuals inside that scope (amendment A8): token length and marker encoding are consequences of the framing strings and move with the treatment by construction; arm-level directional channels — batch composition and numerics correlated with the foreign prompts' length — are not separated by this instrument and are printed beside the claim so the label is never read as a mechanism certificate.

    11. Naming audit (binding build condition, executed)¶

    The fresh review required the build's USAGE naming audit to either adopt a prepend-carrying flag name or keep --history-frame only with first-sentence disclosure of the prepend scope. Audit (cycle 219 build log): zero collisions for --history-frame / history_frame in cli.py, USAGE, or the package; the name's plain reading ("the frame of the history") understates the construction — the probe prepends a frozen synthetic history and reframes only that segment — so the disclosure form was adopted: the CLI help's first sentence, the USAGE section's first sentence, and the report section's first sentence all state that the probe prepends the frozen six-turn synthetic history to every search fixture and reframes only that prepended segment, and that the fixtures' own messages are never reframed.

    12. Uniqueness pass (primary sources)¶

    The review round re-surveyed the neighbouring instruments against primary sources (LM Evaluation Harness, HELM, OpenAI Evals, DSPy — see the research log entry for cycle 219): none ships segment-level cross-format reframing of a prepended conversation history with paired verdict discordance, divergence-gated eligibility, and per-frame selection recomputation. Within the tree, the neighbours are synthhistory (history presence/depth, native framing only), canonical-form (Unicode normalisation of the whole prompt), and template robustness (whole-template swaps). The differentiation claimed is the combination — a frozen history held byte-identical in content while only its framing strings are exchanged for a foreign family's, under the run's own fixtures and scorer — never a claim of being first at any single element.

    1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects of the candidate as specified¶

    F1 — The population is undefined: "history" has three incompatible readings, and the shipped fixtures are half-vacuous¶

    The sketch never says where the history turns come from, and the tree offers three mutually exclusive sources — the fixture's own messages, synthhistory's scripted turns, self-generated replies — each a different instrument with a different construct and budget (RT-A F1, RT-B F4, R §2 F1, V F-POPULATION). Executed on the shipped example set (examples/fixtures.json, verified by all four lanes): search = search-copy-blue (1 message, no history) and search-latest-green (3 messages); held-out likewise 1 of 2. For a single-turn fixture the foreign condition is byte-identical to control by construction — guaranteed-concordant filler in every denominator. The loader admits 1-message fixtures (datasets.py:53-72; MAX_MESSAGES = 21 at datasets.py:14), so no dataset guarantees the sketch's "100 fixtures" of history. Under the fixture-own reading, history length (dose) and content are per-fixture and uncontrolled — in the shipped set the history assistant turns are task answers ("RED", "NORTH") and the final turns refer to them, so discordance would pool framing with history-content leverage (RT-A F12, RT-B F4). Under the self-generated reading the budget is false: each foreign cell needs one generation per history exchange before the scored generation — up to 28,800 generations at history_6 depth against the sketch's 7,200 (RT-A F1, arithmetic verified).

    F2 — The hybrid renderer does not exist, its seam semantics are unspecified, and the sketch's seam paragraph is wrong in both particulars¶

    Format.render (core.py:76-115) renders a whole message tuple in one framing and requires a final user turn. Executed by all four lanes: render(history_alone) raises ValueError for 29/29 catalog families — a history prefix, which ends with an assistant turn, is not a renderable object. The sketch's literal recipe (render the segments, concatenate) fails its own pre-registered control check: concatenating a history render (which needs a dummy closing user turn to satisfy render at all) with a final-turn render duplicates BOS, strands family A's open suffix mid-prompt, and inserts a dummy turn (RT-A F2: 182 vs 132 chars chatml, 362 vs 246 llama3, control condition included). The sketch's seam note — A's render "may carry its own BOS and closing suffix mid-prompt", to be kept "verbatim (recommended)" — is executed-wrong twice (RT-B F1, verifier V-2.2): in a prefix-truncated A render there is no A closing suffix at the seam (the open suffix is appended only after the final turn), and A's BOS lands at position 0, replacing B's, not mid-prompt. The decisions that determine the prompt's bytes are all unmade in the sketch, and each is load-bearing:

    • BOS ownership. Coordinator execution: the donor-BOS and final-BOS splice readings produce different prompts on 658/812 ordered family pairs. Only the choice is frozen in §3's resubmission; the sketch freezes none.
    • repeat_bos cadence. No catalog base sets it, but shipped candidates do (llama3/toggle-bos via catalog.candidates(mutate=True)). A per-segment cadence restart produces 1 BOS where the control has 4 (RT-A F2).
    • None-vs-empty fallbacks. core.py:57-73 honours empty-string overrides as distinct from None; a hybrid written with the natural or fallback fails control identity for mistral_large alone (open_suffix "" vs assistant_prefix " "), so a casual control test on chatml passes while the renderer is wrong (RT-A F2).
    • Open suffix is not history framing. open_suffix != assistant_prefix for 15/29 families (verifier-confirmed list), 9 of them opening a think block; the suffix is the generation prompt for the next reply, and any recipe that keeps a history render's tail injects it, sometimes unclosed, at the seam.

    The control-identity check the sketch pre-registers is a test, not a specification: three independent executions this round (coordinator: 118 candidate Formats × 2 conversations; verifier; researcher: 944/944 renders) prove a faithful field-splice semantics can reproduce B.render(full) byte-identically when A == B — by a recipe the sketch does not contain.

    F3 — There is no decision rule: the any-difference falsifier fires on noise with probability ≈ 1, and the selection readout is a one-flip tripwire¶

    The sketch's falsifier is verbatim the one this program has killed twice (DIRECTIVE_DESIGN F2; INSTRUCTIONDISTANCE_DESIGN F3): "any fixture whose verdict differs … or a selection flip in any foreign-frame condition." No floor, no discordant-pair rule, no fail-closed vocabulary. Executed (RT-A F3, RT-B F3, V F-NOISE): at per-cell jitter ε = 0.001, P(any discordance in the panel) = 0.909–0.992; at ε ≥ 0.005, ≈ 1.000. batchinv.py exists precisely because identical token sequences need not reproduce across batch composition, so ε = 0 cannot be assumed. The selection half is worse: _select_winner (experiments.py:3847-3863) requires the winner to pass every search fixture, so one flipped cell manufactures a "selection flip"; conditioned on a native winner existing, P(it loses all-pass status in ≥ 1 of 2 foreign conditions) = 0.633 / 0.866 / 0.982 at ε = 0.005 / 0.01 / 0.02 (RT-B F3, verifier re-evaluated). Nor can a builder patch in a familiar floor: any (6,·)-shaped discordant floor fires ≈ 1.000 at this panel's N = 100, because the null discordance mean is 50 (random) or ≈ 18 (competent) — the sibling floors were derived for panels of 16 generated cases, and this panel's N is the user's fixture count (RT-B F3). As specified, the instrument can essentially only report "not invariant" — a verdict decided by the rule, not measured from the model.

    F4 — Attribution: the treatment is a bundle of at least five simultaneous changes, and the claim names one cause¶

    Control vs foreign differ at once in (RT-A F4, RT-B F2, R §2 F4):

    • Marker identity — the named treatment.
    • BOS identity/presence (F2): the prompt's first tokens change with A. The tree treats BOS as a verdict-moving axis in its own right (core.local_repairs, core.py:118-143, ships toggle-bos/no-bos candidates to measure it).
    • History token length. Same six synthetic turns framed: 341 chars (mistral) to 989 chars (kimi3) (verifier-confirmed); per-exchange prompt-length deltas are nonzero for 766/812 ordered pairs, range [−213, +213] chars under a bos-inclusive splice (segment-only splice: 770/812, ±216 — both executions correct under their splice definitions, and the divergence between two honest executions is itself F2's point). Length and final-turn position are shipped axes (needle, distraction, placement).
    • Think scaffolding. Nine families carry think material inside their assistant history prefix itself (glm45, glm53, laguna, ling, nanbeige42, kimi3, k2horizon, hy4, minimax3 — verifier-executed count; §5 corrects RT-A's five). Foreign history in these families injects a foreign reasoning-channel scaffold the control lacks — an output-priming component, not a delimiter relabelling.
    • Closure conventions. deepseek history has no user-turn terminator (user_turn_end=""); some families' turns run together. "Foreign framing" here includes a segmentation change.

    The sketch's claim sentence attributes any discordance to framing-as-such. What the contrast can honestly estimate is discordance between two named, frozen prompt strings per (candidate, fixture) — the payload settlement. The sketch's Monday-morning framing ("whether history must be re-rendered on migration or can be carried over as-is") additionally fails on the artifact: the hybrid (A's BOS at the head of a B session, B's final turn) is neither a carried-over transcript nor a re-render — no migration produces it (RT-B F2).

    F5 — The pair rule is broken: unnamed pairs, a disjointness criterion that excludes the realistic migrations, and a family/candidate unit mismatch¶

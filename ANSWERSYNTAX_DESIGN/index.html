    Answer-syntax probe — design round (ADJUDICATED: NO-BUILD)¶

    Round summary (coordinator #39, design round only — nothing built)¶

    • Tree at main f31e72e1ace4 (cycle 212 closed green, 8,439 tests, precise coverage 95.3455%). This round was READ-ONLY except for this doc and the briefs; no suite was run, no push was made, no tag was cut or discussed.
    • Specialists (blind to each other): researcher (~/workspace/agents_out/answersyntax/research.md), red-team A (redteam_a.md), red-team B (redteam_b.md), verifier/claims auditor (verifier.md). All four briefs landed; the verifier resolved every externally checkable claim against primary sources, re-ran every surviving PoC script, and recomputed all decision math with its own simulator. The coordinator re-executed the load-bearing claims itself (§4).
    • Convergence and divergence, in one paragraph: all three blind briefs return NO-BUILD for the candidate as specified in the circulated sketch (three syntax arms, JSON-vs-YAML endpoint contrast, field-order's decision rule transplanted unchanged), and the verifier upholds that unanimously. The researcher additionally specifies an amended instrument (JSON-vs-XML headline endpoints, YAML descriptive only, neutral schema description, re-derived 13-spec paired pool, parsed-value headline scorer) and recommends BUILD for it; the verifier upholds that instrument as-amended, subject to corrections (§5), and flags that the amendments change the endpoint pair, the paired population, and the headline scorer. The coordinator's verdict is NO-BUILD — for the sketch, and as a build order for the amended instrument in this round — for the procedural reason stated in §2: under this program's own adjudicated distinction (payload round vs negation round), the amended instrument is a resubmission, and a resubmission is reviewed as a new design before it is built. §3 records it as the resubmission candidate in full. No fabricated citation or symbol was found in any brief this round; the failures found were one wrong p-value, several unreproducible secondary figures, and sloppy line ranges, all handled in §5.

    1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects of the candidate as specified¶

    Each defect below is individually blocking and was established by execution (attribution: A = red-team A, B = red-team B, V = verifier, C = coordinator).

    F1 — Population and scorer are both undefined (rank-2 F1/F4, inherited)¶

    The claim names "the shipped flat structured-output spec pool" (9 specs, structured.py:75-223); the budget names field-order's pool (15 specs, 10 paired). The claim, the budget, and the decision rule therefore have three different denominators. Worse, "the answer field's exact-value accuracy" has two readings in the tree — byte-exact (core.py:797: exact_match = status == "completed" and answer == case.expected) and field-order's parsed-value digest comparison — and they give opposite verdicts on identical behaviour (B, executed with stub policies: a JSON-only policy with always-correct values scores endpoint 10/10 vs 0/10 under byte-exact, 10/10 vs 10/10 under parsed-value; a perfect policy with reversed YAML key order scores 0/10 byte-exact). A design whose headline flips with an unmade scorer choice does not have a headline.

    F2 — The conditioning makes the adherence half of the claim unfalsifiable (new fatal)¶

    The sketch's only decision rule conditions the value contrast on specs adherent under both endpoint arms, yet adherence-rate equality is half of the claim sentence — and no rule anywhere in the sketch tests the adherence contrast. Executed (B, deterministic): a candidate with JSON adherence 10/10 and YAML adherence 5/10 — a 50-point adherence gap — leaves a conditioned pool of 5, below the floor of 6, so the verdict is insufficient by construction; Monte Carlo over adherence gaps of 0.95 vs 0.55 falsifies 0.000–0.013 of the time and returns insufficient 0.977–1.000 of the time. The conditioning also biases the value contrast it does test toward the null (C, executed: with a true 0.60 value gap on 40% of a 10-spec pool, mean discordant specs fall from 3.72 unconditioned to 1.74 conditioned — the floor of 6 is unreachable in that world). Outcome-dependent exclusion of exactly the cases the treatment moves is the payload round's F4 lesson, here at the level of the claim itself.

    F3 — The XML arm can never pass the tree's schema checker (new fatal)¶

    XML parsers produce strings for every scalar. The tree's checker is type-exact (structured.check_schema, structured.py:500; _field_value_ok, structured.py:529-565: an integer field requires type(value) is int). Executed (B; C independently): all 10 of field-order's paired specs contain at least one non-string field, so the only document an XML parser can produce for a perfect model answer returns schema_failed on 10/10 paired specs. Scoring XML at all requires a schema-directed coercion layer that does not exist in the tree; the sketch neither names nor freezes one, and its choices (whitespace, "36.0", "yes") would be the headline. That is rank-2 F2 arriving through the scorer: "same value" across syntaxes is exactly what has to be stipulated, and the sketch does not stipulate it.

    F4 — The YAML endpoint rests on ground the rank-2 round already buried (rank-2 F6)¶

    Zero runtime dependencies (pyproject.toml:28, dependencies = []); no stdlib YAML parser exists. PyYAML imports in this VM only as an environment accident — executed (C): version 6.0.3 in the project venv and 6.0.1 in the system interpreter, two different accidents, neither declared. Both parser choices break a claim (B, executed): JSON is a subset of YAML flow style, so a JSON echo is adherent and correct under a real YAML parser and parse_failed under a block-only subset parser — the "YAML adherence rate" is a property of the parser choice, not of the model. And a real parser imports YAML 1.1's coercion folklore into the measurement (executed by A, B, and C: yes/on → True; no in a string field → False; 0123 → 83 (octal); 0x24 → 36 (hex); 500,000 → the string "500,000"; duplicate keys silently last-wins, with no hook to express field-order's duplicate-key pairing condition). The sketch supplies no reckoning with any of this; rank-2 F6 already held that a hand-rolled mirror parser is new untrusted code whose choices become hidden treatments, and that holding was not answered.

    F5 — The transplanted decision rule is borrowed, and near-inert on its own panel¶

    The binding constraint of this program is that floors are derived for the panel, never borrowed (the negation round removed borrowed (6,2) floors that fired on a random labeller 58% of the time). The sketch transplants field-order's (6, sign-test) rule unchanged and prints none of this panel's operating characteristics. Executed (A, B, C, V — independent code, reconciled by V): the rule's false-fire rate is fine (exact 0.9% on a random labeller, 10-spec pool, adherence 1.0 — C), but its power at the canonical gap field-order itself advertises (0.90 vs 0.60) is 3.2% through the full conditioned pipeline (A), 5.1% at adherence 0.95 (B), 7.4–7.7% at adherence 1.0 (B, C, V). On the shipped pool alone (9 specs, 4 paired under field-order's flags) the floor of 6 discordant specs is unreachable by construction (C): the instrument can only ever answer insufficient. An instrument that returns its withheld label 82–97% of the time across the plausible world will be read — whatever its docs say — as an acquittal machine.

    F6 — The congruence dilemma: the schema description bundles the treatment (rank-2 F3)¶

    structured.describe_schema (structured.py:403-443) renders a JSON-shaped description — executed verbatim (V): {"age": integer (>= 0); "name": string} (required: age, name; no other keys allowed). Hold it constant across arms and every non-JSON arm is depiction-incongruent (a JSON-shaped schema inside a YAML/XML demand); re-render it per arm and the instruction's shape, length, and escaping move together with the treatment. The sketch mentions neither horn. The arm-length bundle is likewise real and systematic (C, executed on the shipped 9 specs with an <answer>-root XML renderer: expected documents run YAML 0.65–0.79× and XML 1.57–2.12× the JSON length; the researcher's <response>-root renderer gives XML 1.49–2.35× across the 15 — quote lengths only with the renderer named, §5 R-11). A paired contrast over these arms attributes a flip to "syntax" only if the claim is worded as discordance between named frozen prompt+rendering pairs; the sketch's claim is worded as syntax sameness in the abstract.

    F7 — The XML surface is unfrozen, and its parser surface is unsafe as shipped defaults¶

    Attribute-form and element-form documents encoding identical content score {} under each other's parsers (A and B, executed); namespaced answers yield {urn:x}-style keys and fail schema; no attribute-vs-element ruling, namespace policy, whitespace policy, DTD policy, or size cap is named in the sketch. The stdlib parser the build would reach for, xml.etree.ElementTree, expands internal entities on untrusted model output (C, executed: a depth-4 nested-entity document expands to 10,000 chars; A, executed and V-reproduced: expanded lengths 7,290 / 65,610 / 590,490 chars at increasing depth), while external entities raise ParseError. ET alone is therefore not a safety story for parsing model output; a frozen pre-parse screen is required, and even behind such a screen the ET call needs a bandit disposition at build time (B314 fires regardless — V, §5).

    Non-fatals, adjudicated¶

    • Multiplicity. Per-candidate decisions at 24 candidates compound: B executed P(≥1 falsified candidate) ≈ 0.197 on pure noise for the sketch's panel (and ≈0.48 if all three pairs are read as decisions). Any successor design must print its panel-wise expectation beside the verdicts; the amended instrument does (0.36 expected false falsifications per 24-candidate panel at its executed random rate).
    • Naming. The sketch performs no naming audit; "syntax adherence" is already owned by the tool-call probe (toolcall.py:1; cli.py:374). The researcher's in-design audit (§3, item 8) resolves this and is carried into the resubmission specification.
    • Overlap. structured owns JSON adherence, fieldorder owns value contrasts within JSON, toolcall owns its call notation. The residue — demanded document syntax as a treatment — is real but thin relative to the slate's largest build; that is a value judgment the fresh review of §3 must make with the power question (§3, item 6a) in front of it, not a fatal in itself.
    • ID-divergence discipline is unstated in the sketch; the amended instrument states it (reported, not gated, with a rendering-anomaly exclusion) and the resubmission inherits that text.

    2. Why the amended instrument is a resubmission, not an amendment¶

    The negation round's BUILD verdict rested on a stated test: every defect was corrected inside the adjudication doc, by amendment to the same instrument — same population, same arms, same scorer, same joint-rule shape — whose population and arms were already frozen and executable before the round began. The payload round's NO-BUILD rested on the complementary test: where the amendments replace an undefined instrument with a different one, the result is a resubmission and "must be reviewed as a new design, not built as 'this probe with amendments'".

    Measured against that test, the researcher's amended instrument changes: the endpoint pair (JSON–YAML → JSON–XML); the YAML arm's role (headline endpoint → descriptive only, behind a new schema-directed subset decoder); the paired population (10 → 13, under a new pairing derivation); the headline scorer (byte-exact/adherence conjunct → parsed answer-field value accuracy, with adherence demoted to descriptive rates); and the prompt's schema text (the shipped describe_schema → a neutral field list). The sketch's instrument was never frozen and executable in the first place — its YAML arm has no parser, its scorer is ambiguous between opposite-verdict readings (F1), and its population is self-contradictory (F1). This is the payload configuration, not the negation one.

    The verdict is therefore NO-BUILD for this round. This is explicitly not a finding that the amended instrument is unsound: the verifier upheld it as-amended (subject to §5's corrections), it answers every fatal in §1 by construction rather than by disclosure alone, and measured against red-team B's nine resubmission conditions it satisfies 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and the naming half of 9 in full. What it has never faced is a blind red team of its own: its novel machinery — the §3.4 coercion table, the neutral description renderer, the YAML subset grammar, the XML screen-and-decoder rules, the 13-spec pairing derivation — was claim-verified and math-verified in this round, not adversarially attacked. The program does not build its largest instrument on verification alone.

    Express narrowing accepted by the coordinator (B's condition 3, decision half): the candidate's claim is narrowed to answer-field value invariance between the JSON and XML endpoints. Adherence is reported as per-arm descriptive rates with denominators and carries no decision rule in the resubmission. Any future design that reintroduces an adherence falsification claim must supply an unconditioned adherence decision rule with its own derived operating characteristics; it may not reattach adherence to the conditioned value rule.

    7.     7. What a build cycle must NOT do on the basis of this doc¶

    • Do not build the circulated sketch, any YAML headline endpoint, or the transplanted field-order decision rule.

    • Do not carry field-order's "~15% power" figure, the removed figures of §5, or any arm-length figure without its renderer named, into any doc or help text.
    • Do not ship the flag under --answer-syntax; the resubmission's name is --document-syntax, and even it ships only after the build cycle's USAGE naming audit.

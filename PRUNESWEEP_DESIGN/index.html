    Prune-sweep probe — design of record¶

    Status¶

    BUILT in cycle 220 as run --prune-sweep, under the fresh review's BUILD-as-amended verdict (coordinator #62, Part III below): amendments A–I are incorporated into Part I, which is the instrument as built. Part II is the round-1 adjudication (coordinator #45, NO-BUILD) whose §3 resubmission candidate the fresh review judged; the candidate text of record is research §§3–10 (~/workspace/agents_out/prunesweep/research.md), of which Part I is the as-amended, as-built form. Part III is the fresh-review adjudication itself (coordinator #62). Endpoints (the doses 0/18/36/72 words, the six arms as decision structure, the label vocabulary), the population (24 generated cases under the frozen construction rules, C-conditioning as designed in §5), and the headline scorer (core.evaluate byte-exact + boundary) are frozen — an amendment touching any of them would have been a resubmission, not this build.

    Part I — The instrument as built (candidate text as amended)¶

    1. What exactly varies — the treatment dimension¶

    Which eligible words of a frozen fact-recall passage are deleted, at four nested doses, with the answer-bearing sentence, the instruction, and the question held byte-identical across every arm. The treatment is a bundle (§6): information loss, prompt shortening, fact-position shift, and sentence breakage move together under the scattered operator, and the report says so in words rather than letting the label imply a single channel. The displaced control (tail72) deletes a different, position-extreme set of the same size, so the panel can say when the honest reading is word-count loss rather than interior placement.

    2. Population and frozen texts (amendment C: lists printed)¶

    A generated, stage-local panel of 24 cases (prune-00 … prune-23, group label prune). Each case is a single user message: a frozen instruction + a 17-sentence passage + a frozen question, joined by "\n". The population is defined by the construction rules of §3 plus the invariant battery of §3.3; the lexical lists below are the rules' deterministic output, printed here as record-binding (the cycle-218 TOPICS precedent) and re-verified by the battery on every run.

    • Instruction: Read the passage and answer the question.
    • Fact template: The {topic} code is {value}. (exactly 6 words with a two-word topic; the fact sentence is passage sentence 9 of 17, index 8, and is never pruned).
    • Question template: What is the {topic} code? Reply with only the code word.
    • Topics (8, cycled over cases): harbor light, summit trail, quarry gate, tundra post, granite arch, willow ford, copper mill, saddle pass.
    • Code values (24, VALUES[case index]): amber, birch, cobalt, dune, ember, flint, garnet, hazel, ivory, jasper, kelvin, linden, magnet, nickel, onyx, pepper, quartz, rowan, saffron, timber, umber, violet, walnut, yonder.
    • Slot pools (12 each). Subjects: archive, ledger, cabinet, chapel, meadow, workshop, station, garden, library, market, office, depot. Objects: forms, letters, tools, charts, samples, records, parcels, volumes, tickets, bundles, reports, crates. Places: annex, attic, cellar, landing, terrace, alcove, gallery, storeroom, corridor, balcony, vestibule, loft. Times: morning, midday, evening, weekdays, season, harvest, holiday, weekend, noon, dusk, dawn, closing.
    • Sentence templates (4, each exactly 9 words; slot s uses template s mod 4): The {subject} holds {object} inside the {place} each {time}.; A {subject} keeps {object} near the {place} during {time}.; Workers quietly move {object} past the {place} during {time}.; Each {time} the {subject} stores {object} under the {place}.
    • Negation list (frozen, for the §3.3 screens and the §6 dropped-set shares): no, not, never, none, cannot, without, neither, nor, nothing, nobody, nowhere, hardly, barely, scarcely.

    Three list choices were forced by the screens and are recorded here: hazel replaces a value that would have shared its stem with a topic word; chapel replaces a subject drawn from the topic vocabulary; corridor replaces a place drawn from the instruction vocabulary, and harvest replaces a time word sharing a 4-character prefix with a code value — the battery (§3.3) rejects any future list edit that reintroduces such a collision.

    3. Construction (frozen)¶

    3.1 Case generation¶

    Per case, 16 context sentences are drawn by hash-ranked selection with distinctness rejection: for slot s and counter c from 0, sha256(f"{seed}:{case_id}:slot:{s}:{c}") supplies four pool indices (hex slices 0:8, 8:16, 16:24, 24:32, each mod 12); the rendered sentence is kept if it is not already in this case, else c increments and the draw repeats. The fact sentence is inserted at passage index 8. Distinctness is over rendered sentences.

    3.2 The pruning operator (amendment E: key pinned)¶

    The eligible set is the 144 words of the 16 context sentences (16 × 9), indexed 0–143 in passage order (sentence slot × 9 + word offset). One permutation per case orders positions by sha256(f"{seed}:{case_id}:{position}") hex ascending, where position is the eligible-list index (amendment E). Dose arms drop the first 0 / 18 / 36 / 72 positions of that order — nested by construction, so first-flip and returns are well-defined. tail72 drops positions 72–143 exactly (the eligible words of passage sentences 10–17). The fact sentence, instruction, and question are never pruned. The dose unit is words; rendered-token deltas are reported per candidate via backend.encode, never asserted and never gated. Measured on the shipped lists (seed 0): the tail72 drop set overlaps the dose72 drop set in 32 of 72 words on case 0 (panel mean 35.92, min 29, max 41) — the control is displaced, not disjoint, and the report does not claim otherwise.

    3.3 Invariant battery and screens (amendments D and C)¶

    validate_case re-derives each case from the frozen rules and asserts, per case: exactly 144 eligible words; 16 distinct context sentences of exactly 9 words; the 6-word fact sentence at index 8; protected parts byte-identical in every arm; each dose arm removes exactly its dose of words and nothing else; no context word shares a 4-character prefix with the case's code value; slot pools disjoint from the values, the topics, the fact template's fixed words, and — amendment D — the instruction and question vocabulary; no context sentence contains a digit; no context sentence contains a frozen-negation-list word; and an acrostic screen (amendment D): the sentence-initial letters of the 16 context sentences must not spell any pool value, as a whole string or in any single sentence pair window the screen enumerates. Any failure excludes the case before generation under the stage detail prune_screen_failed, with the audit's by_reason naming the failing screen (case_invariant, special_token_literal, or the boundary-text containment screen, which reuses the sibling stages' form: no arm content may contain the rendered boundary text). Context sentences are declarative statements from the frozen templates — the templates carry no question, imperative, or negation shapes (amendment D).

    4. Arms and scorer¶

    Six arms: dose0, dose18, dose36, dose72 (the headline ladder), tail72 (descriptive displaced control), closed_book (screen only: instruction + question, no passage). The scorer is core.evaluate byte-exact ∧ boundary, unchanged. Every arm of a case is requested under the case's own fixture id, so request seeds are identical across arms by construction. Records carry answer digests only — no raw answer text — plus per-arm token counts, per-arm rendered-id digests, the derived ids_identical flag, per-arm content digests, and the per-case dropped-set shares (§6).

    5. Decision rule and operating characteristics (amendment A: corrected)¶

    Per candidate, over the cases completed in all four headline arms, minus closed-book-screened cases (the closed-book cell completed and passed — passage loss cannot move a case the candidate answers with no passage at all), minus ids-identical cases (the dose0 and dose72 renders tokenise identically, derived from the recorded id digests): C = dose0 passes; A = cases in C failing dose72, decomposed as in §6 — a boundary_only dose72 cell does not count toward A (amendment B); B = cases failing dose0 and passing dose72. Rule: C < 12 → insufficient; C ≥ 12 ∧ A ≥ 5 ∧ two-sided exact McNemar p(A, B) < 0.05 (confidentiality.mcnemar_exact_p) → prune_drop_observed; else below_threshold. No winner readout.

    Corrected operating characteristics. The candidate as submitted printed a 40,000-trial Monte Carlo claiming zero fires on every null ("95% upper bound ≈ 0.00008"; "< 0.01 expected false labels per panel"). All three sentences are false and are deleted wherever the candidate printed them. The rule's discreteness is the defect: at C = 24 the smallest firing A is 6, and (A, B) = (6, 0) already gives p = 0.03125, so any null with discordance mass fires at a per-decision rate of about one percent. The figures below are exact enumerations (integer arithmetic, independently re-derived at build time), conditional on headline completion, and are printed in the report section with their conditioning stated.

    Stated basis — pass0 ~ Bernoulli(p0); fail72|pass0 = q; pass72|fail0 = r; the two arms conditionally independent given the case class:

    • random (p0 = 0.5, q = r = 0.5): 0.0121
    • competent (p0 = 0.9, q = r = 0.1): 0.0143
    • jitter (p0 = 0.97, q = r = 0.02): 0.000005
    • power at a 25 / 50 / 75 percent conditional drop (p0 = 0.9, r = 0.1): 0.3969 / 0.9601 / 0.9996

    Exchangeable basis — cases exchangeable with marginal pass rate m and per-direction discordance v (joint (pass, pass) = m − v, (pass, fail) = (fail, pass) = v, (fail, fail) = 1 − m − v), competent m = 0.9:

    • v = 0.09 / 0.05 / 0.02: 0.003 / 0.0004 / 0.000004

    Family-wise per 24-candidate panel: stated basis E = 0.34, P(≥ 1) = 0.29; exchangeable basis E = 0.07, P(≥ 1) = 0.07. On the stated basis the closed-book screen shrinks the competent null from 0.0143 to 0.0017 at the frozen screen floor (16 unscreened passes). The firing census is exact: 492 (C, A, B) triples fire, none with A ≤ B. The conditioning matters and is printed: under free completion a model that attempts every case and fails often completes few joint cells, and these rates rise toward the stated random row — the enumeration's conditioning on completion is a disclosed limitation of the printed figures, not a hidden one.

    6. Report shape (printed, in this order, per candidate)¶

    Per-arm pass rates with attrition beside them (context overflows and evaluation failures counted, never pooled); the per-arm four-way decomposition in one frozen order over (verdict, boundary_found, answer_digest) — exact_pass, boundary_only, wrong_value (the digest is a different pool value's), non_value (amendment B: decidable from digests alone); the headline counts C, A, B with boundary attrition printed beside A and the decision; A by first-flip dose; the descriptive shape readout (first-flip histogram, returns, per-dose pass rates — never gated, §8); the tail72 contrast with its discordant counts; the id report (per-arm id-length deltas, ids-identical counts, same-ids flips); and the per-dose dropped-set shares for the numeral and negation classes (expected 0 under the §3.3 screens; printed and validator-mirrored, amendment D).

    Bundle fencing (amendment F). The section states that the label attributes the drop to the named word-loss treatment as a bundle — information loss + prompt shortening + fact-position shift + sentence breakage — and prints the executed operator constants: under dose72 the fact sentence's word position shifts by a mean of 36.0 words (sd 3.01, the exact hypergeometric figure) while under tail72 the shift is exactly 0 and the fact instead lands in passage-final position; an expected 15.95 of the 16 context sentences are partially broken at dose72 (0.02 removed whole, 0.02 intact) versus 0 under tail72. When the tail72 discordance is within noise of dose72's (the frozen rival test: dose72 fails exist, and either there are no discordants in either direction or the two-sided McNemar p of the discordants is ≥ 0.05), the section prints the sentence: the honest reading of this panel is word-count loss at 50 percent of the eligible words, not interior placement.

    7. Utility and scope (amendment G)¶

    The headline's subject is the class of operations that delete interior passage words: question-aware compressors of the LLMLingua class, middle-chunk RAG trimming, and word-level summarisation/redaction pipelines. Character caps and tail cuts — the operations whose loss is positional by definition — are assigned explicitly to the tail72 contrast, not to the headline. The instrument cannot detect a trim that deletes the answer itself: the fact sentence is protected precisely so the measurement is sound, and answer-deleting trims are named out of scope in the section's printed limitations. Monday-morning sentence: a prompt engineer whose pipeline deletes interior passage words uses this, per candidate, to learn whether deleting half the non-answer-bearing words of a frozen fact-recall passage (answer sentence, instruction, and question byte-identical) produces a large verdict drop at the printed operating characteristics, before that trim ships.

    8. Shape gate — declined, with the executed figures (amendment I)¶

    The shape readout is descriptive, never a gate. Under the frozen graded alternative — a linear interpolation of the conditional top-dose drop, q_d = q72 · d / 72, at p0 = 0.9, r = 0.1, so the base 50-percent-drop power is 0.9601 — a returns cap collapses power to 0.094 (the candidate printed 0.031; that figure does not reproduce under the printed interpolation and is corrected here), and an intermediate-support requirement collapses threshold-drop power to exactly 0.000. Any shape gate makes a real shape invisible; the panel prints the shape and declines to judge it.

    9. Counts (frozen, with derivations)¶

    24 cases × 6 arms = 144 generations per candidate; 2,304 headline generations per 24-candidate panel; 3,456 legal maximum generations (24 × 6 × 24); 576 records maximum; the house records cap of 12 MiB applies unchanged. The stage is generation-based like its siblings: it runs its own cells under the run's backend, formats, seed, and cancellation, inside the run, and the whole-stage failure payload is {"available": false, "detail": "prune_unavailable"} in the tree's frozen shape.

    10. Records and validator mirror¶

    Records are digest-only (answer digests, id digests, content digests; no raw answers). The diff validator holds an independent generator mirror: it rebuilds the protocol whole (including the case-material digest over all frozen lists and the ground digest over the regenerated cases), regenerates every case, rebinds every record's content digests to the regenerated arm contents for all six arms, checks the recorded ids_identical flag against its derivation from the recorded id digests, recomputes the dropped-set shares, binds every completed cell to its record's arm token count, re-derives every verdict from digest identity, and recomputes every aggregate — including the headline population pipeline in its frozen order — before any payload is called valid. Drift metrics per candidate: decision, C, A, boundary attrition, B, population size, the tail72 discordants, the rival reading, and same-ids flips at dose72, plus protocol / generator / backend drift and candidate added/removed sets.

    11. The claim (only falsification-class sentence)¶

    For this candidate, on this frozen panel, deleting half the non-answer-bearing words of a fact-recall passage — as the named bundle of §6 — did (or did not) produce a large verdict drop at the operating characteristics printed in §5. What would prove the instrument wrong: a candidate whose dose72 verdicts fall while its tail72 verdicts hold, on a panel where the printed null rates say the joint pattern is common — the contrast is printed precisely so that reading can be checked.

    12. Naming audit (amendment H, re-executed at build)¶

    The flag is run --prune-sweep; the help text's first sentence states that the probe prunes passage words. Re-audit at cycle-220 build start, against the post-cycle-219 namespace: the run subparser carries 61 optionals before this flag (argparse introspection of cli.build_parser()); 70 --flag literals exist in cli.py overall; --prune-sweep / prune_sweep occur nowhere in the package or tests before this build; "prune" occurs in no source file and once in the docs (USAGE.md, in the candidate-set prose sense of selecting survivors). No collision.

    13. Uniqueness pass (primary sources)¶

    The neighbours were resolved at primary in the design rounds (Part II §5): LLMLingua (arXiv:2310.05736), LongLLMLingua (arXiv:2310.06839), and LLMLingua-2 (arXiv:2403.12968, §4.2 for the word-level compression unit τ) are subjects of the measurement class in §7, not implementations of it: none ships a seeded, nested-dose, answer-protected word-loss panel with a derived decision rule, a displaced tail control, and digest-only validator-mirrored records. In-tree neighbours are bounded exactly in Part II §3 and the round-1 text: density (character share of one field), token-depth-sweep (prefix truncation), needle-depth (insertion depth), distraction (added distractors), surface-noise (character corruption), ablation (field removal) — each varies a different dimension; this probe is the only one that deletes interior words of a fixed passage under protection of the answer sentence. The claim is the combination, and it is scoped to the sources surveyed in the design rounds, which did not re-survey the general evaluation harnesses this round — that limit is stated, not hidden.

    1. Why NO-BUILD: the fatal defects of the candidate as specified¶

    Each defect below is individually blocking and was established by execution (attribution: R = researcher, A = red-team A, B = red-team B, V = verifier, C = coordinator).

    F1 — Evidence destruction: the dose-response is answer-survival arithmetic, not robustness¶

    The sketch holds expected fixed while pruning deletes the tokens the expected answer is derived from, with no solvability or redundancy screen. A perfect-reader stub through the real core.evaluate passes at exactly the closed-form survival probability of its evidence: 1−k/n for k evidence tokens among n — executed at 0.86/0.75/0.49 (A) and 0.890/0.740/0.485 (B) at 10/25/50% for single-token evidence, rising to 1.000/0.995/0.910 when the same evidence is merely repeated 3×. A prompt-ignoring stub passes all doses. The headline curve therefore measures fixture redundancy, not template robustness: two fixtures with identical templates and different evidence redundancy produce different "first-flip doses" with no template property involved. (A F1, B F1, V upheld, C re-executed: survival = 1−dose to three decimals at n=20 and n=100.)

    F2 — The pruning policy is an unfrozen second treatment riding on the dose¶

    Which tokens are removed is not specified: policy, seed derivation, dose-rounding, and nesting are all unfrozen, and they are not cosmetic. At one fixed dose (drop 5 of 18 words), three honest policies give opposite verdicts — uniform-random broke the "code is" context around a surviving answer while function-word removal passed (A F2, executed). The two red teams themselves, instructed only by the sketch, chose different roundings — the sketch does not determine its own instrument. Rounding is load-bearing at the shipped scale: floor(10%) of every shipped fixture's last user message is 0 tokens (C re-executed on examples/fixtures.json: spans of 7, 8, 5, and 8 words), so the 10% arm is ids-identical filler on the entire shipped population, while ceil-rounding silently turns "10%" into 12.5–14.3%. Non-nested draws additionally make the sketch's own flip-back clause ("a verdict that flips at 25% and flips back at 50% is noise") incoherent: with independent draws per dose there is no fact of the matter about which draw a return belongs to. (A F2, B F2, V upheld.)

    F3 — Grammaticality and semantics collapse: the construct is not "loss"¶

    Pruned text is not shorter text; it is broken text, and the breakage is not dose-monotone in any single construct. Single-token deletions invert ground truth while expected stays frozen — "is not locked" → "is locked", "Ana, not to Ben" → "Ana, to Ben" (A F3, B F5, executed) — at doses as low as one token (~7% of a 14-word span). A numeral drop deletes the answer inside otherwise fluent text; a filler drop is harmless. The sketch pools three different events — evidence loss, meaning inversion, and harmless noise — under one "content loss" headline with no decomposition, so the construct it measures is robustness-to-ungrammaticality-and- inversion, not robustness-to-loss. On the tree's own fixtures the "content span" is an instruction sentence (F7), so the same instrument would measure instruction mutilation there and document loss elsewhere, pooled. (A F3, B F5, V upheld.)

    F4 — No decision rule: the first-flip falsifier fires on noise with P = 1.000¶

    The sketch's falsifier — "the first dose at which any candidate's pass set changes" — is the any-difference rule this program has already killed twice (directive F2, instruction-distance). Over the sketch's own 7,200-cell panel it fires with P=1.000 at per-cell jitter ε ≥ 0.001 (A: 2000/2000 trials at ε ≥ 0.005; B: conditioned selection-flip 0.778–0.998; C re-executed: 300/300 at each of ε = 0.001, 0.005, 0.01, analytic 1−(1−ε)^7200 = 0.9993 at ε=0.001). The selection half is a one-cell tripwire. The falsifier is also shape-blind and self-contradictory: decay, threshold, single-dip, U/recovery, and improvement all return the identical headline "FALSIFIED", while the sketch's own text says a flip-and-return "is noise, counted as such" — a clause the headline cannot express. Borrowed sibling floors fire ≈1.000 at this panel's N=100 (B, executed). The common-completed set the headline conditions on is post-treatment in both directions, with no attrition reporting. (A F4, B F4, V upheld.)

    F5 — The "sweep" headline is permutation-invariant, and one deletion bundles four channels¶

    A first-flip dose is invariant to permuting the dose order: graded decay, a threshold cliff, a single dip, and a U all return the same number, so the word "sweep" promises a shape the readout cannot distinguish (A F5, executed; the instruction-distance F5 settlement). Separately, one interior deletion simultaneously removes information, shortens the prompt, shifts every downstream token's position, and breaks syntax — four channels, one number, no length-matched (pad / sham / placeholder) control to separate them, and no such control exists anywhere in the sketch. The claim names one cause ("loss fraction"); the treatment delivers a bundle. (A F5, B F6 in part, V upheld.)

    F6 — The dose unit is unexecutable as specified: token fractions are per-checkpoint, and there is no frozen tokenizer¶

    Doses are stated as fractions of content tokens, but the tree has no per-candidate frozen tokenizer (the instruction-language fertility settlement: no unexecuted token figures may be printed). The same nominal 25% dose deletes different text under different tokenizers — word-level: clean whole words; character-level: the answer 4821 → 421 (A F6, executed). A "10% dose" is therefore not one treatment across the 24-candidate panel; it is 24 different treatments sharing a label, and any cross-candidate comparison of first-flip doses compares unlike quantities. (A F6, V upheld.)

    F7 — The population is undefined, and empty on the shipped fixtures under any honest floor¶

    The sketch says "100 fixtures" without stating the population or the split scope. On the only population the tree ships, the last user messages are 5–8-word instructions that contain the expected answer itself ("Reply with exactly BLUE…", "Return only the numeral 7.") — not the long documents of the utility paragraph. C re-executed: all four shipped cases (search 2, held-out 2) have last-user spans of 5–8 words; floor(10%) = 0 on all four; any span floor ≥ 9 words — the minimum at which a 10% dose is even one token with margin — makes the evaluable share 0/2 search fixtures. The utility population (long pasted documents, support tickets) is a different population, owned in-tree by the needle/distraction family's generated corpora, and importing it is a new instrument, not a reading of the sketch. The copied token-depth pattern also drags in treated held-out winner evaluation, which the sketch never scopes. (A F8, B F2/F7, V upheld.)

    F8 — The span machinery does not exist for this use, pruned id sequences are non-canonical, and the build surface is misstated¶

    The sketch's span location rests on the behavioural template probe's differencing, but templateprobe.find_span (templateprobe.py:283) resolves a span only between sentinels the probe itself inserted into synthetic probe content; on real fixture renders it returns None for every fixture × family (R S1, B F3, V and C verified: the function counts sentinel occurrences and returns None when either does not occur exactly once). No tree function maps a message's character span to a token-id span in an arbitrary render. The substrate is worse than unlocated: a seam token ('\nR', executed with a merging tokenizer) is simultaneously skeleton and content, so no id interval equals the content span and "skeleton untouched" is unachievable in id space; and pruned id sequences are the tokenisation of no text (decode→re-encode ≠ pruned ids, executed by A, B, and V) — prompts no truncation tool emits, with the sketch's defence being disclosure of the artefact share, not removal of the artefact. The cited wrapper beside backends.py:811 exposes encode(text) only and cannot locate a last-user span from text alone; delivery would need a plan-keyed surface. The sketch's "M–L, a copy of the token-depth pattern" understates the build surface on every axis. (R S1, A F9, B F3, V upheld.)

    F9 — No frozen fail-closed vocabulary, decomposition, or attrition discipline¶

    The sketch freezes no vocabulary for span failure, seam straddling, non-canonical renders, zero-eligible populations, or per-dose attrition; no answer-decomposition (the negation settlement's digest-derived classes); no paired-subset seed discipline; no native-digest binding. Every one of these is a place a build would have to invent verdict words, which the program forbids. Verified by reading the full slate text (B F7, V). (A F10, B F7, V upheld.)

    Partially upheld — duplication and utility (A F7, B F6)¶

    Not a standalone fatal, recorded because it kills the sketch's novelty paragraph: the instrument's shape — a dose ladder over prompt ids with a first-flip headline — is the token-depth sweep's (experiments.py:3621, backends.py:810), and the production operations the utility paragraph names (tail cuts, character caps) are already shipped as prompt_token_depth_sweep / prompt_sensitivity_sweep with the same first-flip headline. Text-level interior deletion also already ships (perturbation punctuation_strip, surface-noise). What is genuinely unshipped is only the residue: dose-graded interior deletion itself. On documents, uniform pruning is furthermore distraction run in reverse — ~90% of drops hit filler (A, executed). A resubmission must argue its utility against the shipped tail-cut sweeps, naming the real operation interior deletion models; the sketch does not.

    2. Amendment or resubmission?¶
